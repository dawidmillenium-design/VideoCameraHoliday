City Through the Lens: Santiago, Chile

Local videographers reveal how they film their hometown — the real locations, permit hurdles, and techniques you won't find in guidebooks.

🎬 Local videographer interview coming soon — subscribe on YouTube to see it first

Quick Takeaways for Filmmakers

  • Best filming season: Mediterranean; hot dry summers, cool wet winters
  • Top 3 landmarks: Plaza de Armas, Cerro San Cristóbal, La Moneda Palace
  • Main airport: Arturo Merino Benítez
  • Drone rules: Check local regulations
  • Featured camera: Nikon Z7 II

3 Must-Film Locations in Santiago

1. Plaza de Armas

Filming Tip: Historic center; cathedral, post office; street life; metro accessible.

2. Cerro San Cristóbal

Filming Tip: Funicular or cable car; Virgin statue; city panorama; clearest mornings.

3. La Moneda Palace

Filming Tip: Presidential palace; changing guard; Allende history; underground cultural center.

Weather Reality Check

Mediterranean; hot dry summers, cool wet winters. Best Oct-Apr.

Pro tip: Plan your shoot dates around these conditions. The "best" months mean better light, fewer crowds, and more predictable weather windows.

Airport Transfer & Arrival Tips

Arturo Merino Benítez (SCL) 30min Uber.

Factor in transfer time when booking flights. Arriving early gives you buffer for gear checks and acclimatization before shooting.

Permits, Rules & Local Etiquette

Government buildings restricted. Andes drone zones. Check regulations.

Remember: Always ask before filming people, especially in religious or residential areas. Respect local customs — it's the difference between getting great footage and being asked to delete it.
