Hong Kong — Vertical City & High Contrast. This guide covers essential filming locations, weather considerations, and permit requirements.

⚡ Key Takeaways — Hong Kong

  • Top 3 locations: Victoria Peak, Star Ferry, Temple Street Night Market.
  • Weather: Typhoons Jul-Sept; humid summers. Best Oct-Dec.
  • Airport: HKIA (HKG) Airport Express 24 min.
  • Permits: Drones effectively illegal without a Civil Aviation Dept permit; tripod permits needed in MTR and some parks.

3 Must-Film Locations in Hong Kong

1. Victoria Peak

Filming tip: Shoot the skyline at dusk; arrive early for tripod space at viewpoints.

2. Star Ferry

Filming tip: Upper deck, handheld only; HK$5 for the best moving skyline shot on Earth.

3. Temple Street Night Market

Filming tip: Neon peaks 7-10 PM; ask vendors — some stalls refuse filming.

Weather Reality for Videographers
