Local videographers reveal how they film their hometown — the real locations, permit hurdles, and techniques you won't find in guidebooks.

Quick Takeaways for Filmmakers

  • Best filming season: Tropical; monsoon Jun-Sep extreme
  • Top 3 landmarks: Gateway of India, Chhatrapati Shivaji Terminus, Dhobi Ghat
  • Main airport: Chhatrapati Shivaji
  • Drone rules: need DGCA approval.
  • Featured camera: Sony A7S III

3 Must-Film Locations in Mumbai

1. Gateway of India

Filming Tip: Sunrise emptiest; ferry to Elephanta Caves; street vendors backdrop.

2. Chhatrapati Shivaji Terminus

Filming Tip: UNESCO Victorian Gothic; interior bustling; platform filming permission needed.

3. Dhobi Ghat

Filming Tip: Open-air laundry; Mahalaxmi station view; respect workers' space.

Weather Reality Check

Tropical; monsoon Jun-Sep extreme. Best Nov-Feb.

Pro tip: Plan your shoot dates around these conditions. The "best" months mean better light, fewer crowds, and more predictable weather windows.

Airport Transfer & Arrival Tips

Chhatrapati Shivaji (BOM) 45-90min traffic dependent.

Factor in transfer time when booking flights. Arriving early gives you buffer for gear checks and acclimatization before shooting.

Permits, Rules & Local Etiquette

Railway property strict rules. Drones need DGCA approval.

Remember: Always ask before filming people, especially in religious or residential areas. Respect local customs — it's the difference between getting great footage and being asked to delete it.
