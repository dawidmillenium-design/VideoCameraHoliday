Ahmedabad is a city of historic Pols, bustling markets, and the serene Sabarmati River – a blend of heritage and modernity. Filming here comes with challenges: intense heat, dust, and crowded streets. We sat down with Priya Shah, an Ahmedabad‑based videographer who has shot documentaries and cultural content across Gujarat, to get the real story on filming in this vibrant city.

🎯 What You'll Learn in This Interview

  • Best cinematic spots: The Sabarmati Riverfront, the Old City's Pols, and the historic markets.
  • Gear for heat & dust: Why Priya favours weather‑sealed bodies and dust‑proof lenses.
  • Permits & etiquette: Where you can film freely and where you need a permit.
  • Golden hour on the river: Her go‑to settings for that warm, golden light.

🏛️ Priya's Top Filming Locations

  • Sabarmati Riverfront: The waterfront offers stunning views of the city skyline. Sunset is the best time for warm, cinematic light.
  • Old City – Pols: The historic neighbourhoods are a maze of narrow lanes and traditional architecture. Visit early morning (6 AM) before the crowds arrive.
  • Markets: The bustling bazaars offer endless B‑roll opportunities. A fast prime lens (35mm f/1.4) is essential for low‑light interiors.

⚡ Quick Ahmedabad Filming Tips

  • Protect from dust: Use a weather‑sealed camera and carry a dust blower.
  • Heat management: Keep gear in the shade when not shooting.
  • Use a variable ND filter to keep shutter angle correct in bright sunlight.
  • Ask permission – always ask before filming people, especially in religious sites.
