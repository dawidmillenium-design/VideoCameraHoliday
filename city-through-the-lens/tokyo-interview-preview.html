📹 Holiday Video Camera

Tokyo is the most photogenic city on earth – neon-lit streets, tranquil temples, and a hyper-efficient transport system that creates endless visual opportunities. But filming here is a challenge: crowds are relentless, permission is strict, and the city's scale can be overwhelming. We sat down with Kenji Tanaka, a Tokyo‑based videographer who has shot everything from commercials to travel documentaries, to get the real story on filming in Japan's capital.

🎯 What You'll Learn in This Interview

  • Best cinematic spots: Shibuya Crossing, Shinjuku, and the hidden backstreets of Yanaka.
  • Gear for crowds & neon: Why Kenji favours fast primes and compact gimbals.
  • Permits & etiquette: Where you can film freely and where you need a permit.
  • Night settings: His go‑to settings for Tokyo's famous neon-lit streets.
  • Editing style: How he grades his footage to bring out Tokyo's electric energy.

🏙️ Kenji's Top Filming Locations

  • Shibuya Crossing – at night: The world's busiest pedestrian crossing is best shot after dark when the neon lights are on. A gimbal is essential for smooth footage through the crowds.
  • Shinjuku – neon alleys: The Golden Gai area offers narrow alleys filled with tiny bars and neon signs. A fast prime lens (f/1.8 or faster) is essential for low-light handheld shooting.
  • Yanaka – old Tokyo: A quiet neighbourhood that survived the war – narrow streets, wooden houses, and a sense of old Tokyo. Early morning is the best time to film.

📸 The Kit He Actually Uses

  • Primary camera: Sony ZV‑E10 II – lightweight, great low light, and reliable autofocus.
  • Go‑to lens: Sony 24‑70mm f/2.8 G Master – versatile for both wide and detail shots.
  • Compact gimbal: DJI RS 3 Mini for smooth walking shots through the crowds.
  • Audio: RØDE Wireless Go II for capturing interviews in noisy environments.
  • Backup: An iPhone 16 Pro for quick B‑roll.

⚡ Quick Tokyo Filming Tips

  • Use a variable ND filter to keep shutter angle correct in bright daylight.
  • Shoot in flat profile to get the most dynamic range for grading.
  • Ask permission – always ask before filming people, especially in temples and residential areas.
  • Check drone regulations – Tokyo has strict drone rules.
