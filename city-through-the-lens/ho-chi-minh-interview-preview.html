Quick Takeaways for Filmmakers

  • Best filming season: Tropical; dry season Dec-Apr, wet May-Nov
  • Top 3 landmarks: War Remnants Museum, Notre-Dame Basilica, Ben Thanh Market
  • Main airport: Tan Son Nhat
  • Drone rules: banned urban area.
  • Featured camera: Sony ZV-E10

3 Must-Film Locations in Ho Chi Minh City

1. War Remnants Museum

Filming Tip: Emotional exhibits; outdoor tanks/aircraft; no-flash interiors; respectful tone.

2. Notre-Dame Basilica

Filming Tip: Currently under renovation; exterior scaffolding; nearby Central Post Office.

3. Ben Thanh Market

Filming Tip: Day market inside, night market outside; bargain hard; food section.

Weather Reality Check

Tropical; dry season Dec-Apr, wet May-Nov. Best Dec-Mar.

Pro tip: Plan your shoot dates around these conditions. The "best" months mean better light, fewer crowds, and more predictable weather windows.

Airport Transfer & Arrival Tips

Tan Son Nhat (SGN) 30min taxi (traffic dependent).

Factor in transfer time when booking flights. Arriving early gives you buffer for gear checks and acclimatization before shooting.

Permits, Rules & Local Etiquette

War museums sensitive content. Drones banned urban area.

Remember: Always ask before filming people, especially in religious or residential areas. Respect local customs — it's the difference between getting great footage and being asked to delete it.
