🎬 About This Series

Every travel video you see online is filmed by an outsider looking in. But the people who know a city best aren't tourists — they're the local creators who film it every single day.

City Through the Lens changes the perspective. In each episode, Dawid Millennium sits down with a local videographer and asks them to show their city through their eyes. What gear do they actually use? Where do they film that tourists never find? How do they make money? What do they wish they'd known when they started?

The result is 50 honest, unfiltered conversations about filmmaking, creativity, and making it pay — from Tokyo to Marrakech, from Mexico City to Mumbai.

💡 5 Lessons From 50 Conversations

1 Volume beats perfection. Every filmmaker who "made it" sent hundreds of cold emails, posted hundreds of videos, and pitched dozens of clients before getting consistent work.
2 Specialization wins. The filmmakers earning the most aren't "travel filmmakers" — they're food-tourism filmmakers, adventure-sports filmmakers, or luxury-hotel filmmakers. Narrow beats broad.
3 Income diversity is survival. Every filmmaker who survived 2020 had 3+ revenue streams. Client work alone is too fragile.
4 Gear doesn't matter as much as you think. 38 of 50 filmmakers started with a phone or a sub-$500 camera. Clients care about results, not specs.
5 Community compounds. The filmmakers who grew fastest were the ones who collaborated, referred work, and shared knowledge freely.
