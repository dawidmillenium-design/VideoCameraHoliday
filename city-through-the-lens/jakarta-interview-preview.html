Quick Takeaways for Filmmakers

  • Best filming season: Equatorial; wet Nov-Mar, drier Apr-Oct
  • Top 3 landmarks: Monas (National Monument), Kota Tua (Old Town), Istiqlal Mosque
  • Main airport: Soekarno-Hatta
  • Drone rules: need permit.
  • Featured camera: DJI Action 5

3 Must-Film Locations in Jakarta

1. Monas (National Monument)

Filming Tip: Central Jakarta icon; park grounds; elevator to observation deck.

2. Kota Tua (Old Town)

Filming Tip: Dutch colonial buildings; Fatahillah Square; bicycle rentals.

3. Istiqlal Mosque

Filming Tip: Largest SE Asia mosque; non-Muslim visiting hours; respectful dress.

Weather Reality Check

Equatorial; wet Nov-Mar, drier Apr-Oct. Hot year-round.

Pro tip: Plan your shoot dates around these conditions. The "best" months mean better light, fewer crowds, and more predictable weather windows.

Airport Transfer & Arrival Tips

Soekarno-Hatta (CGK) 45-90min traffic dependent.

Factor in transfer time when booking flights. Arriving early gives you buffer for gear checks and acclimatization before shooting.

Permits, Rules & Local Etiquette

Government buildings restricted. Drones need permit.

Remember: Always ask before filming people, especially in religious or residential areas. Respect local customs — it's the difference between getting great footage and being asked to delete it.
