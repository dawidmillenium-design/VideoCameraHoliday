Chengdu Filming Guide: Canon EOS R5 II
🎬

Dawid Millennium — Travel Videographer

Travel vlogger since 2019. Tested 50+ cameras on real trips.

Chengdu — Overcast Light & Panda Rules. This guide covers essential filming locations, weather considerations, and permit requirements.

⚡ Key Takeaways — Chengdu

  • Top 3 locations: Chengdu Panda Base, Kuanzhai Alleys, People's Park Teahouses.
  • Weather: Overcast most of the year; humid summers. Best Mar-June and Sept-Nov.
  • Airport: Shuangliu (CTU) 30 min; Tianfu (TFU) 60+ min — check your ticket.
  • Permits: Panda areas strictly regulated; drones banned at the base.

3 Must-Film Locations in Chengdu

1. Chengdu Panda Base

Filming tip: No flash, no tripods; go 8-10 AM when pandas are active; overcast light is soft — expose +0.7.

2. Kuanzhai Alleys

Filming tip: Crowded by 10 AM; shoot at opening; handheld.

3. People's Park Teahouses

Filming tip: Locals are happy to be filmed if you buy a tea and ask first.

Weather Reality for Videographers

Overcast most of the year; humid summers. Best Mar-June and Sept-Nov.

Pro tip: Plan shoots according to seasonal conditions. Check forecasts regularly.

Airport Transfer

Shuangliu (CTU) 30 min; Tianfu (TFU) 60+ min — check your ticket.

Permits & Etiquette

Panda areas strictly regulated; drones banned at the base.
