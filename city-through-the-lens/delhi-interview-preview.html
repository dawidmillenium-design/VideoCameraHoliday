Quick Takeaways for Filmmakers

  • Best filming season: Extreme summer (45°C), foggy winter
  • Top 3 landmarks: Red Fort, Qutub Minar, Chandni Chowk
  • Main airport: Indira Gandhi
  • Drone rules: banned in capital.
  • Featured camera: Canon EOS R6

3 Must-Film Locations in Delhi

1. Red Fort

Filming Tip: UNESCO Mughal architecture; sound & light show evening; tripods need permit.

2. Qutub Minar

Filming Tip: Tallest brick minaret; iron pillar mystery; archaeological survey rules.

3. Chandni Chowk

Filming Tip: Old Delhi market; food tours; cycle rickshaw POV; chaotic energy.

Weather Reality Check

Extreme summer (45°C), foggy winter. Best Oct-Mar.

Pro tip: Plan your shoot dates around these conditions. The "best" months mean better light, fewer crowds, and more predictable weather windows.

Airport Transfer & Arrival Tips

Indira Gandhi (DEL) 45min Airport Express metro.

Factor in transfer time when booking flights. Arriving early gives you buffer for gear checks and acclimatization before shooting.

Permits, Rules & Local Etiquette

Government buildings restricted. Drones banned in capital.

Remember: Always ask before filming people, especially in religious or residential areas. Respect local customs — it's the difference between getting great footage and being asked to delete it.
