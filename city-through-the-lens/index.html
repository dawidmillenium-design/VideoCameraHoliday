    What Is City Through the Lens?

    City Through the Lens is a 50-part interview series in which Holiday Video Camera asks one local videographer in each of the world's 50 largest cities a single question: how do you really film your hometown? Each episode covers the interviewee's working camera kit, the exact settings they use for their city's light and weather, where filming is restricted or requires permits, and the mistakes tourists make with a camera in that city. The series exists because camera advice written at a desk fails on location — a gimbal that is perfect in a studio behaves differently in Dhaka's monsoon humidity or on a moving Istanbul ferry. If you are planning how to edit the footage afterward, our technical guide to syncing 24fps and 120fps holiday footage to AI-generated music shows the post-production side of the same workflow.

    Every interview follows the same 15-question protocol, so you can compare cities directly: kit, settings, light windows, permits, safety, and one signature shot the local recommends. Interviews are published weekly and linked from this page as they go live.

    ⚡ Key Takeaways

    • 50 cities, 50 local experts: one working videographer per city, from Tokyo (the world's largest metro area) to Santiago.
    • Field-tested only: every recommendation comes from someone who films that city every week, not from spec sheets.
    • Same 15 questions everywhere: kit, settings, light, permits, and etiquette — so answers are comparable across cities.
    • New interview weekly: this hub page is updated each time an episode is published; bookmark it.

    How We Choose and Interview Local Videographers

    We built this series around one rule: no interviewee, no article. We do not write "city guides" from desk research. Each episode starts only after a working local shooter agrees to go on the record.

    Our selection criteria

    • Actively working: the interviewee must have published footage shot in their city within the last 6 months — wedding films, street documentaries, commercial work, or a consistent vlog channel all qualify.
    • Local for 3+ years: they must know the city's seasons, light windows, and filming rules from experience.
    • Verifiable work: we link to their channel or portfolio so you can judge their footage before trusting their advice.

    The standard 15 questions

    Every interviewee answers the same core protocol: (1) current camera body and why it survives their city; (2) the one lens they would keep; (3) their default exposure settings for local light; (4) worst weather month and how they protect gear; (5) where filming is banned or permit-only; (6) how locals react to cameras; (7) the signature shot every visitor misses; (8) audio strategy in noise; (9) stabilization approach; (10) storage and backup on the move; (11) battery strategy for local temperatures; (12) the mistake they see tourists make most; (13) their budget alternative kit; (14) editing workflow; (15) one tip they have never published before. Budget-conscious travelers will recognize many picks from our best holiday cameras under $500 guide — locals rarely carry the most expensive option.

    Editorial standard: Interviewees are never paid and receive no gear. If a future episode is ever sponsored, it will be labeled at the top of the article. Quotes are edited only for length and clarity, and each interviewee approves the final transcript before publication.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is the City Through the Lens series?

    City Through the Lens is a 50-part interview series by Holiday Video Camera. In each episode, one local videographer in one of the world's 50 largest cities answers 15 standard questions about how they film their hometown: camera kit, settings, light windows, filming permits, safety, and local etiquette. The goal is practical, first-hand filming advice that desk-written travel guides cannot provide.

    How are the 50 cities chosen?

    The cities are the 50 largest urban metro areas in the world by population, spanning East Asia, South and Southeast Asia, the Middle East and North Africa, Europe, Sub-Saharan Africa, and Latin America. Exact rankings vary slightly by source and year, but the list covers every metro area that consistently appears in global top-50 population rankings.

    How do you select the videographers you interview?

    Three rules: they must be actively publishing footage shot in their city within the last six months, they must have lived there for at least three years, and their work must be publicly verifiable so readers can judge it. Interviewees are never paid and never receive gear, and they approve the final transcript before publication.

    Which cameras are covered in the series?

    Whatever the local expert actually uses — from smartphones and pocket gimbals like the DJI Osmo Pocket 3 to mirrorless cinema rigs and action cameras like the GoPro Hero 13 and Insta360 X4. Because budget matters to most travelers, every interviewee is also asked to name a cheaper alternative kit that would still work in their city.

    When will the interviews be published?

    New episodes are published weekly, starting with East Asia. Each published interview is linked from this hub page, and this page is updated the same day. Bookmark this page or subscribe to the newsletter to follow the series.

    Can I suggest a city or a videographer to interview?

    Yes. If you film professionally in one of the 50 cities — or you know someone who does — reach out through the contact details in the footer. We prioritize candidates who can show recent, verifiable work shot in their city.
