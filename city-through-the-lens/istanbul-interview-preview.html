Local videographers reveal how they film their hometown — the real locations, permit hurdles, and techniques you won't find in guidebooks.

Quick Takeaways for Filmmakers

  • Best filming season: Mediterranean climate; hot summers, cool winters
  • Top 3 landmarks: Hagia Sophia, Blue Mosque, Grand Bazaar
  • Main airport: Istanbul Airport
  • Drone rules: require military approval.
  • Featured camera: Sony A7C II

3 Must-Film Locations in Istanbul

1. Hagia Sophia

Filming Tip: Free entry; prayer times close to tourists; no flash; modest dress.

2. Blue Mosque

Filming Tip: Avoid prayer times; remove shoes; women cover hair; tripods restricted.

3. Grand Bazaar

Filming Tip: Opens 8:30 AM; go early; negotiate politely; tight aisles = wide lens.

Weather Reality Check

Mediterranean climate; hot summers, cool winters. Best Apr-Jun and Sep-Oct.

Pro tip: Plan your shoot dates around these conditions. The "best" months mean better light, fewer crowds, and more predictable weather windows.

Airport Transfer & Arrival Tips

Istanbul Airport (IST) 45min metro or Sabiha Gökçen (SAW) 60min bus.

Factor in transfer time when booking flights. Arriving early gives you buffer for gear checks and acclimatization before shooting.

Permits, Rules & Local Etiquette

Mosques allow personal filming. Drones require military approval.

Remember: Always ask before filming people, especially in religious or residential areas. Respect local customs — it's the difference between getting great footage and being asked to delete it.
