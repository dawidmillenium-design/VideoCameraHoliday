The 2026 Travel Camera Winners

  • Best Overall: DJI Osmo Pocket 3 — the perfect balance of portability, image quality, and ease of use.
  • Best Action: GoPro Hero 13 — still the king of rugged, waterproof adventure filming.
  • Best Vlogging: Sony ZV-E10 II — unmatched autofocus and flip-screen usability.
  • Best Compact: Fujifilm X-M5 — retro charm meets serious image quality.
  • Best 360: Insta360 X5 — invisible selfie stick magic at 8K resolution.
  • Best Wearable: Insta360 GO 3S — 39g of hands-free POV freedom.
  • Best Budget: DJI Action 5 Pro — 4-hour battery and 20m waterproofing under $350.
  • Best Premium: Sony a7CR — full-frame power in a sub-520g body.
  • Best Phone: iPhone 16 Pro — the camera you already have in your pocket.

2026 has been a landmark year for travel cameras. Handheld gimbal cameras have dominated the market, AI-powered workflows have transformed editing, and vertical video has become the default format for destination content. After spending 12 months testing more than 50 cameras across six continents — from the neon-lit streets of Tokyo to the dusty dunes of the Sahara — we've narrowed the field down to the absolute best in every category.

Whether you're a weekend warrior, a full-time travel vlogger, or a parent trying to capture family memories, there's a winner here for you.

🏆 Best Overall

DJI Osmo Pocket 3

The Pocket 3 has earned our top spot for the second year running, and for good reason. Its 1-inch sensor delivers stunning low-light performance, the 3-axis gimbal produces buttery-smooth footage without any learning curve, and the entire system fits in a jacket pocket. From night markets in Bangkok to family dinners in Rome, this camera has been our most-used tool of 2026. The rotating screen for instant vertical video switching is the feature we didn't know we needed until we couldn't live without it.

Price: $519 | Read our full DJI Osmo Pocket 3 review

Runner-up: DJI Osmo Pocket 3 Creator Combo — adds the DJI Mic 2 and wide-angle lens for the ultimate out-of-the-box vlogging kit.
🏔️ Best Action

GoPro Hero 13 Black

Despite fierce competition from DJI, the GoPro Hero 13 remains the default choice for adventure travelers. Its modular lens system, HyperSmooth 6.0 with HorizonLock, and 10m waterproof depth make it the most versatile action camera on the market. The new lens mods (Ultra Wide, Macro, and Pristine) unlock creative possibilities no other action cam can match. For mountain biking, surfing, skiing, and dune bashing, this is still the camera we reach for first.

Price: $399 | Read our full GoPro Hero 13 review

Runner-up: DJI Action 5 Pro — superior battery life (4 hours) and better low-light performance, but lacks GoPro's lens mod ecosystem.
🎤 Best Vlogging

Sony ZV-E10 II

The ZV-E10 II is the camera that made us stop recommending smartphones for serious vloggers. Its AI-powered Real-Time Tracking autofocus is genuinely magical — it locks onto eyes and never lets go, even when subjects turn away or pass behind obstacles. The fully articulating screen, Product Showcase mode, and cinematic vlog settings make it the most intuitive dedicated vlogging camera of 2026. Pair it with the Sigma 16mm f/1.4 and you have a professional-grade setup for under $1,500.

Price: $998 with kit lens | Read our Sony ZV-E10 II Night Market Test

Runner-up: Canon EOS R50 V Creator Kit — better straight-out-of-camera colors and includes mic/grip, but slightly behind in autofocus speed.
🎒 Best Compact

Fujifilm X-M5

The X-M5 captures the magic of travel photography better than any other compact camera we tested this year. Its legendary Film Simulations (Classic Chrome, Nostalgic Neg, Reala Ace) produce stunning straight-out-of-camera JPEGs that require zero editing — a massive time-saver on the road. The retro dial-based controls encourage mindful, deliberate shooting, and the APS-C sensor delivers image quality that embarrasses many full-frame cameras from just a few years ago. At 355g with the kit lens, it's a joy to carry all day.

Price: $799 with kit lens

Runner-up: Sony ZV-1 II — fixed-lens simplicity with a bright f/1.8-2.8 zoom, perfect for those who don't want to change lenses.
🌐 Best 360

Insta360 X5

The X5 took everything great about the X4 and elevated it with an 8K sensor, improved low-light performance, and AI-powered reframing that actually works. The "invisible selfie stick" effect continues to deliver third-person drone-style shots without the drone, and the FlowState stabilization remains best-in-class. For adventure travelers who want to capture every angle and decide the framing later, this is the only camera that truly delivers.

Price: $599 | Read our Insta360 X5 review

Runner-up: Insta360 X4 — still excellent at 8K and often available at a discount, making it the best value 360 camera.
👕 Best Wearable/POV

Insta360 GO 3S

At just 39 grams, the GO 3S is less a camera and more a memory extension. We wore it skiing, biking, cooking, and playing with kids — and it captured authentic first-person moments that no traditional camera could match. The 4K upgrade over the GO 3 makes the footage finally viable for social media without heavy upscaling. The Action Pod transforms it into a complete vlogging setup with viewfinder and extended battery. For POV storytelling, nothing else comes close.

Price: $349 | Read our Insta360 GO 3S POV Test

Runner-up: GoPro Hero 13 with Chesty mount — bulkier but more rugged for extreme sports.
💰 Best Budget

DJI Action 5 Pro

The Action 5 Pro is the best value in travel cameras, period. At $349, you get a 1/1.3" sensor that outperforms many cameras twice its price in low light, 4-hour battery life that destroys every competitor, 20m waterproof depth, and 47GB of internal storage as a safety net. We took it to the Sahara, the beaches of Bali, and the ski slopes of Austria — and it never let us down. For travelers who want rugged, reliable footage without breaking the bank, this is the answer.

Price: $349 | Read our DJI Action 5 Pro Adventure Test

Runner-up: Insta360 GO 3S — cheaper at $349 but shorter battery life; better for POV than general action.
💎 Best Premium

Sony a7CR

The a7CR is the camera that proves you don't need to sacrifice image quality for portability. Packing 61 megapixels of full-frame resolution into a 515g body, it delivers the kind of detail and dynamic range that makes landscape photographers weep. The AI-powered autofocus tracks everything from hummingbirds to mountain goats, and the 5-axis IBIS means you can leave the gimbal at home. For professionals and serious enthusiasts who demand the absolute best, this is it.

Price: $2,998 body only | Read our Sony a7CR vs Fujifilm X-T50 comparison

Runner-up: Fujifilm X-T50 — half the price, twice the joy, with legendary Film Simulations and tactile controls.
📱 Best Smartphone Alternative

iPhone 16 Pro

For many travelers, the best camera is the one they already have. The iPhone 16 Pro's computational photography, ProRes video, and seamless ecosystem integration make it a legitimate primary camera for casual travel content. The 5x telephoto lens finally delivers useful reach, and the Action Mode stabilization rivals dedicated gimbals in good light. If you're not ready to commit to a dedicated camera, this is the phone to get.

Price: $999+ | Read our iPhone 16 Pro Video Review

Runner-up: Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra — better zoom range and S Pen for remote triggering, but slightly behind in video quality.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it worth upgrading from a 2024 camera in 2026?

It depends on what you're upgrading from. If you have a DJI Pocket 2 or GoPro Hero 10, the improvements in low-light performance, stabilization, and AI features are substantial enough to justify the upgrade. If you have a 2025 flagship (like the Pocket 3 or Hero 12), the gains are more incremental — wait for 2027 unless you have a specific need.

Should I buy a dedicated camera or just use my phone?

If you're posting casually to Instagram and don't care about low-light performance or creative control, your phone is probably fine. But if you want better audio, more dynamic range, optical zoom, or the ability to shoot in challenging conditions (night markets, deserts, underwater), a dedicated camera makes a massive difference. Start with a Pocket 3 or Action 5 Pro to see if the hobby sticks.

What's the best camera for a complete beginner?

The DJI Osmo Pocket 3 or the Insta360 GO 3S. Both are essentially point-and-shoot with zero learning curve, yet deliver professional-quality results. Avoid interchangeable lens cameras until you understand the basics of exposure, composition, and editing.

Will these cameras still be good in 2027?

Absolutely. Every camera on this list shoots 4K video, has excellent stabilization, and produces images that will look great on social media and prints for years to come. Camera technology evolves, but the fundamentals of good footage — clean exposure, sharp focus, stable framing — don't change. These cameras nail the fundamentals.

What about the new 2026 releases like the Osmo Pocket 4 or Insta360 Luna Ultra?

We're keeping a close eye on these upcoming releases and will update this roundup as soon as we've completed full field testing. Based on early specs, they look promising, but until we've tested them in real-world conditions, the cameras listed above remain our proven, battle-tested recommendations.
