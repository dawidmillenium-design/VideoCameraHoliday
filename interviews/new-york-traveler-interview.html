Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Insta360 X5 good for filming in New York City crowds?

Yes. Marcus films in Times Square by holding the X5 out on the invisible stick, and because the camera sees everything, the result looks like a drone shot. Traditional cameras struggle in that chaos; the X5 does not.

How do you upload 8K travel footage on slow hotel Wi-Fi?

Back up locally and only upload the edited 4K version at night. Marcus reports Manhattan hotel speeds as low as 5 Mbps, where uploading everything to Insta360 Cloud takes hours, so selective nighttime uploads are the practical workaround.

What is the invisible stick effect on the Insta360 X5?

It is a selfie stick the camera automatically erases from footage, so solo shots look like they were filmed by a drone. Marcus calls it the main reason he bought the X5, since he no longer needs to ask strangers to film him.

Is 8K worth it for travel videos?

Yes, mainly for the cropping flexibility. Marcus uses the 8K resolution to crop down to perfect 1080p angles after the trip, and he finds editing the footage super fast despite having more material than he knows what to do with.
