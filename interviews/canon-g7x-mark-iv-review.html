Canon PowerShot G7 X Mark IV — First Look & Travel Review

Canon's legendary 'influencer camera' just got a major upgrade. We tested the Mark IV on a city break — here's what's new and if it's worth the hype.

🎬

Dawid Millennium — Travel Videographer

Travel vlogger since 2019. Tested 50+ cameras on real trips across Southeast Asia, Europe, and South America.

In Short

  • The Mark IV finally adds 4K/60fps and a fully articulating screen, addressing the biggest complaints of the Mark III.
  • Canon's legendary color science remains intact, producing beautiful skin tones straight out of the camera.
  • The improved autofocus is significantly faster and more reliable, especially for video tracking.
  • It remains a bit pricey compared to competitors, but the build quality and user experience justify the cost for many.

The Canon PowerShot G7 X series has long been the go-to compact camera for vloggers and influencers, thanks to its flattering color science and pocketable size. With the release of the G7 X Mark IV, Canon has listened to user feedback and addressed the most glaring omissions of its predecessor. We took the Mark IV on a weekend trip to London to see if it can reclaim its crown as the best compact vlogging camera on the market.

What's New in Mark IV?

The most significant upgrades are in video capabilities and usability. The Mark IV now shoots 4K video at 60fps, a massive leap from the 30fps limit of the Mark III. It also features a fully articulating touchscreen, finally allowing for easy framing when shooting from high or low angles. The autofocus system has been overhauled with Canon's latest Dual Pixel CMOS AF II, promising faster and more accurate subject tracking.

Real Test: City Walking & Street Photography

Walking through the bustling streets of London, the G7 X Mark IV felt right at home. Its compact size made it easy to carry all day, and the improved grip provided a secure hold even when shooting one-handed. The 24-100mm equivalent zoom range is perfect for street photography, allowing you to capture wide architectural shots and zoom in for candid portraits without changing lenses. The image stabilization did a commendable job of smoothing out handheld footage while walking.

Real Test: Food & Indoor Shots (The classic G7 X use case)

The G7 X series is famous for its food photography capabilities, and the Mark IV does not disappoint. The bright f/1.8-2.8 lens excels in low-light restaurant environments, producing creamy bokeh that makes dishes look incredibly appetizing. Canon's color science shines here, rendering warm, inviting tones that require minimal editing. The touchscreen interface makes it easy to tap to focus on specific elements of a dish, ensuring sharpness where it matters most.

Video Quality & Vlogging Features

Video quality is excellent, with sharp 4K/60fps footage that looks professional straight out of the camera. The fully articulating screen is a game-changer for vloggers, allowing for easy self-framing without the need for a mirror. The new autofocus system locks onto faces quickly and holds focus reliably, even when moving around. Audio quality from the built-in microphone is decent, but for serious vlogging, an external mic via the 3.5mm jack is still recommended.

G7 X Mark IV vs. Sony ZV-1 II

The Sony ZV-1 II is the most direct competitor to the G7 X Mark IV. While the Sony offers a wider 18-50mm lens and slightly better autofocus for video, the Canon wins on color science and overall build quality. The G7 X Mark IV produces more flattering skin tones straight out of the camera, which is a significant advantage for vloggers who want to minimize editing time. Ultimately, the choice comes down to personal preference: Sony for technical video features, Canon for aesthetics and ease of use.

Who Should Buy It?

The Canon PowerShot G7 X Mark IV is the ideal camera for vloggers, content creators, and travel photographers who prioritize image quality, color science, and ease of use. If you want a compact camera that produces beautiful photos and videos with minimal effort, this is the camera for you. However, if you need the absolute best autofocus for fast-moving subjects or prefer a wider lens for architecture, you might want to consider the Sony ZV-1 II.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the G7 X Mark IV have a microphone input?

Yes, it features a 3.5mm microphone jack, allowing you to connect external microphones for improved audio quality during vlogging and video recording.

Is the G7 X Mark IV good for beginners?

Absolutely. Its intuitive touchscreen interface, automatic modes, and excellent image quality make it very user-friendly for beginners, while still offering manual controls for those who want to grow their skills.

How is the battery life?

Battery life is decent, lasting for about 260 shots or 50 minutes of continuous 4K video recording. For a full day of shooting, it's recommended to carry a spare battery.
