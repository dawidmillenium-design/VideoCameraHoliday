Shooting in the Rain

London weather is famously unpredictable. You can have sunshine and a downpour in the same hour. The Insta360 X5 is IPX8 waterproof, meaning you can shoot in heavy rain without a housing. However, water droplets on the lenses will ruin your 360 stitch. The solution? Keep a microfiber cloth in your pocket and wipe the lenses every 5 minutes. Alternatively, apply a cheap hydrophobic coating to the lenses so water beads right off.

The Condensation Trap

The real danger in London isn't the rain; it's the condensation. When you walk from a cold, rainy street into a warm, crowded pub, moisture will instantly form inside the camera lenses. Pro tip: Put the X5 in a sealed Ziploc bag before you go inside. Let it acclimate to the room temperature for 20 minutes before taking it out. This prevents internal fogging that can ruin your shoot.

FAQ

Is the X5 completely waterproof for Thames river trips?

Yes, but be careful. The X5 is waterproof to 10m without a case. However, if you are on a boat, avoid submerging it in salty or dirty river water. Rinse it with fresh water immediately after any splashes to prevent corrosion on the charging port.
