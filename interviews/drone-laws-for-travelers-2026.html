Drone Laws for Travelers — Where You Can and Can't Fly (2026)

Drone footage is stunning. But breaking the law is expensive. Here's a country-by-country guide to drone regulations for travelers — from Europe to Asia to the Americas.

⚡ Golden Rule: Always check local laws before flying. Fines can be thousands of dollars. Some countries ban drones entirely.

Drone Regulations by Region

🌍 Europe (EU)

  • Registration required for all drones
  • "Open" category rules:
    • Sub-250g: Minimal restrictions
    • 250g-2kg: Must stay below 120m, keep line of sight
    • Flight over people: Requires specific license
  • Popular countries: Italy, France, Spain, Germany — all follow EU rules

🇺🇸 North America (USA, Canada)

USA: FAA registration for all drones >250g

  • Recreational flyer: FAA test (TRUST) required
  • Commercial flyer: Part 107 license
  • National parks: Most prohibit drones
  • City rules: Many cities ban drones in public spaces

Canada: Transport Canada rules

  • Basic vs. Advanced license
  • 120m altitude limit
  • Avoid air traffic zones

🌏 Asia (Japan, Singapore, China)

Japan: Drones under 200g require no registration; heavier require permit

  • No flights over populated areas
  • No night flights

Singapore: Registration required for all drones >250g

  • No flights over "protected" areas
  • Heavy fines for violations

China: Strict registration rules, real-name requirement

  • Many cities have drone bans

🇦🇺 Australia / New Zealand

Australia: CASA rules — register if >250g

  • Stay below 120m
  • Keep line of sight
  • Avoid national parks without permit

New Zealand: Similar to Australia

  • No flights over people
  • Keep below 120m
  • Respect Māori cultural sites

🇦🇪 Middle East (UAE, Saudi Arabia)

UAE: Registration required, permit needed

  • No flights over "sensitive" areas
  • Heavy fines for violations
  • Ramadan restrictions

Pro Tips for Drone Travel

  • Always carry proof of registration (printed and digital)
  • Download offline maps of drone-restricted zones
  • Check weather conditions before flying
  • Insurance: Consider liability insurance (some countries require it)
  • Safety: Never fly over crowds, airports, or sensitive sites
  • Weight: Sub-250g drones are the safest for hassle-free travel
