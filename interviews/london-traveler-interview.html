London Traveler Interview: Why the Insta360 X5 is the Ultimate Rain Camera

A UK traveler reveals how the Insta360 X5 performs in unpredictable London weather, and the secret to uploading videos on slow cafe Wi-Fi.

🎬

Dawid Millennium — Travel Videographer

Travel vlogger since 2019. Tested 50+ cameras on real trips across Southeast Asia, Europe, and South America.

In Short

  • The X5's waterproofing makes it a reliable camera for London's sudden downpours.
  • Raindrops on the lens are fixable in post thanks to 360 reframing.
  • FlowState stabilization removes London Underground train shake, even while running.
  • Cafe Wi-Fi is too slow for 8K uploads; switch to 5G mobile data.
  • The X5 excels at moody low-light shots; supplement lighting after sunset.

London is infamous for its sudden downpours, yet its charm lies in the moody, wet streets. We interviewed Sarah, a UK-based traveler who uses the Insta360 X5 to capture this unique atmosphere. To see our top picks for these conditions, read our Best Camera for Winter Holidays: Ski & Snowboard guide.

Battling the British Weather

Traveler: “The X5’s waterproofing is a lifesaver! I shoot in the rain all the time. The 360 view means I don’t have to worry about raindrops hitting my lens at the exact wrong moment; I just fix it in post. Always keep your gear safe and dry by checking out our Best Waterproof Camera Bags for Travel guide.”

Stabilization on the Underground

Traveler: “It is incredible. The FlowState stabilization completely removes the shake of the London Underground train. It feels smooth even when I’m running for a train.”

Uploading from Coffee Shops

Traveler: “The biggest issue is cafe Wi-Fi! It’s painfully slow for uploading large 8K files. I end up switching to my 5G mobile data. In low light, though, the X5 is unbeatable for capturing moody, cinematic shots. I use my Travel Video Lighting Guide to supplement the lighting when the sun goes down.”

Sarah’s Final Thoughts

Traveler: “I love the versatility. I can shoot architecture, then flip to vlogging seamlessly. It’s the perfect camera for a dynamic city like London.”

Conclusion

Rain or shine, the X5 provides unmatched reliability and immersive perspectives. Before you head out into a wet climate, make sure your equipment is protected from the elements. Read our Best Travel Camera Insurance guide to secure your gear.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Insta360 X5 a good camera for rainy cities like London?

Yes, the X5 handles sudden downpours well because its waterproofing lets you shoot in the rain all the time. Any raindrops hitting the lens at the wrong moment can be fixed in post thanks to the 360 view.

How does the X5 handle shaky train and Underground footage?

FlowState stabilization completely removes the shake of the London Underground. Footage stays smooth even when you are running for a train.

How can I upload 8K travel videos on slow cafe Wi-Fi?

Switch to 5G mobile data when cafe Wi-Fi proves painfully slow for large 8K files. This is the practical workaround for uploading on the go in the city.

Is the Insta360 X5 good for low-light city shooting?

Yes, the X5 is unbeatable for capturing moody, cinematic low-light shots in the city. A supplemental lighting setup helps once the sun goes down.

Is the X5 versatile enough for a dynamic city trip?

Yes, it flips seamlessly between shooting architecture and vlogging. That versatility makes it a strong fit for a dynamic city like London.
