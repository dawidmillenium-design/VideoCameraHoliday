Best ND Filters & Polarizers for Travel Video — 2026 Guide

Filters are the most overlooked accessory — yet essential for professional-looking video. We tested the best ND filters and polarizers for travel filmmaking.

⚡ Quick Verdict: For most travel videographers, the Hoya Variable ND is the best all-round filter. For landscapes, the Tiffen CPL is essential.

Why You Need Filters for Travel Video

  • ND Filter: Controls light — allows wide apertures in bright conditions
  • Variable ND: Adjustable — perfect for changing light (e.g., sunrise to sunset)
  • CPL (Circular Polarizer): Reduces glare, boosts colors — essential for water, sky, and foliage
  • UV Filter: Basic lens protection — controversial but useful in dusty/sandy conditions

Best ND Filters for Travel

B+W Neutral Density

Price: ~$80-150

Best For: Premium, highest quality

German-made, excellent color neutrality, no color cast. The gold standard.

Hoya Variable ND

Price: ~$60-120

Best For: Most travelers, great value

Good quality, affordable, widely available. Covers 85% of travel scenarios.

K&F Concept Variable ND

Price: ~$40-80

Best For: Budget-conscious, magnetic mount

Excellent value with magnetic attachment. Ideal for vloggers who need quick lens changes.

Tiffen Variable ND

Price: ~$50-100

Best For: American-made, reliable

Solid build, good color, trusted by Hollywood filmmakers.

Best CPL Polarizers for Travel

Tiffen CPL

Price: ~$40-80

Best For: Landscapes, water, and skies

Industry standard. Reduces glare and boosts saturation in blue skies and water.

B+W CPL

Price: ~$70-120

Best For: Premium quality, German glass

Exceptional color neutrality and build quality.

K&F Concept CPL

Price: ~$30-60

Best For: Budget, magnetic mount

Great value for the money. Magnetic system makes quick work.

Best UV Filters for Lens Protection

  • Recommended: Hoya UV or B+W UV — clear, multi-coated, and durable
  • When to Use: Beach, desert, hiking, or travel where dust/sand is a concern
  • Note: Some photographers argue UV filters reduce image quality — we find good brands solve this problem.

Which Filter Should You Buy?

  • If you want one filter for most travel scenarios: Hoya Variable ND — covers 85% of needs
  • If you film in changing light (sunrise to sunset): K&F Concept Variable ND with magnetic mount — quick adjustments
  • If you shoot landscapes, water, or sky: Tiffen CPL — essential for color
  • If you want premium quality: B+W filters — German glass, no compromise
Pro Tip: "A Variable ND filter is the best investment for most travel videographers. It replaces 3-4 fixed filters and covers 85% of your travel scenarios."
