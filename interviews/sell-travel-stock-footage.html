Every travel creator sits on a goldmine of unused footage. Those beautiful B-roll clips you shot but never used in your vlogs? They could be generating passive income for years. Stock footage agencies are actively seeking authentic, high-quality travel content — and they'll pay you for it.

This guide walks you through exactly what sells, which agencies to use, and how to keyword your footage for maximum sales.

Why Sell Stock Footage?

  • Passive Income: Upload once, earn royalties forever. A single popular clip can generate $50-$500+ per month.
  • Monetize Unused Footage: Turn those beautiful B-roll clips sitting on your hard drive into cash.
  • Low Effort: You're already shooting the footage — just upload it with proper keywords.
  • Global Reach: Your footage can be licensed by advertisers, filmmakers, and businesses worldwide.

What Travel Footage Actually Sells

Contrary to popular belief, the best-selling travel footage isn't always the most cinematic. It's the most useful. Here's what buyers actually want:

  • Generic but Authentic: Hands holding a passport, walking through an airport, looking at a map. These are the clips advertisers use constantly.
  • Vertical Video (9:16):** Massive demand for social media ads, Reels, and TikTok content. Most creators still shoot horizontal — vertical is underserved.
  • Drone Shots: Aerial views of beaches, mountains, cities, and landmarks. Always in high demand.
  • Cultural Moments: Local markets, traditional ceremonies, street food, festivals. Authentic cultural footage sells well.
  • Seasonal Content: Cherry blossoms in spring, fall foliage, Christmas markets, summer beaches. Seasonal footage has predictable demand spikes.

Technical Requirements

  • Resolution: Minimum 1080p HD, but 4K sells for 3-5x more. Always shoot in 4K if possible.
  • Frame Rate: 24fps, 30fps, or 60fps. Avoid 25fps or 50fps (PAL standards) — they sell less in the US market.
  • Codec: H.264 or ProRes. Most agencies accept MP4 files.
  • Length: 10-30 seconds is ideal. Shorter clips are harder to sell, longer clips are less versatile.
  • Stabilization: Shaky footage gets rejected. Use a gimbal or tripod, or apply stabilization in post.
  • No Logos/Brands: Remove or blur any visible logos, brand names, or recognizable trademarks.

Keywording for Maximum Sales

This is the #1 factor in whether your footage sells. Most creators under-keyword their clips. Here's the formula:

  • 15-25 Keywords: Most agencies recommend 15-25 relevant tags. Don't spam irrelevant keywords — you'll get rejected.
  • Start Specific: First 5 keywords should be the most important: location, subject, action.
  • Include Variations: "beach," "ocean," "seaside," "coast," "shore" — cover all synonyms.
  • Add Concepts: "vacation," "travel," "holiday," "relaxation," "summer" — buyers search by concept, not just subject.
  • Technical Tags: "4K," "vertical," "drone," "aerial," "slow motion" — buyers filter by technical specs.

Example Keywords for a Beach Sunset Clip:
beach, sunset, ocean, sunset, tropical, vacation, travel, holiday, relaxation, summer, paradise, palm trees, sand, waves, 4K, vertical, drone, aerial, golden hour, scenic, beautiful, nature, coastline, seaside, peaceful

Frequently Asked Questions

How much money can I make selling stock footage?

It varies wildly. Most creators make $50-$500/month in passive income. Top contributors with large portfolios (1000+ clips) can earn $2,000-$10,000+/month. Your earnings depend on portfolio size, quality, keywording, and how well your footage matches buyer demand.

Do I need to shoot in 4K?

You can sell 1080p HD footage, but 4K sells for 3-5x more and is in higher demand. If your camera shoots 4K, always use it. The extra storage cost is worth the higher royalties.

Can I sell footage with people in it?

Yes, but you need model releases signed by any recognizable people. Most agencies provide model release forms. Without a model release, your footage can only be sold as "editorial" (news, educational use), which pays less.
