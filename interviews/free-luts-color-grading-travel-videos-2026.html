Free LUTs and Color Grading Settings for Travel Videos — 2026

Color grading turns good footage into cinematic footage. Here are free LUTs and settings designed specifically for travel videos — plus a guide to applying them.

⚡ Quick Verdict: For most travel footage, start with a cinematic LUT and adjust intensity to 60-70%. For beach footage, add warmth. For night markets, add contrast.

What Are LUTs?

  • Look-Up Tables: Presets that apply color profiles to your footage
  • Why They Matter: Consistent look, professional feel, saves hours of manual grading
  • Types: Technical (log to Rec.709) and Creative (cinematic looks)

How to Apply LUTs

In Adobe Premiere Pro

  1. Open Lumetri Color panel
  2. Click "Basic Correction"
  3. Under "Input LUT," select your LUT file
  4. Adjust intensity with the slider

In DaVinci Resolve

  1. Open Color page
  2. Right-click on node → "LUT" → "Browse"
  3. Select your LUT
  4. Adjust blend

In CapCut (Mobile)

  1. Select your clip
  2. Tap "Adjust" → "LUT"
  3. Upload your LUT file
  4. Adjust strength

Pro Tips for Color Grading

  • "Shoot in flat or log profile for maximum flexibility in grading"
  • "Apply LUTs as a starting point, not a final look — always adjust"
  • "Less is more — a subtle grade looks more natural"
  • "For beach footage, warm up the white balance and add contrast"
  • "For night markets, boost shadows and add a touch of magenta"
Pro Tip: "The best LUT is the one you customize. Use the LUT as a starting point, then adjust contrast, saturation, and white balance for your specific footage."
