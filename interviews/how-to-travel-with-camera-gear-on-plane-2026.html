How to Travel with Camera Gear on a Plane — TSA & Airline Rules 2026

Traveling with expensive camera gear is stressful. Here's everything you need to know about TSA rules, battery restrictions, and packing strategies for your next flight.

⚡ Golden Rule: Always carry your camera gear in your carry-on. Never check it. Checked bags are lost, mishandled, or stolen.

Airline Rules for Camera Gear

Rule Detail
Carry-On or Checked? ALWAYS carry-on. Checked bags are lost, mishandled, or stolen.
Lithium Battery Rules Must be in carry-on (never checked). Max 100Wh per battery (most camera batteries are ~7-16Wh, so fine).
Spare Batteries Must be in carry-on, individually protected (plastic cases, tape over terminals).
Limit No strict limit, but many airlines recommend under 20 spare batteries total.

Packing Strategies for TSA

  • Keep batteries accessible: TSA often asks to inspect them separately
  • Pack gear in layers: Camera → lens → accessories → cables
  • Use a TSA-friendly bag: Some bags have fold-flat designs for easy scanning
  • Label everything: Name, phone number, email on every bag

TSA Security Tips

  1. Remove batteries from camera and place in tray
  2. Keep camera gear in a separate bin — makes scanning easier
  3. Do not pack tripods in carry-on — they can be confiscated as "blunt objects"
  4. Check camera bag size — ensure it fits under seat or overhead bin

International Travel — Country-Specific Rules

Region Considerations
Europe (EU) Similar rules to US, but stricter with lithium batteries (>160Wh prohibited)
Australia / New Zealand Biosecurity — clean gear thoroughly (no soil, sand)
Asia (Singapore, Japan, China) Very strict on battery limits — check airline-specific rules
Middle East (UAE) Strict policies — no spare batteries in checked luggage

Travel Insurance

  • "Always insure your camera gear before traveling"
  • "Check if your travel insurance covers theft and loss"
  • Recommended insurers: World Nomads, SafetyWing, Allianz

Packing Checklist for Air Travel

  • Camera body (carry-on)
  • Lenses (carry-on)
  • Batteries (carry-on, protected)
  • Charger (carry-on)
  • Tripod (CHECKED — or carry-on if small)
  • Travel insurance policy (saved on phone)
  • Bag lock (TSA-approved)
  • Backup SD cards (carry-on)
Pro Tip: "Keep your camera gear in your carry-on at all times. If you're forced to gate-check your bag, remove your camera, lenses, and batteries first and carry them on separately."
