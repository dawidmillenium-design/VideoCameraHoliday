Navigating NYC Crowds with an Invisible Stick

New York City is dense, fast, and unforgiving to slow gear. Marcus, a NYC-based creator, uses the Insta360 X5 on a 1-meter invisible stick to get drone-like shots in Times Square without the legal nightmare of flying a drone. The key? Keep the stick perfectly vertical. If you tilt it, the stitching algorithm fails, and the stick becomes visible against the bright neon signs.

The Manhattan Wi-Fi Problem

Manhattan hotel Wi-Fi is notoriously bad for uploads. Marcus paid for a "premium" hotel tier and still only got 5 Mbps upload speeds. Uploading a single 5-minute 8K 360 file took over 45 minutes and dropped three times. The Fix: Marcus now carries a Samsung T7 Shield portable SSD. He offloads footage at the hotel, then uses a local coffee shop with fiber internet to do his heavy cloud uploads while drinking a $7 latte.

FAQ

Is it legal to use a selfie stick in Times Square?

Yes, but be careful. You cannot block pedestrian traffic. Swinging a 1-meter stick in a crowded sidewalk will get you yelled at (or fined). Keep it close to your body and only extend it fully in open plazas like Washington Square Park.
