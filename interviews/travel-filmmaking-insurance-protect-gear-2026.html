Travel Filmmaking Insurance — Protecting Your Gear & Liability (2026)

Your gear is expensive. Protect it. Here's what you need to know about travel insurance for filmmakers — gear theft, liability, medical coverage, and more.

⚡ Quick Verdict: World Nomads is the best all-round travel insurance for filmmakers. SafetyWing is great for digital nomads. Allianz is best for comprehensive coverage.

Types of Coverage You Need

  • Gear Theft & Loss: Covers stolen, lost, or damaged equipment
  • Liability Insurance: If your drone causes damage or injury
  • Medical Insurance: For yourself while traveling
  • Trip Cancellation: If you can't travel due to emergencies

Best Travel Insurance for Filmmakers

World Nomads

Best For: Long-term travelers, adventure activities

Coverage: Gear theft, liability, medical, trip cancellation

Popular among creators — covers gear up to a certain limit (check policy)

SafetyWing

Best For: Digital nomads, long-term travel

Coverage: Medical, gear (add-on), liability

Affordable monthly subscriptions — good for extended trips

Allianz

Best For: Comprehensive coverage, families

Coverage: Gear, medical, trip cancellation, liability

One of the most trusted insurers — broad coverage options

PPA Insurance

Best For: Professional photographers

Coverage: Full gear replacement, liability

Specialized insurance for pros — but requires PPA membership

What to Document Before You Go

  • Serial Numbers: Of all camera bodies, lenses, and accessories
  • Photos of Gear: With receipts for proof of ownership
  • Policy Numbers: Saved on your phone and in the cloud
  • Emergency Contacts: Insurer's 24-hour helpline
  • Insurance Card: Digital copy on your phone

Packing Checklist for Insurance (Documentation)

  • Copy of travel insurance policy
  • Photos of all gear with serial numbers
  • Receipts for expensive gear
  • Emergency contact numbers
  • Digital copies saved in cloud storage

How to Make a Claim — Practical Steps

  • Report Loss Immediately: To local police (for theft) or airline (for lost luggage)
  • Gather Documentation: Police report, receipts, photos, travel itinerary
  • Contact Insurer: Use their 24-hour helpline
  • Follow Up: Keep records of all communications
Pro Tip: "Keep a spreadsheet of all your gear — including model numbers, serial numbers, and purchase dates. Store it in the cloud. If your gear is stolen, you'll have everything you need to file a claim immediately."
