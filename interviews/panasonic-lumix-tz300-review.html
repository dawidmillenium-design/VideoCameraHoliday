Panasonic Lumix TZ300 (ZS99) — The Travel Zoom Camera Returns

The legendary travel-zoom camera is back. We tested the new TZ300 (ZS99) with its 15x optical zoom and 1-inch sensor — is this the ultimate 'one camera for everything'?

🎬

Dawid Millennium — Travel Videographer

Travel vlogger since 2019. Tested 50+ cameras on real trips across Southeast Asia, Europe, and South America.

In Short

  • The 15x optical zoom combined with a 1-inch sensor offers a unique blend of reach and image quality.
  • It's the perfect camera for travelers who want to capture everything from wide landscapes to distant wildlife without changing lenses.
  • Video capabilities are decent but not class-leading; it's primarily a photography powerhouse.
  • Build quality is excellent, and the compact size makes it easy to carry all day.

Panasonic's TZ (or ZS in North America) series has long been the gold standard for travel zoom cameras, offering incredible versatility in a pocketable package. The new Lumix TZ300 (ZS99) aims to push the boundaries even further, combining a massive 15x optical zoom range with a 1-inch sensor. We took it on a trip to the Swiss Alps to see if it can truly be the only camera you need on holiday.

What Makes the TZ300 Special? (15x zoom, 1-inch sensor, compact body)

The standout feature is undoubtedly the lens. A 15x optical zoom (24-360mm equivalent) is remarkable for a camera of this size. When paired with a 1-inch sensor — significantly larger than the 1/2.3-inch sensors found in most travel zooms — it promises superior image quality, especially in low light. The body is compact and well-built, with a comfortable grip and intuitive controls that make it easy to use for both beginners and enthusiasts.

Real Test: Zoom Range — From Wide to Telephoto in Seconds

During our trip, the zoom range proved invaluable. We used the wide end (24mm) to capture sweeping panoramic views of the Alps, then quickly zoomed in to 360mm to photograph ibex grazing on distant cliffs. The zoom ring is smooth and responsive, allowing for precise framing. The ability to go from ultra-wide to super-telephoto without changing lenses is a huge advantage for travel photography, where moments can be fleeting.

Real Test: Image Quality Across the Zoom Range

Image quality is impressive, particularly at the wide end. The 1-inch sensor delivers excellent dynamic range and color accuracy, producing photos that look great straight out of the camera. As you zoom in, there is a slight drop in sharpness and an increase in noise, especially at the maximum 360mm equivalent. However, for a camera of this size and zoom range, the image quality is more than acceptable for social media and prints up to A3 size.

Video Capabilities (4K, stabilisation, vlogging)

The TZ300 shoots 4K video at 30fps, which is sufficient for most travel vloggers. The image stabilization is effective at the wide end but struggles to completely eliminate shake at full zoom. While it's not the best camera for serious vlogging due to the lack of a fully articulating screen and limited microphone options, it's perfectly capable of capturing high-quality video memories of your trip.

TZ300 vs. Smartphone (When the zoom wins)

Modern smartphones have excellent cameras, but they simply cannot match the optical zoom range of the TZ300. While phones use digital zoom to get closer, which results in a significant loss of quality, the TZ300's optical zoom maintains sharpness and detail. For capturing distant subjects like wildlife or architectural details, the TZ300 is in a completely different league. Additionally, the larger sensor provides better low-light performance and more natural bokeh.

Who Should Buy It? (vs. pocket cameras vs. phones)

The Panasonic Lumix TZ300 is the ideal camera for travelers who prioritize versatility and want a single camera that can handle any situation. If you frequently find yourself wishing you could zoom in closer with your phone or pocket camera, this is the solution. It's perfect for cruise holidays, safari trips, or any travel scenario where you encounter a mix of wide landscapes and distant subjects. However, if your primary focus is vlogging or low-light photography, you might be better served by a dedicated vlogging camera or a fast prime lens compact.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the TZ300 good for low-light photography?

Yes, the 1-inch sensor performs significantly better in low light than smaller sensors found in typical travel zooms. While it's not as good as a full-frame camera, it's more than capable for evening shots and indoor photography.

Can I use the TZ300 for vlogging?

It can record 4K video, but the lack of a fully articulating screen and limited external microphone options make it less ideal for serious vlogging compared to cameras like the Sony ZV-1 II or DJI Pocket 3.

How is the battery life?

Battery life is decent, lasting for about 300 shots on a single charge. For a full day of shooting, it's recommended to carry a spare battery.
