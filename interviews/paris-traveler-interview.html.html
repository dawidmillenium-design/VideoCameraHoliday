Museum Rules & Stealth

Parisian museums like the Louvre and Musée d'Orsay have strict rules: no tripods, no selfie sticks, and no large camera rigs. Elena loves the X5 because it looks like a small lollipop. Security guards rarely stop her. Pro tip: Do not use the invisible stick inside museums. Just hold the camera body like a point-and-shoot. The 360 lens will still capture the grandeur of the Mona Lisa room without triggering security.

Cafe Culture Vlogging

Filming in a Parisian cafe requires subtlety. You don't want to annoy other patrons. Elena uses the X5's bullet-time mode on a tiny tabletop tripod to get a sweeping 360 view of her croissant and coffee, then reframes it in post to look like a high-end commercial. The key is to keep the camera small and out of the way.

FAQ

Can I use flash with the X5 in dark museums?

No. Flash is strictly prohibited in almost all Paris museums to protect the art. The X5's low-light performance is decent, but you will need to rely on the ambient light and keep your shutter speed slow (use a mini tripod if allowed).
