GoPro Hero 13 vs. DJI Osmo Action 6 vs. Insta360 Ace Pro 2 — Action Camera Showdown (2026)

Three top action cameras — but which one survives your adventure? We tested the GoPro Hero 13, DJI Osmo Action 6, and Insta360 Ace Pro 2 on beaches, ski slopes, and everything in between.

⚡ Quick Verdict: The DJI Osmo Action 6 wins for most adventurers — best battery life, waterproof depth, and great value. The GoPro Hero 13 is still the most rugged option. The Insta360 Ace Pro 2 is best for 8K and low-light.
