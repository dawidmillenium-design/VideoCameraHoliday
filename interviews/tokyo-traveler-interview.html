Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Insta360 X5 good for filming in Tokyo?

Yes — Kenji, a Tokyo-based travel filmmaker, calls it a must-have for urban explorers. The 8K 360 capture handles crowded crossings and neon-lit streets, and the upgraded sensor manages night shots in Shinjuku without crushing shadows.

How does the Insta360 X5 handle neon and low-light shooting?

The X5's upgraded sensor performs very well at night, according to the interview. Its dynamic range handles bright neon signs beautifully while keeping shadow detail intact, making it strong for cities like Tokyo after dark.

How long does it take to upload 8K footage on Tokyo hotel Wi-Fi?

It can take around 4 hours to upload a single 20GB 8K file to the Insta360 Cloud on a poor hotel network. Kenji's workaround is to save everything locally on an industrial-grade SD card instead of relying on the cloud.

Can the Insta360 X5 replace multiple cameras for travel filming?

For 360-style coverage, yes. Kenji shoots in 8K 360 and crops to standard 4K afterward, pulling multiple angles from a single take — he describes it as having a camera crew in his pocket.

What is the biggest downside of using the X5 while traveling in Japan?

The biggest letdown is hotel internet speed, not the camera itself. Slow networks make cloud uploads of large 8K files painfully slow, so reliable local storage is essential.
