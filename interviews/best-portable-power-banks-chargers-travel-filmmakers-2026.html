Best Portable Power Banks & Chargers for Travel Filmmakers — 2026 Guide

Running out of power ruins your shoot. We tested the best power banks, fast chargers, and portable batteries for travel filmmakers — essential for long days of filming.

⚡ Quick Verdict: For most travelers, the Anker PowerCore 26800 is the best all-round power bank. For fast charging, the Samsung 25W USB-C is top.

Why Power Banks Matter for Travel Filmmakers

  • Batteries Always Die: Especially in cold weather or during long shoots
  • No Access to Power: You're in a remote location, train, or plane
  • Multiple Devices: Camera, gimbal, phone, action cam, laptop
  • Peace of Mind: Never miss a shot because of a dead battery

Charging Tips for Travel Filmmakers

  • "Look for Power Delivery (PD) for camera charging"
  • "70W+ PD chargers can charge laptops too"
  • "Solar power banks are gimmicky — avoid for travel"
  • "For day trips, bring a 10,000mAh bank; for week-long trips, bring 26,800mAh+"
  • "Label your chargers and cables — you'll thank yourself in a crowded hostel"
Pro Tip: "For camera batteries, look for PD (Power Delivery) support. It charges compatible cameras much faster than standard USB-A."
