Best Portable Power Banks & Chargers for Travel Filmmakers — 2026 Guide

Running out of power ruins your shoot. We tested the best power banks, fast chargers, and portable batteries for travel filmmakers — essential for long days of filming.

⚡ Quick Verdict: For most travelers, the Anker PowerCore 26800 is the best all-round power bank. For fast charging, the Samsung 25W USB-C is top.

Why Power Banks Matter for Travel Filmmakers

  • Batteries Always Die: Especially in cold weather or during long shoots
  • No Access to Power: You're in a remote location, train, or plane
  • Multiple Devices: Camera, gimbal, phone, action cam, laptop
  • Peace of Mind: Never miss a shot because of a dead battery

Charging Tips for Travel Filmmakers

  • "Look for Power Delivery (PD) for camera charging"
  • "70W+ PD chargers can charge laptops too"
  • "Solar power banks are gimmicky — avoid for travel"
  • "For day trips, bring a 10,000mAh bank; for week-long trips, bring 26,800mAh+"
  • "Label your chargers and cables — you'll thank yourself in a crowded hostel"
Pro Tip: "For camera batteries, look for PD (Power Delivery) support. It charges compatible cameras much faster than standard USB-A."

Camping & Off-Grid Power Solutions

Standard power banks work great in cities with outlets every few blocks — but camping, multi-day treks, or remote locations demand a different approach. Here's what works when you're truly off-grid:

  • High-Capacity Power Stations (20,000mAh+): The Anker 757 (1,229Wh) or EcoFlow River 2 can charge camera batteries 40-50 times and run small devices for days. Heavy (around 2kg/4.4lbs) but essential for week-long wilderness shoots.
  • Solar Panels (with caveats): Foldable 20W-60W panels from Goal Zero or Anker work well — but only in direct sun. Cloud cover drops output to 10-20%. Best used as a trickle-charge backup, not primary power. Avoid cheap "solar power banks" with tiny integrated panels; they're gimmicks.
  • Multi-Device Charging: Look for stations with AC outlets, USB-C PD, and wireless charging pads. You can charge your camera, phone, drone, and headlamp simultaneously at camp.
  • Battery Management: In cold weather (alpine camping), keep batteries inside your sleeping bag overnight. Cold drains lithium-ion cells fast — a warm battery in the morning beats a frozen one.
⛺ Field-Tested Setup: For a 5-day backcountry shoot: 1x 26,800mAh power bank (Anker) + 1x 20W foldable solar panel + 4 spare camera batteries. This kept us powered filming in the Scottish Highlands with zero outlet access. Total weight: ~1.2kg.
