## 🔗 Step 3: Fix Orphan Pages (Internal Linking)

### What Are Orphan Pages?
Pages with **NO internal links** pointing to them. This means:
- Search engines can't find them easily
- They don't get "link juice" from other pages
- They're less likely to rank well

### How to Fix Orphan Pages

#### Method 1: Add Links from Related Content

Example: If you have an orphan page about `comparisons/kaso-ek7000-pro-vs-gopro-hero`, add links to it from:

1. **Homepage:** "Check out our detailed comparison of the Kaso EK7000 Pro vs GoPro Hero"
2. **Guides page:** "Compare budget options: Kaso EK7000 Pro vs GoPro Hero"
3. **Related reviews:** In the GoPro Hero review, link to the comparison

#### Method 2: Create a Sitemap or Index Page

```html
<!-- Create a comparison index page -->
<h1>All Camera Comparisons</h1>
<ul>
  <li><a href="/comparisons/kaso-ek7000-pro-vs-gopro-hero">Kaso EK7000 Pro vs GoPro Hero</a></li>
  <li><a href="/comparisons/sony-zv-e10-vs-canon-r50">Sony ZV-E10 vs Canon R50</a></li>
  <!-- Add all comparison links -->
</ul>
```

#### Method 3: Add Related Posts Section

Add this to your blog post template:

```html
<!-- Related Posts -->
<div class="related-posts">
  <h3>You Might Also Like</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><a href="/comparisons/kaso-ek7000-pro-vs-gopro-hero">Kaso EK7000 Pro vs GoPro Hero</a></li>
    <li><a href="/reviews/sony-zv-1-ii-review">Sony ZV-1 II Review</a></li>
  </ul>
</div>
```
