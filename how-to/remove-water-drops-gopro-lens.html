Key Takeaways

  • Surface Tension is the Enemy: Water beads up due to tension. You need to break it with surfactants (spit, soap, coating).
  • Don't Wipe Wet: Wiping a wet lens with a dry cloth often spreads the drop into a smear. Dry first, then wipe.
  • Hydrophobic > Hydrophilic: For action cams, you want water to sheet off (hydrophobic), not stick (hydrophilic).
  • Post-Production exists: AI tools like After Effects Content-Aware Fill can remove static droplets if you shot in flat profile.

The "Spit Trick" (Quick Fix)

It sounds gross, but divers have used this for decades. Saliva acts as a natural surfactant, breaking the surface tension of water so it sheets off the glass instead of beading up.

  1. Lick the lens: Apply a thin layer of saliva to the dry lens.
  2. Rub gently: Use your finger to spread it evenly.
  3. Rinse lightly: Dip quickly in water (don't scrub).
  4. Shoot: Water should now slide off instantly.
Pro Tip: Baby shampoo works even better than spit and tastes less awful if you're using a mouth-mounted mount! Mix one drop with water in a small spray bottle.

Underwater Specifics: Red Filters & Housings

When shooting underwater, droplets often form inside the housing or on the red filter, not just the main lens.

  • Check the O-Rings: A single hair on the O-ring can cause a slow leak, creating internal condensation that looks like drops. Clean and grease O-rings before every dive.
  • Red Filter Traps: Clip-on red filters often trap water between the filter and the lens. Rinse the mount in fresh water before clipping it on underwater.
  • Silica Gel: Throw a small silica gel packet in your camera bag when storing the housing. This prevents internal fogging which mimics water drops.

Fixing Drops in Post-Production (AI)

If you already have the footage, all is not lost. If the droplet is static (on the lens) and the background is moving:

  1. After Effects: Use "Content-Aware Fill". Mask the droplet, generate a fill layer, and AE will replace the drop with pixels from surrounding frames.
  2. DaVinci Resolve: Use the "Object Removal" tool in the Color page. Track the droplet (if it moves) and blend.
  3. Mobile Apps: Apps like "TouchRetouch" (photo) or specialized video cleaners can work for short clips.

FAQ

Why does my GoPro lens have water inside?

If water is inside the lens glass (not on the surface), your seal is broken. This usually happens after a deep dive or exposure to high pressure. Disassemble carefully, dry with silica gel for 48 hours, and check O-rings.

Does Vaseline work for anti-fog?

Vaseline is too thick and oily; it will ruin your image quality. Use dedicated "Anti-Fog Inserts" (small silica tabs) that stick inside the housing instead.

Can I use windshield wiper fluid?

No. Many contain alcohol or ammonia which can degrade the plastic housing or lens coatings over time. Stick to baby shampoo or dedicated camera coatings.

How do I clean salt water off my lens?

Rinse immediately in fresh water. Salt crystals left behind will act like sandpaper if wiped. Soak the camera in fresh water for 15 mins, then dry with a microfiber cloth.
