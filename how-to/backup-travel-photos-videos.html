Backup Strategy for Travelers

One lost bag, one stolen camera, one failed card — and your memories are gone. Here's how to ensure that never happens.

☁️ The 3-2-1 Rule for Travel

  • 3 copies of every file
  • 2 different media types (cloud + physical)
  • 1 offsite copy (cloud storage)

📱 Cloud Backup Options

Google Photos

Unlimited compressed backups (free) or original quality with Google One ($9.99/mo for 2TB). Works on any phone.

iCloud

Seamless for iPhone users. 2TB for $9.99/mo. Photos sync automatically when on WiFi.

Amazon Photos

Included with Prime membership. Unlimited full-resolution photo storage. Video storage is limited but generous.

💾 Local Backup Solutions

Portable SSD

SanDisk Extreme Portable SSD (1TB, $100) or Samsung T7 Shield. Rugged, fast, and tiny. Copy cards to SSD every evening.

Wireless Hard Drive

WD My Passport Wireless SSD has an SD card slot. Insert your card, press a button, and it backs up automatically — no laptop needed.

🔄 Daily Backup Workflow

End-of-Day Routine

1
Copy to SSD

Transfer all cards to your portable SSD at the hotel.

2
Upload to Cloud

Start cloud upload on hotel WiFi before bed. Let it run overnight.

3
Keep Cards Separate

Store used cards separately from your camera. If the camera is stolen, cards in your hotel safe are safe.

4
Verify

Spot-check a few files on the SSD to confirm the copy worked.

💡 Pro Tip: Buy a tiny waterproof case for your SD cards. Keep it in a different bag than your camera. Redundancy is everything.
