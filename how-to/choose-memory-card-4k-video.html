Choose the Right Memory Card for 4K

A slow card will cause recording to stop mid-shot. A small card will fill up on day one. Here's how to choose correctly.

📊 Speed Classes Explained

SpecWhat It MeansMinimum For
Class 1010 MB/s write1080p
UHS-I U330 MB/s write4K30
UHS-II V6060 MB/s write4K60, 6K
UHS-II V9090 MB/s write8K, ProRes

💾 Capacity Recommendations

  • 1080p: 64GB is sufficient for a week
  • 4K30: 128GB minimum for a week
  • 4K60/6K: 256GB recommended
  • 8K/ProRes: 512GB or 1TB

🏆 Best Cards for Travel

Best Overall: SanDisk Extreme Pro V30 256GB

Reliable, fast enough for 4K60, and reasonably priced. The go-to choice for most travelers.

Best for 8K: Lexar Professional V60 512GB

V60 speed handles 8K recording. 512GB stores hours of footage without card swaps.

Best Budget: Samsung EVO Select 128GB

Reliable and cheap. Good for 4K30 recording. Buy multiples rather than one giant card.

⚠️ Card Failure Warning: Memory cards fail. Always carry backups, and never store all footage on a single card. Swap cards daily and backup to a second device.
