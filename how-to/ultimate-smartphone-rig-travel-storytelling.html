⚡ Quick Answer

The Ultimate Travel Rig: SmallRig Cage + Sandmarc Anamorphic Lens + DJI Mic 2 + DJI OM 6 Gimbal. Total weight: ~600g. This setup gives you cinematic widescreen footage, pro audio, and buttery smooth stabilization while fitting in a jacket pocket.

Key Takeaways

  • Cage is King: A good cage provides mounting points for mics/lights and protects your phone from drops.
  • Anamorphic Magic: Anamorphic lenses give you that true cinematic 2.40:1 aspect ratio with oval bokeh, impossible to fake digitally.
  • Audio First: Viewers forgive bad video, not bad audio. Always use an external mic (wireless or shotgun).
  • Balance Before Power: If using a gimbal, balance the rig perfectly BEFORE turning it on to save battery and motor life.

Why Build a Smartphone Rig?

Modern smartphones like the iPhone 15/16 Pro Max have sensors that rival dedicated cameras. But shooting handheld limits you. A rig unlocks:

  • Cinematic Aspect Ratios: Native anamorphic recording without cropping.
  • Professional Audio: XLR inputs via USB-C for direct mic connection.
  • Extended Battery: Mount external power banks directly to the cage.
  • Ergonomics: Proper handles reduce fatigue during long shooting days.

Assembly & Balance

Pro Tip: Assemble in this order: Phone → Cage → Lens → Mic → Gimbal. Balance the gimbal horizontally first, then vertically. If the motors strain, your rig is unbalanced.

Once assembled, the rig should feel like a single unit. Test the zoom and focus controls on the gimbal handle to ensure they reach your phone screen.

FAQ

Does anamorphic work on Android?

Yes! Samsung S24 Ultra and Pixel 9 Pro work great with clip-on anamorphic lenses. Just ensure the clip fits your specific model.

Do I need ND filters?

Highly recommended. ND filters let you keep your shutter speed at 1/50 in bright sunlight without overexposing. Many cages have threads for screw-on ND filters.

Is this rig allowed on planes?

Yes. It fits in carry-on easily. Just remove the gimbal batteries if they are removable (most aren't, so keep it in your bag).

How do I desqueeze the footage?

In editing apps (Premiere, DaVinci, CapCut), apply a "Desqueeze" effect or scale the width by 1.33x (or 75% height) to restore normal proportions.
