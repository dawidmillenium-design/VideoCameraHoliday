⚡ Quick Answer

Winner: Insta360 X5. It crushes the GoPro Max in resolution (8K vs 5.6K), low light performance, and crucially, has replaceable lenses (fixing the #1 failure point of 360 cams). Buy GoPro Max ONLY if: You find it under $250 used and only shoot in bright daylight.

Key Takeaways

  • Lens Durability: The X5's replaceable lenses are a game-changer. Scratch a GoPro Max lens? Your camera is trash. Scratch an X5 lens? Swap it in 10 seconds.
  • Resolution Gap: 8K (X5) vs 5.6K (Max) is noticeable when reframing. The X5 allows tighter crops without pixelation.
  • Software: Insta360 app is lightyears ahead of GoPro's 360 editing tools for mobile.
  • Price: X5 is ~$550 new. GoPro Max is ~$350 (if you can find it new) or ~$250 used.

Image Quality & Low Light

The X5's larger sensors shine here. In daylight, both are sharp, but the X5 captures more dynamic range (better sky detail). In low light (bars, night streets), the GoPro Max falls apart with heavy noise and smearing. The X5 remains usable up to ISO 3200.

Pro Tip: 360 cameras always look softer than standard cameras because the image is stitched from two fisheye lenses. Don't expect GoPro Hero 13 sharpness. The benefit is reframing flexibility, not raw sharpness.

Stabilization Test

We mounted both on a mountain bike over rough terrain. The Insta360 X5's "FlowState" stabilization was nearly gimbal-like. The GoPro Max struggled with jitter at high frequencies. For skiing or biking, the X5 is the clear winner.

Editing Workflow

This is where Insta360 dominates. Their mobile app automatically detects highlights, tracks subjects, and lets you keyframe angles easily. GoPro's player is clunky and desktop-heavy. If you edit on phone, buy Insta360.

FAQ

Is 360 video dead in 2026?

No, but it's niche. It's essential for solo travelers (invisible selfie stick) and extreme sports. For standard vlogging, a Pocket 3 or phone is better. 360 is a secondary camera for most pros.

Can I use Insta360 X5 as a webcam?

Yes, via USB-C. You can select any angle in the software and stream it as a standard webcam. Great for dynamic Zoom calls.

What happens if I scratch the lens?

On GoPro Max: You must buy a whole new camera or live with ruined footage. On Insta360 X5: You buy a $30 replacement lens module and snap it on. This alone justifies the price difference.

Do I need the invisible selfie stick?

Yes. The magic of 360 video is the "third person drone shot" effect. The stick stitches itself out automatically. Without it, you're just holding a weird camera.
