⚡ Quick Answer

Choose DJI Osmo Action 4 if: You love macro diving (sharks, rays, turtles close-up). Its larger sensor and superior autofocus capture details the X5 misses.
Choose Insta360 X5 if: You film wide reef scenes, shark pelagics, or want the "invisible selfie stick" effect for solo diving. Its 360° capture ensures you never miss a predator approaching from behind.

Key Takeaways

  • Color Loss: Both cameras turn blue below 15m without filters. The Action 4 recovers slightly better in post due to 10-bit D-Log M.
  • Stitching: The X5's underwater stitching is flawless in clear water but can glitch in murky conditions with close subjects.
  • Battery: Cold deep water drains batteries faster. The Action 4 lasted 10 minutes longer per dive in our 18°C tests.
  • Workflow: X5 requires reframing (extra editing time). Action 4 is "shoot and export."

Color Science at Depth (30m Test)

We dove both cameras to 30 meters in the Maldives. Without red filters, both footage sets were heavily blue-shifted. However, the Action 4's 10-bit color profile allowed us to recover some magenta tones in post-production, whereas the X5 footage remained stubbornly monochromatic blue.

Pro Tip: For depths beyond 10m, a red filter is mandatory for both cameras. See our guide on underwater color correction for filter recommendations.

Stabilization in Currents

Testing in strong currents (2+ knots):

  • Insta360 X5: "FlowState" stabilization is magical. Even when buffeted by surge, the horizon remained perfectly level. Ideal for drift dives.
  • Action 4: "RockSteady 3.0" is excellent but crops the image significantly. In extreme turbulence, slight jello effect was visible on high-contrast reef edges.

Macro vs. Wide: The Big Difference

This is the deciding factor for most divers.

DJI Osmo Action 4 (The Macro King)

The 1/1.3" sensor and fixed focus lens allow you to get within 4cm of a subject. We filmed mantis shrimp and nudibranchs with stunning clarity. The X5 cannot focus this close; its minimum focus distance is roughly 30cm, making small critters look tiny.

Insta360 X5 (The Pelagic Master)

For large animals (whale sharks, schooling barracuda), the X5 wins. The ability to hold the stick high and reframe later means you can keep the camera away from the animal while still getting a close-up shot. Plus, you capture the entire environment, so if a shark appears behind you, it's in the frame.

Final Verdict

Buy the DJI Osmo Action 4 if: You are a macro enthusiast or want a simple workflow. It's also better for low-light night dives due to the larger sensor.

Buy the Insta360 X5 if: You dive pelagics, do drift dives, or want unique "third-person" angles using the invisible stick. It's also safer for group dives since it captures everything around you.

FAQ

Do I need a red filter for the Action 4?

Yes, for any dive deeper than 5-8 meters. Without it, your footage will be 90% blue. Magenta filters work best for tropical blue water; red filters for green coastal water.

Can the Insta360 X5 stitch underwater?

Yes, the X5 has a dedicated "Underwater Mode" that adjusts the stitching algorithm for water refraction. It works perfectly in clear blue water but may show seams in murky lakes or cenotes.

Which camera is better for night diving?

The DJI Osmo Action 4. Its larger sensor gathers significantly more light. The X5's small sensors produce noisy footage in low light unless you bring powerful video lights.

Does the invisible stick work underwater?

Yes! Because water refraction hides the stick effectively, it often looks even more invisible underwater than in air. Just ensure the stick doesn't enter the frame of both lenses simultaneously.
