⚔️ Canon PowerShot V1 vs Sony ZV-1 II (2026)

Two of the best compact video cameras for travel — but which one is right for you? We compare 4K video, autofocus, audio, IBIS, and real-world travel performance.

📅 Tested: Sep 2026 🎥 Both 4K/60 💰 Price: $850 vs $899

Quick Verdict: The Sony ZV-1 II is the better all-rounder for vlogging — lighter, better autofocus, and more compact. The Canon PowerShot V1 wins for enthusiasts who need log recording, built-in ND, and a wider lens. Choose ZV-1 II for ease of use; choose V1 for creative control.

🎬 Real-World Travel Performance

📸 Video Quality

Canon PowerShot V1: The 10-bit C-Log 3 is a game-changer for colour grading. You get far more latitude in post-production than the Sony's 8-bit S‑Log. The built-in ND filter is brilliant for bright travel conditions — you can keep your shutter angle correct without stacking filters. The cooling fan means you can shoot 4K/60 indefinitely.

Sony ZV-1 II: The 1" sensor produces crisp, punchy video out of the box. S‑Log is good, but 8-bit can band in the sky. The lack of ND means you'll need a screw-on filter or accept a faster shutter speed. For short clips and social media, it's fantastic — but for serious grading, the Canon wins.

🎯 Autofocus

Sony ZV-1 II: This is Sony's territory. The Real-time Tracking and Product Showcase mode lock onto subjects instantly and reliably. For vlogging, it's nearly flawless.

Canon PowerShot V1: Dual Pixel AF II is very good, but it's not quite at Sony's level. It's fast and accurate, but occasionally hunts in low light. If you rely heavily on autofocus, Sony is the safer bet.

🎤 Audio

Both have 3-capsule mic arrays. The Canon has a slight edge with its built-in audio processing, but the Sony's wind reduction is better for outdoor vlogging. For critical audio, both have 3.5mm mic jacks — use an external mic for best results.

✈️ Portability

Sony ZV-1 II: At 292g, it's noticeably lighter and more compact. It fits in a jacket pocket easily.

Canon PowerShot V1: At 426g, it's heavier than the Sony but still manageable. The extra weight is the price you pay for the larger sensor, ND filter, and cooling fan.

🤔 Which One Should You Buy?

Choose Canon PowerShot V1 if…

  • You want 10-bit Log for colour grading
  • You shoot in bright sunlight (ND filter needed)
  • You shoot long takes (cooling fan)
  • You want a wider lens (16mm vs 18mm)
  • You prioritise creative control over portability

Choose Sony ZV-1 II if…

  • You want reliable, sticky autofocus
  • You want a lightweight, travel-friendly body
  • You shoot short clips for social media
  • You prefer out-of-camera colour
  • You're on a consistent budget (~$899)

🏆 Final Verdict

Sony ZV-1 II – Best for most travel vloggers

The ZV-1 II is lighter, has better autofocus, and is easier to use straight out of the box. If you want a compact vlogging camera that just works, this is it.


Canon PowerShot V1 – Best for enthusiast creators

The V1 offers 10-bit Log, a built-in ND filter, and a cooling fan — real pro features in a compact body. If you're serious about grading and creative control, choose this.
