⚔️ Canon PowerShot V1 vs Sony ZV-1 II (2026)

Two of the best compact video cameras for travel — but which one is right for you? We compare 4K video, autofocus, audio, IBIS, and real-world travel performance.

📅 Tested: Sep 2026 🎥 Both 4K/60 💰 Price: $850 vs $899

Quick Verdict: The Sony ZV-1 II is the better all-rounder for vlogging — lighter, better autofocus, and more compact. The Canon PowerShot V1 wins for enthusiasts who need log recording, built-in ND, and a wider lens. Choose ZV-1 II for ease of use; choose V1 for creative control.

🎬 Real-World Travel Performance

Canon PowerShot V1: The 10-bit C-Log 3 is a game-changer for colour grading. The built-in ND filter is brilliant for bright travel conditions. The cooling fan means you can shoot 4K/60 indefinitely.

Sony ZV-1 II: The 1" sensor produces crisp, punchy video out of the box. S‑Log is good, but 8-bit can band in the sky. For short clips and social media, it's fantastic — but for serious grading, the Canon wins.

🏆 Final Verdict

Sony ZV-1 II – Best for most travel vloggers

Canon PowerShot V1 – Best for enthusiast creators
