🛡️ How to Protect Your Gear at the Beach

If you insist on bringing your Pocket 4P or ZV-1 II to the coast, you must take extreme precautions:

  • Never change lenses or batteries on the beach. A single grain of salt or sand in the battery compartment will cause corrosion.
  • Use a clear plastic rain cover. Even if it's sunny, a sudden wave or sea breeze can coat your camera in saltwater. See our Guide to Protecting Gear in Rain & Sand.
  • Rinse with fresh water immediately. If your camera gets splashed, wipe it down with a damp (freshwater) microfiber cloth immediately. Do not use compressed air, as it can blow salt deeper into the seams.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I buy a waterproof case for the DJI Pocket 4P?

While third-party waterproof housings exist, they are bulky, degrade the audio quality, and often cause the gimbal to overheat. We do not recommend them for active snorkeling. Stick to a GoPro or OM System TG-7 for underwater use.

Is the Sony ZV-1 II splash-proof?

No. Sony does not rate the ZV-1 II as weather-sealed or splash-proof. Humidity and salt spray are highly dangerous to its internal components and extending lens barrel.

Which camera is better for filming a beach wedding or resort vlog?

For a resort vlog where you are talking to the camera, the Sony ZV-1 II is better due to its superior built-in microphone and face-tracking autofocus. For cinematic B-roll of the beach and walking shots, the DJI Pocket 4P provides much smoother footage.
