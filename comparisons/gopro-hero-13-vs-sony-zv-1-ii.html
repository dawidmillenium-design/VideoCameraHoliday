⚔️ GoPro Hero 13 vs Sony ZV-1 II

Action cam vs vlogging compact – which is the better travel camera for 2026? We tested both on real holidays to find out.

VS
✅ Updated: September 5, 2026 • Tested on beach, city, and adventure trips

Two cameras, two very different approaches to travel video. The GoPro Hero 13 is a rugged, waterproof action cam built for adventure. The Sony ZV-1 II is a compact vlogging camera with a 1-inch sensor and excellent autofocus. Both are popular travel cameras, but they serve completely different types of travelers.

I've tested both cameras extensively – the GoPro Hero 13 on beach holidays and water sports, the Sony ZV-1 II on city breaks and night markets. Here's my honest comparison to help you decide which one is right for your next trip.

⚡ Quick Verdict

  • Choose GoPro Hero 13 if: You're an adventure traveler who needs waterproofing, ruggedness, and a wide field of view for action shots.
  • Choose Sony ZV-1 II if: You're a vlogger or city traveler who prioritizes image quality, bokeh, and versatile focal lengths.
  • Our recommendation: The GoPro Hero 13 wins for adventure travelers. The Sony ZV-1 II wins for city break vloggers.

🌍 Real-World Testing Results

🏖️ Beach & Water (GoPro Wins)

The GoPro Hero 13 is the clear winner for beach and water activities. It's waterproof to 10m without a case, so you can snorkel, surf, and swim without worrying about your camera. The ultra-wide 155° field of view captures the full scene, and HyperSmooth 6.0 handles the motion of waves and walking on sand. The Sony ZV-1 II needs a bulky waterproof case – and even then, the 18-50mm lens is too narrow for underwater.

🏙️ City & Vlogging (Sony Wins)

The Sony ZV-1 II is the clear winner for city breaks and vlogging. The 1-inch sensor delivers superior image quality, especially in low light – night markets, street food stalls, and candlelit restaurants look fantastic. The 18-50mm zoom range gives you flexibility to frame different shots. The Product Showcase mode is a game-changer for food and product vloggers. The GoPro's ultra-wide lens is great for action but distorts faces in vlogs.

🏔️ Adventure & Hiking (GoPro Wins)

The GoPro Hero 13 is rugged, lightweight (154g vs 298g), and built to be dropped, scraped, and soaked. It's the camera you can strap to your helmet, chest, or handlebar. The Sony ZV-1 II is more delicate – you'd want to keep it in a padded bag.

🌙 Night & Low-Light (Sony Wins)

The Sony ZV-1 II's 1-inch sensor is vastly superior in low light. The GoPro Hero 13 is noisy and loses detail as soon as the sun goes down. For night markets, evening street scenes, and indoor shooting, the Sony is the only option.

🎙️ Audio Quality

Both cameras have good built-in microphones, but they excel in different scenarios:

  • GoPro Hero 13: 3 microphones with wind reduction – excellent for outdoor action shots where wind noise is a problem. The microphone array is also great for capturing immersive ambient sound.
  • Sony ZV-1 II: Directional 3-capsule microphone with a dedicated windscreen. It captures clear voice audio for vlogging – you'll sound like you're in a studio. The supplied windscreen is essential for outdoor use.

Verdict: Sony is better for vloggers who talk to camera. GoPro is better for capturing ambient sound in action shots.

🎒 Portability & Travel

  • GoPro Hero 13 (154g): Tiny, lightweight, and comes with a handy clip mount. You can attach it to your backpack, helmet, or bike. Fits in your pocket.
  • Sony ZV-1 II (298g): Heavier but still small enough for a jacket pocket. The built-in lens means you don't need to carry extra glass.

Verdict: Both are travel-friendly. The GoPro is smaller, but the Sony is still very portable for a camera with a 1-inch sensor.

🏆 The Final Verdict

Choose GoPro Hero 13 if you're an adventure traveler. You want waterproofing, ruggedness, and an ultra-wide field of view for action shots. You're going to the beach, skiing, snorkeling, or hiking. You don't need perfect low-light performance.

Choose Sony ZV-1 II if you're a vlogger or city traveler. You want superior image quality, shallow depth of field, and versatile zoom. You're filming night markets, street scenes, and talking-to-camera vlogs. You're not going underwater.

Our recommendation: If you can afford both, they complement each other perfectly – the GoPro for adventure, the Sony for everything else. If you have to choose one, ask yourself: "Will I spend more time underwater or at night markets?"

🔄 Other Options to Consider

  • DJI Osmo Pocket 4 Pro ($649): The best of both worlds – gimbal stabilization, 1-inch sensor, and pocket-sized. A great alternative to both.
  • DJI Osmo Action 5 ($349): Cheaper than GoPro, waterproof to 18m, and excellent value for adventure travelers.
  • Sony ZV-E10 II ($899): If you want interchangeable lenses, this is the upgrade from the ZV-1 II.
  • iPhone 16 Pro ($999): The camera you already own – with Action Mode for stabilization and excellent video quality.
