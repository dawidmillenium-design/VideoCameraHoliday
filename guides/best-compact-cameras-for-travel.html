I used to carry a full-frame DSLR on every trip. It was heavy, bulky, and I often left it in the hotel room because I didn't want to carry it all day. I missed shots because my camera was too big to have with me.

That's when I discovered compact cameras. They changed the way I travel. Suddenly, I could carry a camera in my jacket pocket or even my jeans. I was shooting more, enjoying my trips more, and getting better photos because I had my camera with me all the time.

After 6 years of travel and testing over 50 cameras, I've found the best compact cameras for travel in 2026. We tested them in Kyoto's temples, Goa's beaches, Lyon's night markets, and the alpine landscapes of Salzburg. Every recommendation on this list is based on real-world use — not spec sheets.

Skip to the verdict? The Fujifilm X100VI is our hero pick – it's the best overall travel compact for stills. But the Ricoh GR IV is unbeatable if you need to slip a camera into a jeans pocket, and the Panasonic ZS99 is the only pocket camera with 720mm reach.

🧪 How We Tested These Cameras

Locations: Kyoto (Japan) – temples and gardens; Goa (India) – beaches and markets; Lyon (France) – night markets and architecture; Salzburg (Austria) – alpine landscapes.

Conditions: Bright sunshine, low‑light evenings, humid markets, and sudden rain. We shot in every condition a traveller might face.

Duration: Each camera was used as the primary stills camera for at least 2 weeks of travel.

What we looked for: Image quality (JPEG and RAW), lens sharpness, autofocus reliability, battery life, handling, and overall portability. We also judged the intangible "joy of shooting" — does this camera make you want to take photos?

🏆 Our Top Compact Camera Picks for 2026

🏆 Best Overall

Fujifilm X100VI

$1,799
  • 40.2MP APS‑C X‑Trans CMOS 5 HR
  • 23mm f/2 (35mm‑e) fixed lens
  • 5‑axis IBIS (6 stops)
  • Film Simulations (Velvia, Classic Chrome, etc.)
  • 6.2K video as a bonus

Consensus "best compact" from Cotswold, AP, CNET, Wirecutter, and Away Lands.

📸 Real-World Field Notes

Shooting the X100VI in Kyoto was a revelation. The film simulations (Classic Chrome, Velvia) produced stunning JPEGs straight out of camera — I saved hours of editing. The 40MP sensor captured incredible detail in temple carvings. The only challenge: the fixed 35mm lens meant I had to be creative with composition. It forced me to see the shot, not just zoom into it.

👖 Best Pocketable

Ricoh GR IV

$1,499
  • 26MP APS‑C BSI sensor
  • 28mm‑e f/2.8 lens
  • True jeans‑pocket size (~262g)
  • 5‑axis IBIS (6 EV)
  • 1080p/60 video (weak point – fine for stills)

DPReview, CNET, and Cotswold all agree: the best pocketable travel camera.

📸 Real-World Field Notes

The GR IV lived in my jeans pocket for two weeks in Lyon. It was so discreet that I could shoot street photography without anyone noticing. The snap focus mode at 2.5m meant I could shoot from the hip and nail focus. The 28mm lens is perfect for environmental street shots. The only downside: the video is basic — this is a stills camera first.

🔭 Best Travel Zoom

Panasonic Lumix ZS99 / TZ99

~$499 – $699
  • 20.3MP 1/2.3" sensor
  • 24‑720mm (30x) Leica zoom lens
  • Pocketable, tilt touchscreen
  • 4K/30 video, USB‑C charging
  • ~430 shots per charge

CNET's "best overall P&S" and AP's "best compact for travel". Fills the zoom hole.

At a wildlife reserve in Goa, the ZS99's 720mm zoom captured a kingfisher that was easily 100 metres away. The pocketable size meant I could carry it alongside my main camera. Image quality is excellent in daylight, but the small sensor struggles in low light. For daylight safari or sightseeing, it's unbeatable.

It's expensive, but it's the closest thing to a perfect all-round compact.

🤔 How to Choose Your Travel Compact Camera

  • 📸 Prioritise image quality and are upgrading from a phone? → Fujifilm X100VI (best overall) or Ricoh GR IV (if pocketability is key).
  • 🔭 Need zoom for wildlife, landmarks, or concerts? → Panasonic ZS99 gives you 30x optical zoom. If you want better quality and can stretch the budget, the Sony RX100 VII is a premium step up.
  • 🎥 Want a hybrid that shoots great video and stills? → Canon PowerShot V1 has built‑in ND and a cooling fan for long takes.
  • 💰 On a strict budget? → Look at the Canon G7 X III (when on sale) or the Kodak PixPro FZ55 for casual use.
  • 🆕 Want the latest enthusiast zoom? → Keep an eye on the Panasonic Lumix L10 – it's a 2026 launch that AP and DPReview are already talking about.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is the Fujifilm X100VI worth the price over the Ricoh GR IV?
A: Yes, if you value a hybrid viewfinder, film simulations, and a slightly more classic shooting experience. The GR IV wins on pocketability and discretion – choose based on your priorities.
Q: Which compact camera is best for vlogging?
A: The Canon PowerShot V1 is the best hybrid compact for video. If you want a dedicated vlogging camera, check our Best Vlogging Cameras guide.
Q: Can a compact camera replace my smartphone for travel photos?
A: For image quality, yes – dedicated compacts have larger sensors and better lenses. But phones are more convenient and have better computational photography. It depends on your priorities.
Q: What accessories should I buy with a compact camera?
A: Extra batteries, a fast memory card (V30 or UHS‑II), a protective case, and a wrist strap. For the X100VI, a lens hood is also recommended.
