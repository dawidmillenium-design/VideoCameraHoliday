📹 Holiday Video Camera

⚡ Quick Answer

Set your camera to 4K 24fps, shutter speed 1/50, and ISO Auto (max 3200). This 'baseline' works for 90% of travel scenarios from beaches to cities.

The "Set It and Forget It" Baseline

You are on holiday, not a film set. You need settings that work everywhere without constant tweaking.

Recommended Settings

SettingValueWhy?
Resolution4K UHDStandard for YouTube/TV. Allows cropping in post.
Frame Rate24fps (or 25fps EU)Cinematic look. Use 60fps only for slow-mo.
Shutter Speed1/50 (double frame rate)Natural motion blur. Prevents choppy video.
ISOAuto (Limit Max 3200)Prevents grain in dark scenes.
White BalanceDaylight or AutoKeep colors consistent.

When to Change Settings

  • Skiing/Action: Switch to 60fps or 120fps for smooth slow motion.
  • Night Markets: Lower shutter to 1/30 if too dark, raise ISO limit to 6400.
  • Time-lapse: Use dedicated Intervalometer mode.

FAQ

Why 24fps and not 60?

60fps looks like a soap opera (too smooth). 24fps looks like cinema.

What is the 180-degree rule?

Shutter speed should be double your frame rate (24fps → 1/50s). It creates natural blur.

Should I shoot Log?

Only if you know color grading. For holidays, use "Standard" or "Vivid" picture profiles.

NTSC or PAL?

NTSC (24/30/60fps) for Americas/Japan. PAL (25/50fps) for Europe/Australia.

Best format?

MP4 (H.264) is universal. H.265 is smaller files but harder to edit on old PCs.
