🏆 Best Under $500: GoPro Hero 11

At $299 (down from $399), the Hero 11 is the best value in action cameras. You get 5.3K video, HyperSmooth 5.0, and GoPro's legendary durability.

💰 Best Ultra-Budget: AKASO EK7000 Pro

$80 gets you a functional action camera with a waterproof case good to 40m. Perfect for kids, casual users, or as a backup you won't cry about losing.

🌐 Best 360° Under $500: Insta360 X3

The X3 at $349 is the cheapest way into 360° filming. You lose the X5's low-light performance and battery life, but the core reframing experience is identical.

💡 Money-Saving Tip: Buy last year's flagship when the new model launches. Hero 11 at $299 is 90% as good as Hero 13 at $399.
