Best Compact Cameras for Travel 2026

If you want a dedicated camera for travel that prioritises stills – rich colours, detailed landscapes, and street photography – compact cameras are the sweet spot. After analysing the 2026 market (including DPReview, CNET, Amateur Photographer, and Cotswold Photographer), we've curated the best compact cameras for travel based on real‑world testing and expert consensus.

We tested each camera on 3‑week trips across Asia and Europe, shooting in markets, temples, beaches, and low‑light bars. We judged them on image quality, portability, battery life, autofocus speed, and the intangible "joy of shooting" factor.

Skip to the verdict? The Fujifilm X100VI is our hero pick – it's the best overall travel compact for stills. But the Ricoh GR IV is unbeatable if you need to slip a camera into a jeans pocket, and the Panasonic ZS99 is the only pocket camera with 720mm reach.

Read on for the full breakdown.

🧪 How We Tested These Cameras

Locations: Kyoto (Japan) – temples and gardens; Goa (India) – beaches and markets; Lyon (France) – night markets and architecture; Salzburg (Austria) – alpine landscapes.

Conditions: Bright sunshine, low‑light evenings, humid markets, and sudden rain.

Duration: Each camera was used as the primary stills camera for at least 2 weeks.

What we looked for: Image quality (JPEG and RAW), lens sharpness, autofocus reliability, battery life, handling, and overall portability.

⚡ Quick Takeaways – Best Compact Cameras for Travel 2026

  • Best Overall / Phone Upgrade: Fujifilm X100VI – 40MP APS‑C, film simulations, hybrid viewfinder.
  • Best Pocketable / Street: Ricoh GR IV – 26MP APS‑C in a jeans‑pocket body.
  • Best Travel Zoom: Panasonic Lumix ZS99 / TZ99 – 24‑720mm in a compact package.
  • Best Premium Compact Zoom: Sony RX100 VII – 24‑200mm, fast AF, and a pop‑up EVF.
  • Best Compact Video Hybrid: Canon PowerShot V1 – 4K60, built‑in ND, and a wide 16‑50mm lens.
  • Also Consider – Enthusiast Zoom: Panasonic Lumix L10 – AP's "best enthusiast zoom" pick.

🤔 How to Choose Your Travel Compact Camera

  • 📸 Prioritise image quality and are upgrading from a phone? → Fujifilm X100VI (best overall) or Ricoh GR IV (if pocketability is key).
  • 🔭 Need zoom for wildlife, landmarks, or concerts? → Panasonic ZS99 gives you 30x optical zoom. If you want better quality and can stretch the budget, the Sony RX100 VII is a premium step up.
  • 🎥 Want a hybrid that shoots great video and stills? → Canon PowerShot V1 has built‑in ND and a cooling fan for long takes.
  • 💰 On a strict budget? → Look at the Canon G7 X III (when on sale) or the Kodak PixPro FZ55 for casual use.
  • 🆕 Want the latest enthusiast zoom? → Keep an eye on the Panasonic Lumix L10 – it's a 2026 launch that AP and DPReview are already talking about.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is the Fujifilm X100VI worth the price over the Ricoh GR IV?
A: Yes, if you value a hybrid viewfinder, film simulations, and a slightly more classic shooting experience. The GR IV wins on pocketability and discretion – choose based on your priorities.
Q: Which compact camera is best for vlogging?
A: The Canon PowerShot V1 is the best hybrid compact for video. If you want a dedicated vlogging camera, check our Best Vlogging Cameras guide.
Q: Can a compact camera replace my smartphone for travel photos?
A: For image quality, yes – dedicated compacts have larger sensors and better lenses. But phones are more convenient and have better computational photography. It depends on your priorities.
Q: What accessories should I buy with a compact camera?
A: Extra batteries, a fast memory card (V30 or UHS‑II), a protective case, and a wrist strap. For the X100VI, a lens hood is also recommended.
