📷 Best Compact Cameras for Travel 2026

The ultimate stills-first guide for travellers who want premium image quality without the bulk. Updated for 2026 with the latest launches.

Updated Sep 2026 🏆 Hero: X100VI 📸 Stills-First

If you want a dedicated camera for travel that prioritises stills – rich colours, detailed landscapes, and street photography – compact cameras are the sweet spot. After analysing the 2026 market (and the competition, including DPReview, CNET, Amateur Photographer, and Cotswold Photographer), we've curated the best compact cameras for travel based on real-world testing and expert consensus.

📌 Not sure if you need a compact? If you're looking for a video-first camera with a gimbal, check our Best Holiday Video Cameras 2026 guide instead. This guide is for stills photographers who occasionally shoot video.

  • Best Pocketable / Street: Ricoh GR IV – 26MP APS-C in a jeans-pocket body. Read Review →
  • Best Travel Zoom: Panasonic Lumix ZS99 / TZ99 – 24-720mm in a compact package. Read Review →
