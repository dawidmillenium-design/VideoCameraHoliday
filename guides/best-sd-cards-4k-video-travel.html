📊 Understanding SD Card Specs for 4K Video

Manufacturers plaster cards with confusing acronyms. Here is what actually matters for travel videography:

  • V60 vs V90 (Video Speed Class): This is the most critical metric. The 'V' number indicates the minimum sustained write speed. A V60 card writes at a minimum of 60MB/s (perfect for standard 4K). A V90 card writes at 90MB/s (required for high-bitrate 4K 120fps, All-Intra codecs, or 8K).
  • UHS-I vs UHS-II: UHS-II cards have a second row of pins on the back, offering much faster transfer speeds when offloading footage to your laptop. However, they are more expensive and require a UHS-II compatible card reader.
  • Capacity: For 4K video, 128GB is the sweet spot. 64GB fills up in about 45 minutes of 4K 60fps, while 256GB+ increases the risk of total data loss if the card fails.
