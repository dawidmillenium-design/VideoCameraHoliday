I've rented gear in 15 countries and bought over $20k of equipment. Here's how to decide what makes financial sense.

⚡ Quick Answer

Buy: Everyday carry cameras (DJI Pocket 3, GoPro), basic lenses, memory cards, bags. Rent: Specialized telephoto lenses, cinema cameras, drones for specific trips, expensive lighting kits. If you'll use it less than 10 days a year, rent it.

Key Takeaways

  • The 10-Day Rule: If you'll use the gear fewer than 10 days per year, renting is almost always cheaper.
  • Try Before You Buy: Renting lets you test expensive gear ($3k+) before committing to purchase.
  • Travel Light: Rent locally at your destination to avoid carrying heavy kits through airports.
  • Insurance Matters: Always opt for damage waiver protection when renting; one drop can cost thousands.

When to Rent: Pros & Cons

Renting shines for specialized, expensive, or bulky gear you only need occasionally.

✅ Pros of Renting

  • Access to Pro Gear: Use $10,000 cinema cameras or $3,000 telephoto lenses for a fraction of the cost.
  • No Maintenance: No sensor cleaning, firmware updates, or long-term storage worries.
  • Try Before Buying: Test a Sony A7S III for a weekend before dropping $3,500.
  • Travel Flexibility: Rent at your destination (e.g., pick up a drone in Iceland) to avoid airport hassles.
  • Latest Tech: Always use the newest model without constant upgrades.

❌ Cons of Renting

  • Availability Risk: Popular gear books out weeks in advance during peak seasons.
  • No Ownership Equity: Money spent is gone; no resale value later.
  • Learning Curve: Limited time to master complex menus or features.
  • Damage Liability: You're responsible for scratches, dents, or loss (unless waived).

When to Buy: Pros & Cons

Buying makes sense for daily drivers, frequently used items, and inexpensive accessories.

✅ Pros of Buying

  • Always Available: Grab your camera anytime inspiration strikes.
  • Mastery: Deep familiarity with menus, buttons, and quirks leads to better shots.
  • Resale Value: Quality glass and bodies retain 50-70% value after years.
  • Customization: Modify settings, add LUTs, and personalize your rig.
  • Long-Term Cost: Cheaper if used frequently (over 10-15 days/year).

❌ Cons of Buying

  • High Upfront Cost: $2,000-$5,000 hits hard immediately.
  • Depreciation: New models release yearly, dropping your gear's value.
  • Maintenance Burden: Cleaning, repairs, and insurance are on you.
  • Bulk: Carrying unused gear weighs you down on trips.

The Break-Even Calculator

Use this simple formula to decide:

Formula: (Rental Cost per Day × Days Needed) vs. (Purchase Price - Resale Value).
Example: A $3,000 lens rents for $150/day. Need it for 5 days? Rental = $750. Buying costs $3,000 but resells for $2,000 later (net $1,000). Rent saves $250.

Top Rental Services 2026

These trusted platforms offer global shipping and local pickup:

  • Lensrentals (US/Global): Huge inventory, excellent insurance options. Best for lenses and cameras.
  • ShareGrid (US/Europe): Peer-to-peer marketplace. Often cheaper, but verify user ratings.
  • Kayak (Europe): Strong presence in EU cities. Good for last-minute local pickups.
  • Fat Llama (UK/US): Rent anything from locals. Great for niche accessories.

Insurance & Damage Waivers

⚠️ Critical: Standard credit card travel insurance often excludes rented professional camera gear. Always purchase the rental company's damage waiver (~10-15% of rental cost) or verify your specialized equipment policy covers rentals.

Most services offer tiered protection: Basic (deductible ~$500), Premium (deductible ~$0), and Third-Party Liability. For expensive cinema rigs, the premium waiver is worth every penny.

FAQ

Can I rent a drone while traveling abroad?

Yes, but check local laws first. Many countries (e.g., Morocco, UAE) ban drones entirely. Renting locally avoids customs issues but doesn't bypass legal restrictions.

What happens if I damage rented gear?

You pay the repair cost or replacement fee minus your damage waiver deductible. Document condition with photos/video before and after use to dispute unfair claims.

Is it cheaper to rent locally or ship from home?

Renting locally at your destination saves baggage fees and hassle. However, verify availability and quality reviews beforehand. Shipping from home ensures familiarity but adds weight/risk.

Do rental companies clean sensors between uses?

Reputable companies like Lensrentals perform full maintenance including sensor cleaning. Always check images upon receipt and request a swap if spots appear.

Can I extend a rental if my trip runs long?

Usually yes, but notify them ASAP. Extensions are charged at the daily rate. Late returns without notice incur hefty penalties.
