Insta360 X4 vs Canon G7X Mark III – 2026 Camera Comparison

Two very different tools end up in the same travel bag. The Insta360 X4 is a 360-degree action camera built around the idea that you can point once and decide the framing later. The Canon G7X Mark III is a compact vlogging camera with a conventional lens and a familiar point-and-shoot feel. This comparison is based on real travel use — no lab charts. Below we break down what each camera is actually good at on the road, and which one deserves the space in your carry-on.

At a Glance

The Insta360 X4 carries a ★ 4.7/5.0 rating in our testing and is priced at 2199 PLN. Its headline advantage is the Invisible Selfie Stick — the mount disappears from the footage, giving you drone-like shots without a drone. Its main trade-offs are stitching lines where the two lenses meet and battery life that rarely feels generous on a full day of shooting.

The Canon G7X Mark III is the classic compact travel vlog camera: a single lens, a flip screen, and straightforward controls. It is the camera most people picture when they think "holiday video camera."

Real-World Use

On a beach holiday, the X4 shines. Mount it on the Invisible Selfie Stick, walk the shoreline, and you capture a shot that looks like a floating camera crew followed you. Because you reframe later, you never miss the moment by pointing the wrong way. The cost shows up in post: you have to sit down and cut the angles, and you have to watch for stitching lines where the lenses overlap, especially when objects pass close to the camera.

The G7X Mark III is the opposite discipline. You compose in the moment, you press record, and the clip is basically done. For city breaks where you want quick cutaways, street food close-ups, and talking-head pieces to camera, that directness saves hours. What you give up is the freedom to change your mind about framing after the fact.

Battery life is the recurring complaint on the X4 side. Plan your charging around your shooting day rather than assuming you can film from sunrise to dinner. Carry spares and top up whenever you stop for coffee.

Which Should You Buy?

  • Choose the Insta360 X4 if you want Invisible Selfie Stick shots, if you are happy to Reframe Later in editing, and if you shoot action, water, or solo travel where nobody is behind the camera.
  • Choose the Canon G7X Mark III if you want a straightforward holiday video camera, if you prefer to frame as you shoot, and if you would rather not spend evenings in an editing app.
  • Consider both if your trips mix action days with vlog days — the two cameras cover each other's weaknesses rather than competing directly.

Neither camera wins outright. The X4 wins on creative flexibility and loses on battery life and stitching artifacts. The G7X Mark III wins on simplicity and loses on perspective. Match the tool to how you actually edit, not to how you imagine you will.

FAQ

Is the Insta360 X4 better than the Canon G7X Mark III for travel?

It depends on your workflow. The X4 is better if you want Invisible Selfie Stick shots and are willing to Reframe Later. The G7X Mark III is better if you want to frame in the moment and skip heavy editing. Our testing gives the X4 a ★ 4.7/5.0 rating, with Stitching Lines and Battery Life listed as its main cons.

What are the main downsides of the Insta360 X4?

The two cons we flag are Stitching Lines and Battery Life. Stitching lines can appear where the lenses overlap, particularly with close subjects, and battery life means you should carry spares on a full shooting day.

How much does the Insta360 X4 cost?

Our reference price is 2199 PLN. Prices move, so check current listings before buying, and compare against the G7X Mark III in your region.
