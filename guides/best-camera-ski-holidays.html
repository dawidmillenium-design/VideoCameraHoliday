Cameras for Ski Holidays

Cold weather destroys batteries, fogs lenses, and makes touchscreens useless with gloves. These cameras are built for the slopes.

🏂 Best Action Camera: GoPro Hero 13

The Enduro battery maintains performance down to -10°C. HyperSmooth 6.0 handles the vibration of skiing. The mounting ecosystem is unmatched.

🎿 Best 360°: Insta360 X5

Mount it to your helmet and capture everything — your POV, the mountain behind you, and your friends skiing alongside. Reframe later for multiple angles.

📸 Best Photo/Video Hybrid: Sony A7C II

Weather-sealed and compact. The 33MP sensor captures stunning landscape shots between runs. Battery life is good even in cold weather.

❄️ Cold Weather Tips

  • Keep spare batteries in an inside pocket (body heat preserves charge)
  • Use anti-fog inserts in waterproof cases
  • Let cameras acclimate to indoor temperatures slowly (prevents condensation)
  • Touchscreen-compatible gloves are essential
⚠️ Battery Warning: Lithium batteries lose 50% capacity at -10°C. Carry 3x as many batteries as you think you need for ski trips.
