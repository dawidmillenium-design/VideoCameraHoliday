Waterproof Cameras for Beach Holidays

Beach holidays demand cameras that can handle sand, salt water, and sun. These picks are tested in real ocean conditions.

🏊 Best for Snorkeling: GoPro Hero 13

GoPro's underwater color processing is unmatched. The Hero 13 automatically white-balances underwater, producing vibrant coral footage without editing.

🤿 Best for Diving: DJI Osmo Action 5 Pro

20m waterproof without a case means recreational scuba diving without extra gear. The front screen is also useful for checking framing underwater.

🌊 Best 360° Underwater: Insta360 X5

15m waterproof and 360° capture creates unique spherical underwater footage. The reframing lets you choose angles after the dive.

🏄 Best for Surfing: GoPro Hero 13 + Bite Mount

The HyperSmooth 6.0 stabilization handles the chaos of waves. The bite mount keeps your hands free for paddling.
