⚡ Quick Takeaways – 3 Cameras Compared

  • Sony ZV-1 II: Best for vlogging and talking-head content. 1" sensor, S‑Log3, and Product Showcase autofocus.
  • GoPro Hero 13: Best for action, beaches, and adventure. Waterproof, rugged, and HyperSmooth 6.0.
  • DJI Pocket 3: Best for smooth walking shots and compact size. Built‑in gimbal and 1‑inch sensor.
