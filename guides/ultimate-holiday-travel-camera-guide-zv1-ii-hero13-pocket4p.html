📹 Ultimate Holiday Travel Camera Guide 2026

ZV-1 II, Hero 13 Black, or Pocket 4P? Compare video quality, stabilization, audio, and field performance – tested on real holidays.

Updated Sep 2026 🎥 3-Way Comparison

Choosing a travel camera can be overwhelming – especially when the best options come from completely different categories. We've tested the Sony ZV-1 II, GoPro Hero 13 Black, and DJI Pocket 3 (the "Pocket 4P" in the title) across multiple holidays to help you decide which one fits your style.

⚡ Quick Takeaways – 3 Cameras Compared

  • Sony ZV-1 II: Best for vlogging and talking-head content. 1" sensor, S‑Log3, and Product Showcase autofocus.
  • GoPro Hero 13: Best for action, beaches, and adventure. Waterproof, rugged, and HyperSmooth 6.0.
  • DJI Pocket 3: Best for smooth walking shots and compact size. Built‑in gimbal and 1‑inch sensor.
