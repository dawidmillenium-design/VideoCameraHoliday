FAQ

Are cheap 4K cameras real?

They record 4K, but the bitrate is low and they lack stabilization. 1080p often looks better on these models.

Is a used GoPro safe?

Yes, check the lens for scratches and battery health. They are built like tanks.

What about smartphones?

An old iPhone XR or Pixel 4 is better than any $200 camera. Use what you have first.

Do I need SD cards?

Yes, buy a reputable brand (SanDisk/Samsung). Cheap cards corrupt 4K video.

Best accessory for budget cams?

A chest mount. It hides camera shake better than handheld shots.
