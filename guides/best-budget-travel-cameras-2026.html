Best Budget Travel Cameras Under $500 (2026)

You don't need to spend $1,500+ to get a great travel camera. We've tested dozens of cameras on real holidays — these are the best sub-$500 options that actually deliver.

🔭 Best Zoom Panasonic ZS99 $499 🏄 Best Action GoPro Hero 13 $349 🏖️ Best Waterproof Fujifilm XP130 ~$150 📸 Cheapest Kodak PixPro FZ55 ~$130 ⭐ Best Value 1" Canon G7 X III ~$699*

⚡ Quick Takeaways — Budget Travel Cameras 2026

  • Best Budget Waterproof: Fujifilm FinePix XP130 — under $200, 15m waterproof, shockproof, and perfect for the beach.
  • Best Budget Compact: Kodak PixPro FZ55 — ~$130, simple and fun for family travel where quality isn't critical.
  • Best Budget Travel Zoom: Panasonic Lumix ZS99 — $499, 24-720mm (30x) reach in a pocket.
  • Best Budget Action: GoPro Hero 13 Black — now $349 (down from $399), rugged, waterproof to 10m, and great for adventure.
  • Best Value 1" Sensor: Canon G7 X III — $699 standard, but watch for holiday sales under $500.

🤔 How to Choose a Budget Travel Camera

🏖️ Beach & Water

You need a waterproof camera that can survive sand, salt, and splashes. Both the Fujifilm XP130 and Panasonic TS30 are built for this — rugged, cheap, and reliable.

→ Fujifilm XP130 or Panasonic TS30

🔭 Wildlife & Landmarks

If you're shooting distant subjects — animals, concerts, architecture — you need reach. The Panasonic ZS99's 30x zoom is the only sub-$500 camera that delivers this range.

→ Panasonic Lumix ZS99

🏄 Adventure & Sports

For action footage — skiing, surfing, biking — you need ruggedness and stabilization. The GoPro Hero 13 is the gold standard, and it's now more affordable than ever.

→ GoPro Hero 13 Black

📸 Everyday Family

For casual snapshots and family memories where quality isn't critical, the Kodak FZ55 is simple, light, and cheap. Good enough for the fridge door.

→ Kodak PixPro FZ55

⭐ Best Value: Canon G7 X III (When on Sale)

Canon PowerShot G7 X III

If you can find it on sale under $500, this is the best value compact camera on the market. The 1" sensor, f/1.8-2.8 lens, and 4K video punch way above its price point. It's a seven-year-old camera that's still selling out in 2026 — and for good reason. The image quality rivals cameras twice the price, and the tilting screen makes it a vlogging favourite.

Watch for Black Friday and holiday sales — it regularly drops under $500 during major shopping events.
