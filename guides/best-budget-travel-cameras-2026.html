You don't need to spend $1,500+ to get a great travel camera. We've tested dozens of cameras on real holidays, and plenty of sub-$500 options deliver fantastic results for the price. This hub aggregates every budget pick from across our guides – waterproof, compact, zoom, and action cameras – so you can find the right one for your trip and your wallet.

⚡ Quick Takeaways – Budget Travel Cameras 2026

  • Best Budget Waterproof: Fujifilm XP130 / Panasonic TS30 – under $200, rugged, and perfect for the beach.
  • Best Budget Compact: Kodak PixPro FZ55 – ~$130, simple and fun for family travel.
  • Best Budget Travel Zoom: Panasonic Lumix ZS99 / TZ99 – $499, 24-720mm reach in a pocket.
  • Best Budget Action: GoPro Hero 13 – $499, rugged, waterproof, and great for adventure.
  • Best Value 1" Sensor: Canon G7 X III – ~$699 (watch for sales under $500).

🤔 How to Choose a Budget Travel Camera

🏖️ Beach & Water

Choose Fujifilm XP130 or Panasonic TS30 – rugged, cheap, and waterproof. Perfect for sand and splashes.

🔭 Wildlife & Landmarks

Get the Panasonic ZS99 – 30x zoom in a pocket. No other sub-$500 camera can reach 720mm.

🏄 Adventure & Sports

Go for the GoPro Hero 13 – rugged, waterproof, and stabilised. Built for action.

📸 Everyday Family

Choose the Kodak FZ55 – cheap, simple, and fun. Good enough for memories.

⭐ Best Value: Canon G7 X III

Canon PowerShot G7 X III – If you can find it on sale under $500, this is the best value compact camera on the market. The 1" sensor, f/1.8-2.8 lens, and 4K video punch way above its price point. Watch for Black Friday and holiday sales.
