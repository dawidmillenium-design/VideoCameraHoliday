Cameras for Hiking Holidays

Every gram matters when you're carrying a pack up a mountain. These cameras deliver maximum quality per ounce.

🏔️ Best Lightweight: DJI Osmo Pocket 3

179g with a 3-axis gimbal and 1-inch sensor. The best footage-to-weight ratio of any camera.

🥾 Best Action: GoPro Hero 13

154g, hyper-durable, and mounts anywhere. Chest mounts capture immersive POV hiking footage.

📷 Best Photo Quality: Sony A7C II + 35mm f/1.8

The full-frame sensor captures landscape detail that smaller sensors miss. The 35mm prime is light and sharp.
