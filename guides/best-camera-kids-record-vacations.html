FAQ

At what age should kids get a camera?

Around 6-7 years old, they start understanding framing and storytelling.

How to protect the lens?

Tempered glass screen protectors work on GoPros too. Buy a "buddy lens" cap.

Should I let them use my phone?

Only if you trust them. Sand in the charging port is a common tragedy.

Best educational value?

Giving them their own camera encourages creativity and observation skills.

Where to store footage?

Get a cheap card reader and let them transfer files to a laptop themselves.
