🏆 Best Compact Camera for Video: Canon PowerShot V1

NEW 2026 PICK

Canon PowerShot V1

Amateur Photographer just named this the "Best Compact Camera for Video," and after testing it, we agree. Canon has finally cracked the code on built-in audio. The V1 features a new multi-capsule microphone array that completely eliminates handling noise, a known issue on older compacts. It also boasts a slightly larger 1.2-inch sensor than the ZV-1 II, giving you cleaner footage in dimly lit restaurants or evening street markets.

  • Pros: Class-leading built-in audio, 1.2-inch sensor, flip-out touchscreen.
  • Cons: No mechanical gimbal (relies on electronic stabilization).
  • Best For: Food vloggers, static talking-head setups, and low-light city breaks.
