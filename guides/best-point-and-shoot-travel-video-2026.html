Best Point and Shoot Camera for Travel Video 2026

By Dawid Millennium | Updated September 4, 2026

⚡ Quick Answer

The Sony ZV-1 II is the undisputed king of travel point-and-shoots with its 1-inch sensor and built-in ND filter. The Canon PowerShot V10 is a close second for vloggers needing a vertical grip.

Why Point and Shoot Still Matters

In an era of smartphones, a dedicated compact offers optical zoom, larger sensors for low light, and better audio inputs—all while fitting in a jeans pocket.

FAQ

Is a point and shoot better than a phone?

Yes, for video quality. The larger sensor creates natural background blur and handles low light better.

Do I need an ND filter?

Essential for sunny beaches. The ZV-1 II has one built-in; others require screw-on filters.

Best for vlogging?

Sony ZV-1 II or Canon V10 due to flip screens and superior microphones.

Can I change lenses?

No, these are fixed-lens compacts. That's the trade-off for pocket size.

Waterproof?

None of these are waterproof. Use a dry bag or housing for water shots.
