Ricoh GR IV Travel Review (2026)

Verdict: The Ricoh GR IV is the best truly pocketable APS‑C camera on the market. It slips into a jeans pocket and delivers exceptional image quality. The 26MP sensor and IBIS make it a street photographer's dream. Just don't expect 4K video or a zoom lens.

📋 Key Specifications

Ricoh GR IV – Specs
Sensor26MP APS‑C BSI CMOS
Lens28mm f/2.8 (fixed)
IBIS5‑axis, up to 6 stops
ViewfinderNone (optional add‑on)
Video1080p/60 (no 4K)
Weight~262g
Price$1,499 MSRP

✅ Pros

  • Truly pocketable (262g)
  • Excellent 26MP APS‑C image quality
  • 5‑axis IBIS
  • Snap focus mode is brilliant
  • Premium build quality

❌ Cons

  • No 4K video
  • No viewfinder
  • No weather‑sealing
  • Contrast‑detect AF
  • Expensive ($1,499)
9.3 / 10

Recommended for Street & Travel Photographers

The Ricoh GR IV is the ultimate travel companion for photographers who prioritise pocketability and image quality. A true street photographer's dream.
