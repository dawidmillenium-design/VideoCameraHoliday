DJI Osmo Action 6 Review: Tested in the Maldives

From the streets of Bangkok to the deep reefs of Maafushi, does DJI's latest action cam hold up against shark encounters and harsh sunlight?

Watch Full Video Showcase →

I picked up the DJI Osmo Action 6 right in the heart of Bangkok, buzzing with excitement just hours before catching my flight to the Maldives. There's something thrilling about testing brand-new gear in one of the world's most demanding environments immediately after unboxing it.

This wasn't a lab test. This was a baptism by fire (and saltwater). Over the next week, the camera faced blinding equatorial sun on pristine white sands and the crushing pressure of a deep dive course off the coast of Maafushi. Here is how it performed.

Fresh out of the box in Bangkok: The anticipation before the Maldives trip.

Surface Testing: Beach Showdown

The first real test happened on the powdery white sands of Maafushi. The sunlight here is unforgiving, creating extreme dynamic range challenges that blow out skies or crush shadows. I ran a side-by-side comparison against the Insta360 X5 to see which sensor handled the contrast better.

Stabilization: The Action 6's new HorizonSteady algorithm is incredibly smooth. While walking along the shoreline, the footage felt locked to a rail. The Insta360 X5 is great, but the reframing process sometimes introduces slight digital jitter that the Action 6 avoids natively.

Color Science: DJI has tweaked their color profile. The blues of the Indian Ocean popped without looking neon, and the sand retained texture rather than turning into a white blob. The Insta360 leaned slightly cooler, which looked nice but required more grading to match reality.

DJI Osmo Action 6: 2026 First Look

The Beach Showdown: DJI Action 6 vs Insta360 X5 in harsh sunlight.

Deep Dive: Sharks & Reefs of Maafushi

The true test of any "action" camera is what happens when you submerge it. I took the Action 6 on a certified deep dive course. At depth, colors usually vanish, turning everything into a monochromatic blue mess. However, the Action 6's native color profile retained surprising amounts of red and orange on the reef fish.

The highlight (or perhaps the terrifying peak) of the dive was a close encounter with a Tiger Shark. As the predator glided within inches of the lens, the autofocus locked instantly, and the stabilization kept the shot usable despite my elevated heart rate and sudden movements. No housing fogged, no leaks, just pure underwater clarity.

DJI Osmo Action 6: Maldives Deep Dive

Footage from the deep dive: Tiger Shark encounter captured on DJI Osmo Action 6.

Final Verdict

The DJI Osmo Action 6 proves itself as a top-tier travel companion. It bridges the gap between rugged durability and cinematic quality. While the Insta360 X5 offers unique 360 flexibility, the Action 6 wins for point-and-shoot reliability in extreme conditions.

👍 The Good

  • Exceptional dynamic range in harsh sunlight
  • Flawless underwater performance without housing
  • Superior horizon leveling stabilization
  • Accurate color science straight out of camera
  • Robust build quality survived sand and salt

👎 The Bad

  • Battery life drains faster in 4K/60fps mode
  • No 360-degree capture capability
  • Slightly bulkier than the GoPro Hero 13
  • App connectivity can be finicky in bright sun
