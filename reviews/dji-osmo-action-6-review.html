DJI Osmo Action 6 Review: Tested in the Maldives

From the streets of Bangkok to the deep reefs of Maafushi, does DJI's latest action cam hold up against shark encounters and harsh sunlight?

📌 In Short

  • Best for: Scuba divers, beach travellers, and adventure seekers who need rugged reliability and stunning underwater colour.
  • Pros: Exceptional stabilisation, waterproof to 18m out of the box, rich out‑of‑camera colours.
  • Cons: Battery drains quickly in 4K/120fps, no 360° capture, bulkier than GoPro Hero 13.
  • Verdict: A top‑tier action cam that shines in extreme environments – buy it if you prioritise durability and image quality over 360 flexibility.

I picked up the DJI Osmo Action 6 right in the heart of Bangkok, buzzing with excitement just hours before catching my flight to the Maldives. There's something thrilling about testing brand-new gear in one of the world's most demanding environments immediately after unboxing it.

This wasn't a lab test. This was a baptism by fire (and saltwater). Over the next week, the camera faced blinding equatorial sun on pristine white sands and the crushing pressure of a deep dive course off the coast of Maafushi. The DJI Osmo Action 6 promised serious upgrades for travel filmmakers, and here is how it truly performed in the field.

Fresh out of the box in Bangkok: The anticipation before the Maldives trip.

Surface Testing: Beach Showdown

The first real test for the DJI Osmo Action 6 happened on the powdery white sands of Maafushi. When you film in harsh sunlight, the environment is unforgiving, creating extreme dynamic range challenges that easily blow out skies or completely crush shadow details. To benchmark its capabilities, I ran a side‑by‑side comparison against the Insta360 X5 to see which sensor handled the contrast better.

Stabilization: The Action 6's new HorizonSteady algorithm is incredibly smooth. While walking along the shoreline, the footage felt locked to a rail. The Insta360 X5 is undeniably great, but the reframing process sometimes introduces slight digital jitter that the DJI Osmo Action 6 avoids natively.

Color Science: DJI has notably tweaked their colour profile for this generation. The blues of the Indian Ocean popped beautifully without looking neon, and the white sand retained its texture rather than turning into a flat, overexposed blob. The Insta360 leaned slightly cooler, which looked nice but required a bit more colour grading in post to match reality.

The Beach Showdown: DJI Action 6 vs Insta360 X5 in harsh sunlight.

Deep Dive: Sharks & Reefs of Maafushi

The true test of any "action" camera is what happens when you submerge it, which is why it often tops the list of waterproof cameras for the beach. I took the Action 6 on a certified deep dive course. At depth, colours usually vanish, turning everything into a monochromatic blue mess. However, the Action 6's native colour profile retained surprising amounts of red and orange on the reef fish.

The highlight (or perhaps the terrifying peak) of the dive was a close encounter with a Tiger Shark. As the predator glided within inches of the lens, the autofocus locked instantly, and the stabilisation kept the shot highly usable despite my elevated heart rate and sudden movements. No housing fogged, no leaks, just pure underwater clarity. For a broader look at gear for diving, check out our comprehensive underwater camera guide.

Who Should Buy It (and Who Shouldn't)

The DJI Osmo Action 6 is undeniably powerful, but it isn't for everyone. If you're deciding between an action camera vs gimbal camera, your choice comes down to how rugged your travels are.

Buy it if: You are a scuba diver, a beachgoer, or an adventure traveller who needs something that can be tossed into a backpack, dropped in the sand, and plunged into the ocean without a second thought. Its low‑light performance also makes it a great choice for evening city walks.

Skip it if: Your priority is cinematic, shallow depth‑of‑field vlogging (look at a mirrorless or gimbal camera instead), or if you strictly want to "shoot now, frame later" in full 360 degrees.

Final Verdict

The DJI Osmo Action 6 proves itself as a top‑tier travel companion. It brilliantly bridges the gap between rugged durability and cinematic quality. While the Insta360 X5 offers unique 360 flexibility, the DJI Osmo Action 6 wins out for raw point‑and‑shoot reliability in extreme conditions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the DJI Osmo Action 6 waterproof without a housing?

Yes, it is waterproof down to 18 meters (59 feet) natively without requiring any additional waterproof housing, making it excellent for snorkeling and scuba diving.

How does it compare to the Insta360 X5?

The DJI Osmo Action 6 excels in traditional point‑and‑shoot action with superior low‑light performance and ruggedness, while the Insta360 X5 is ideal if you need 360‑degree reframing flexibility.

Is it good for scuba diving?

Absolutely. Thanks to its 18m waterproof rating and an advanced custom color science profile that retains reds and oranges at depth, the DJI Osmo Action 6 is one of the best out‑of‑the‑box cameras for scuba diving.

How is battery life in 4K?

The battery performs well overall but drains significantly faster when recording continuously in 4K at 60fps or 120fps. Expect around 70‑80 minutes of actual record time at high frame rates, though it can reach 160 minutes at 1080p.
