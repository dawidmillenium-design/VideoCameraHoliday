📷 Fujifilm X100VI Travel Review (2026)

Is the hype real? We tested the X100VI on trips to Kyoto, Goa, and Lyon to see if it's worth the $1,799 price tag — and whether it beats your iPhone.

📅 Tested: Sep 2026 📸 Trip Count: 3 ⭐ Rating: 9.5 / 10

Verdict: The Fujifilm X100VI is the best fixed-lens compact camera for travel photography in 2026. The 40MP sensor, film simulations, and hybrid viewfinder make it a joy to shoot. It's expensive and lacks zoom, but for the traveller who prioritises image quality and experience, it's unmatched.

🔍 First Impressions: The X100VI Hype is Real

Fujifilm's X100 series has a cult following, and the VI is the most refined version yet. With a 40.2MP APS-C sensor, 5-axis IBIS, and the iconic 23mm f/2 (35mm-e) lens, it's designed for one thing: making you want to take photos. I carried it alongside an iPhone 16 Pro on three trips — here's how it held up.

✈️ Travel Performance – Real-World Testing

🇯🇵 Kyoto – Street Photography & Golden Hour

In the narrow alleys of Gion, the X100VI's hybrid viewfinder was a lifesaver. The OVF gives you a window into the scene without lag, while the EVF confirms exposure. The 23mm lens is perfect for environmental portraits and architectural details. The film simulations (Velvia, Classic Chrome, Pro Neg) delivered beautiful JPEGs straight out of camera — a huge time-saver on the road.

🇮🇳 Goa – Beach & High Contrast

The bright Goan sun is a stress test for any camera. The X100VI handled it with ease. The IBIS was noticeable when shooting at dusk on Palolem Beach, allowing for sharp handheld shots at 1/15s. The dynamic range is excellent — highlights didn't clip, and shadows retained detail. The fixed 35mm lens meant I had to "zoom with my feet", which made me more deliberate with composition.

🇫🇷 Lyon – Low Light & Night Markets

At the Marché de la Croix-Rousse, the f/2 lens and IBIS shone. I could shoot at ISO 3200 with minimal noise, capturing the warmth of the market lights. The autofocus (improved over the X100V) locked onto subjects quickly in low light, though it's not as snappy as a Sony A7 IV — it's fast enough for travel.

📱 Fujifilm X100VI vs iPhone 16 Pro – The "Phone Upgrade" Question

This is the $1,799 question. With computational photography improving every year, can you justify a dedicated camera? Here's my take after comparing them side-by-side:

  • Image Quality: X100VI wins, especially in low light. The larger sensor captures natural depth of field and far more detail. Apple's processing is aggressive; Fujifilm's is natural.
  • Focal Length: iPhone gives you 13, 24, 48, and 120mm equivalents. X100VI gives you just 35mm. If you need zoom, the X100VI is not for you (check the Panasonic ZS99 instead).
  • Experience: X100VI wins hands down. The dials, the viewfinder, the shutter sound — it makes photography an experience, not just a snap.
  • Price: iPhone is already in your pocket. X100VI is a significant additional investment.

📌 The Verdict on vs iPhone: If you want the best possible travel photos and enjoy the creative process, the X100VI is a clear upgrade. If you're happy with phone photos and value convenience over quality, save your money.

Recommended for Travel Photographers

The Fujifilm X100VI is a masterpiece of design and image quality. If you're serious about travel photography and want a camera that inspires you to shoot, this is the one. Just be ready to work with a prime lens — and pay a premium.
