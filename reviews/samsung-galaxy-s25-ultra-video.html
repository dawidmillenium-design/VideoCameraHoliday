The Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra is Samsung's camera flagship. With 8K video, a 200MP main sensor, and 10x optical zoom, it's a versatile tool for travel content creation.

🎬 8K30 Video

The 8K30 footage is incredibly detailed, though file sizes are enormous. More practically, the 4K120 mode is excellent for slow-motion action shots.

🔭 10x Optical Zoom

The 10x optical zoom is unmatched by any phone. For travel, this means capturing distant landmarks, wildlife, and street details without moving. The iPhone 16 Pro maxes out at 5x.

🖊️ S Pen for Editing

The S Pen makes precise editing on the phone actually possible. Trimming clips, drawing masks, and color grading with a stylus is significantly more accurate than finger-based editing.

❌ vs iPhone 16 Pro

  • Video color science is less natural than iPhone
  • Stabilization is good but not quite gimbal-like
  • 8K recording overheats after 5 minutes
  • More expensive than iPhone Pro Max
💡 Verdict: Choose S25 Ultra for zoom versatility and Android flexibility. Choose iPhone 16 Pro for color science and ecosystem.
