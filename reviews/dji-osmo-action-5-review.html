The DJI Osmo Action 5 Pro is the battery-life monster of action cameras, and after two weeks in Vietnam it became the action cam we recommend to most travelers. It outlasts the GoPro Hero 13 by hours, beats it in low light, and its subject tracking makes solo filming effortless. The only things it loses are GoPro's mount ecosystem and lens mods.

We hauled it through Hanoi street food tours, Ha Long Bay kayak trips and Hoi An's lantern-lit nights. If your ideal holiday camera is one you charge every second night and never babysit, read on.

Key Takeaways

  • Battery genuinely lasts a full sightseeing day — up to 4 hours claimed, and real-world use backs it up.
  • Large 1/1.3-inch sensor gives the best low-light footage of any action camera we tested.
  • Subject tracking keeps you centered without a cameraman.
  • Dual touchscreens make selfie filming and framing effortless.
  • Trail the GoPro Hero 13 only in mount variety and lens accessories.

Quick Verdict

For 80% of travelers, this is the action camera to buy in 2026. The Action 5 Pro removes the two biggest action-cam annoyances — dead batteries by lunch and mushy night footage — while matching the GoPro on stabilization and beating it on selfie convenience with a proper front touchscreen. On a humid Hanoi evening, filming sizzling street food stalls in near-darkness, it produced clips we proudly used; the GoPro footage from the same stall needed apologies.

Score: 9/10 for travel. It sits near the very top of our best holiday video cameras 2026 list, and it is the action cam we now pack by default.

Design & Handling for Travel

The Action 5 Pro is compact, rugged and waterproof to 20 metres — no case needed for any snorkeling or waterfall swim we tried in Vietnam. The magnetic quick-release mount is faster than GoPro's system: the camera clicks on and off mounts with one hand, which matters when a kayak is drifting.

The star of the design is the dual touchscreen setup. The front screen means you can actually frame yourself for talking clips instead of guessing, and both screens are bright enough for midday sun. The built-in 47GB of internal storage saved us once when a microSD card filled unexpectedly mid-temple-visit — a small feature that feels enormous when it rescues a shot.

Travel tip: Use the pressure/depth gauge overlay when snorkeling — your underwater clips get automatic depth data, which is a fun touch for dive travel vlogs. For more underwater advice see our underwater camera guide.

Video Quality in Real Holiday Conditions

Daylight

4K footage in daylight is excellent — natural colors that need less grading than GoPro's punchier output, sharp detail, and a 155° field of view that captures the chaos of a Hanoi intersection in all its glory. 4K120 slow motion turned a Ha Long Bay kayak splash into a poster-worthy shot. We shot mostly in the standard color profile and graded lightly; D-Log M is there if you want maximum flexibility.

Low Light

This is the Action 5 Pro's party piece. Hoi An's lantern festival at night is a torture test for small sensors, and the DJI's 1/1.3-inch sensor with SuperNight mode produced footage that looked almost cinematic — rich colors, controlled noise, glowing lanterns that did not blow out. No other action camera we have tested comes close; the GoPro Hero 13 visibly struggles in the same scenes.

Stabilization

RockSteady stabilization is excellent — walking tours look gimbal-smooth, and horizon-leveling keeps kayak footage flat even when the boat was not. It is neck-and-neck with GoPro's HyperSmooth; we genuinely could not pick a winner in blind tests. The subject tracking feature is the differentiator: it crops and follows you in frame, so solo walking-and-talking clips stay perfectly composed without touching the camera.

Watch out: 4K120 slow-mo drains the battery roughly twice as fast and heats the camera in tropical sun. Use it for short bursts of action, not as your default mode.

Audio

Built-in audio is strong for an action camera — the mic array handled Hanoi's street noise well, and wind reduction kept boat commentary clear. The killer feature is direct connection to DJI's wireless mics (like the Mic 2): pairing takes seconds, the audio quality jumps to proper-vlogger level, and you can wear the mic while the camera is mounted meters away on a kayak bow. For travel vloggers, this ecosystem is a genuine reason to choose DJI.

Battery Life on a Full Sightseeing Day

This is why you buy the Action 5 Pro. On our heaviest day — temple mornings, street food lunch filming, a sunset river cruise, night market walk, roughly two hours of total 4K recording — the battery still had around 30% left at 10pm. The GoPro Hero 13 needed a battery swap by mid-afternoon on the same itinerary. DJI claims up to 4 hours of lighter use; even discounting that, we never once carried a spare battery after day three. Combined with fast USB-C charging (a coffee-stop top-up adds hours), battery anxiety simply disappears.

Who Should Buy / Who Should Skip

Buy the Action 5 Pro if: you want one tough camera for everything — beaches, cities, nights, boats — without carrying spares; you film yourself frequently; you value battery life above all; or you are building around DJI's mic ecosystem.

Skip it if: you are deep in the GoPro mount ecosystem, you want lens mods or 5.3K resolution (get the Hero 13), or you want to reframe shots after the fact — that is the Insta360 X4's 360° superpower. And if your filming is 90% walking vlogs, the DJI Osmo Pocket 3 is the more cinematic choice from the same family. Shopping on a budget? The Action 5 Pro now dips under ~$370 in sales — it earns a spot in our best holiday cameras under $500 guide whenever it does.

FAQ

Is the DJI Osmo Action 5 Pro better than the GoPro Hero 13 for travel?

For most travelers, yes. The Action 5 Pro wins on battery life, low-light image quality and dual touchscreens, while the GoPro wins on mount ecosystem, lens mods and burst slow-mo. If you film yourself a lot or hate carrying spare batteries, the DJI is the better holiday companion.

How long does the DJI Osmo Action 5 Pro battery really last?

DJI claims up to 4 hours, and in real holiday use — mixed 4K30/4K60 clips across a sightseeing day — we consistently finished with charge to spare, something no GoPro we have tested manages. Heavy 4K120 slow-mo and cold weather reduce this, but it remains the battery champion of action cameras.

Is the DJI Osmo Action 5 Pro good in low light?

It is the best action camera we have tested after dark. The 1/1.3-inch sensor and SuperNight mode produce clean, colorful footage of evening streets and night markets where rivals turn noisy. Dedicated low-light cameras like the DJI Pocket 3 are better still, but among action cams this is the night king.

Does the Action 5 Pro work with GoPro mounts?

The Action 5 Pro uses DJI's magnetic quick-release system, but adapters to standard GoPro-style three-prong mounts are cheap and included in many bundles, so your existing mounts remain usable.
