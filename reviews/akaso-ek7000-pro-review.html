Akaso EK7000 Pro Review

The Best Budget Action Camera for Travel? Field-tested on a rugged South American backpacking trip.

🎬

Dawid Millennium — Travel Videographer

Tested on a rugged backpacking trip through South America. Focus on durability, value, and budget performance.

⚡ Quick Takeaways

  • The Good: Incredible value for money, comes with a massive box of mounts and accessories, and genuinely waterproof without a bulky case.
  • The Bad: Electronic image stabilization (EIS) struggles at 4K, audio quality is poor in windy conditions, and low-light performance is very limited.
  • The Verdict: The best budget action camera for travelers who want to capture adventures without the anxiety of destroying a $400 device.

Real-World Field Test: South American Backpacking

The Akaso EK7000 Pro isn't going to win any cinematic awards, but that's not its job. Its job is to survive. Strapped to the handlebars of a rental motorbike in Colombia, it captured usable, wide-angle POV footage of the journey. The included waterproof case handled sudden tropical downpours without a single fogging issue.

While the 4K 30fps footage is decent in bright daylight, the moment you move into the shade or try to use the EIS at higher resolutions, the image softens significantly. For the price, however, these compromises are entirely expected and forgivable.

Pros & Cons for Travelers

✅ Pros

  • Unbeatable price-to-performance ratio
  • Includes 2 batteries and tons of mounts
  • Simple, intuitive interface
  • Great for high-risk mounting (drones, bikes)

❌ Cons

  • Weak low-light and nighttime performance
  • EIS causes noticeable cropping and softening
  • Built-in microphone picks up heavy wind noise

Final Verdict

If you are on a strict budget or need a "beater" camera for high-risk activities like kayaking, mountain biking, or attaching to a backpack, the Akaso EK7000 Pro is a fantastic choice. It delivers 80% of the GoPro experience for 20% of the price. For more budget options, check our Best Budget Holiday Cameras Under $500 guide.
