Holiday Video Camera Rating

Incredible value at $80. Not a GoPro killer, but perfect for first-time action camera buyers and kids.

The AKASO EK7000 Pro costs $80. That's 1/5 the price of a GoPro Hero 13. The question is: can it deliver 1/5 the quality, or is it a better value than the math suggests?

Key Specifications

Video4K30 (interpolated), 1080p60
Sensor20MP (interpolated)
Screen2" touchscreen
Waterproof40m (with case)
Battery~90 minutes
Price$79.99

🎬 Real Video Quality

The 4K30 is interpolated from a lower resolution sensor. Real resolution is closer to 2.7K. In good light, footage is acceptable for social media. In low light, it's noisy and soft.

🏊 Waterproof Case

The included waterproof case works to 40m — deeper than GoPro's native 10m. For snorkeling and casual diving, this is actually better than GoPro without a dive housing.

⚠️ Important: The "4K" and "20MP" specs are marketing numbers. Real quality is closer to 2.7K/12MP. Set expectations accordingly.

✅ Buy if:

  • You're on a tight budget
  • It's for kids or casual use
  • You need a backup/beater camera
  • You want to try action cameras before investing

❌ Skip if:

  • You need professional quality
  • You want reliable stabilization
  • You need good low-light performance
