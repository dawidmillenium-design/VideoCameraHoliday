Our verdict in one sentence: The DJI Osmo Pocket 4 Pro is the best gimbal camera ever made – and the best travel camera of 2026 for anyone who values portability and stabilization above all else. But it's not for everyone.

I've tested the Pocket 4 Pro for 6 months across Southeast Asia, Europe, and South America. I've filmed humid night markets in Bangkok, windy coastlines in Portugal, and crowded metro stations in Paris. Here's what I found – and why this camera has replaced my mirrorless kit for 80% of my travel shoots.

👍 Pros

  • Insanely good 3‑axis gimbal stabilization
  • 1‑inch sensor for excellent low‑light video
  • Pocket‑sized – fits in a jeans pocket
  • ActiveTrack 6.0 AI tracking is magical
  • Intuitive UI – perfect for beginners
  • Great battery life for the size

👎 Cons

  • Not waterproof without a case
  • Digital zoom only – no optical zoom
  • Expensive at $649
  • Limited low‑light dynamic range (compared to full‑frame)
  • Can't change lenses

🌍 Real‑World Testing: 3 Continents, 12 Cities

I tested the Pocket 4 Pro on a 6‑month trip across:

  • London, UK: Rainy street scenes and Thames river walks. The gimbal handled the moisture well (though I kept it covered). Read the London interview →
  • Bangkok, Thailand: Night markets, humidity, and chaotic traffic. The 1‑inch sensor performed brilliantly in low light.
  • Lisbon, Portugal: Hilly cobblestone streets and beach sunsets. The ActiveTrack 6.0 kept me centred in every shot.
  • Bali, Indonesia: Rice terraces and beach vibes. The lightweight design meant I could carry it all day without fatigue.

Key takeaway: The Pocket 4 Pro is the camera I reached for when I didn't want to carry a heavy bag. It's the perfect "everyday" camera for travelers who prioritize portability and stabilization.

🎯 Who Should Buy the Pocket 4 Pro?

✅ Buy it if...

  • You want professional‑looking stabilization without carrying a gimbal.
  • You shoot a lot of walking footage (city breaks, travel vlogs, street photography).
  • You value portability above all else – you want a camera that fits in your pocket.
  • You're a beginner who wants great results without learning complex settings.
  • You have the budget for a premium travel camera.

❌ Skip it if...

  • You need a waterproof camera (buy the GoPro Hero 13 instead).
  • You need interchangeable lenses (buy the Sony ZV‑E10 II).
  • You're on a tight budget (the DJI Osmo Action 5 is $300 less).
  • You're a professional who needs full‑frame dynamic range (stick with a proper mirrorless).

🏆 Final Verdict: 4.8/5 Stars

The DJI Osmo Pocket 4 Pro is the best gimbal camera money can buy in 2026. It's not perfect – the lack of waterproofing and optical zoom are real limitations. But for the traveler who wants incredible stabilization in a pocket‑sized package, there's nothing better.

I've used it as my primary travel camera for 6 months, and it's replaced my mirrorless kit for 80% of my shots. The 1‑inch sensor, ActiveTrack 6.0, and intuitive UI make it a joy to use – and the footage speaks for itself.

⭐ Our Rating: 4.8/5 – Exceptional

🔄 Alternatives to Consider

  • GoPro Hero 13 ($499): If you need waterproofing and ruggedness, this is the one.
  • Sony ZV‑E10 II ($899): If you need interchangeable lenses and pro‑level video.
  • iPhone 16 Pro ($999): The camera you already own – with Action Mode for stabilization.
  • DJI Osmo Pocket 3 ($399): The previous model – still great, but the 1‑inch sensor on the 4 Pro is a big upgrade.
