Frequently Asked Questions

Is the GoPro Hero 13 Black worth upgrading from the Hero 12?

If you shoot in low light or need longer battery life, yes. The Hero 13 introduces the new HB-Series lens mods and an Enduro battery that significantly improves cold-weather performance, making it a worthy upgrade for serious travel vloggers.

How does the GoPro Hero 13 Black perform underwater?

It is natively waterproof to 10 meters (33ft) without a housing. The color science underwater is excellent, though we recommend using a red filter for depths beyond 5 meters to restore lost red tones.

Does the GoPro Hero 13 Black have good audio quality?

The built-in microphones are decent for wind-free environments, but for travel vlogging, we highly recommend using the Media Mod or a wireless mic like the DJI Mic 2, as wind noise can still be an issue at high speeds.
