The S5 II shines when the light fades. While filming evening markets in Prague, the full-frame sensor paired with a fast f/1.8 lens delivered clean, usable 4K footage at ISO 6400. The new phase-detect autofocus finally eliminates the "hunting" that plagued the original S5, locking onto subjects instantly even in chaotic environments.

The Active I.S. (stabilization) is genuinely impressive. Handheld walking shots through cobblestone streets look remarkably smooth, reducing the absolute need for a bulky gimbal on casual travel days.
