Insta360 Ace Pro Review: AI Action Camera for Travel

Leica optics, flip screen, and AI smarts make this the best action camera for travel vloggers who want GoPro quality with creative flexibility.

📅 September 4, 2026⏱️ 13 min read

⚡ Quick Answer

The Insta360 Ace Pro is the best action camera for travel creators who prioritize vlogging, low-light performance, and creative flexibility. Its flip screen, Leica lens, and AI features (auto-framing, highlight detection) make it easier to capture professional-looking footage solo. If you mainly shoot extreme sports, GoPro still edges ahead on stabilization.

Why the Ace Pro Stands Out

When Insta360 partnered with Leica for the Ace Pro's lens, skeptics wondered if it was just marketing. After testing this camera across 20 countries — from underwater cenotes in Mexico to neon-lit Tokyo streets — we can confirm: this collaboration delivers real optical advantages.

But the lens is only part of the story. The Ace Pro combines that glass with a large 1/1.3-inch sensor, a fully articulating flip screen, and genuinely useful AI features. Together, they create the most versatile action camera we've tested for travel content creation.

💡 Pro Tip: Enable AI Framing before solo vlogging sessions. The camera automatically keeps you centered as you move, walk, or gesture — perfect for museum tours, market walkthroughs, or hiking trails where you can't monitor the screen.

The Leica Lens Advantage

The Leica SUMMARIT lens isn't just a badge — it measurably improves image quality:

Sharpness Across the Frame

Corners remain sharp even in 8K mode, unlike competitors that soften at the edges. This matters for landscape shots where every detail counts.

Natural Color Rendition

Leica's color science produces natural, film-like tones straight out of camera. Skin tones look accurate, skies aren't oversaturated, and sunsets retain their subtle gradients.

Reduced Distortion

Wide-angle distortion is minimal compared to GoPro's signature fish-eye look. You get a more cinematic, documentary feel without heavy post-processing.

Image Quality & Low Light

🎬 8K Video: Gimmick or Useful?

We'll be honest: 8K is overkill for most travel creators. BUT, shooting in 8K and downsampling to 4K gives you incredibly sharp 4K footage with room to crop and reframe in post. We used this technique for a Netflix travel series, and the downscaled 8K looked better than native 4K from competitors.

🌙 Low-Light Champion

This is where the Ace Pro dominates. The large sensor and fast f/2.4 aperture gather significantly more light than GoPro or DJI action cameras. We filmed:

  • Night markets in Bangkok — clean footage at ISO 6400
  • Evening streets in Rome — usable up to ISO 12800
  • Aurora Borealis in Iceland — captured green hues without excessive noise

The dedicated PureVideo mode uses AI noise reduction for even better low-light results, though it limits resolution to 4K/30fps.

🏖️ Dynamic Range

HDR mode handles high-contrast beach scenes well, retaining detail in bright sand and dark shadows. Not cinema-camera level, but excellent for an action cam.

⚠️ Watch Out: 8K recording generates massive files (~1GB per minute) and drains batteries quickly. We rarely use it except for critical shots. For daily travel vlogging, 4K/60fps is the sweet spot.

AI Features That Actually Help

Insta360's AI implementation is the most practical we've tested:

AI Framing

The camera tracks you and keeps you centered, even as you move through the frame. We used this for solo walking tours through European cities — the framing stayed smooth and natural, not robotic.

Auto Highlight

After a day of shooting, the app analyzes footage and marks exciting moments (fast motion, scene changes, faces smiling). This cut our editing time by ~40% on a recent Japan trip.

Voice Control

"Start recording," "Take photo," "Turn off" — works reliably in English, Spanish, German, French, Japanese, and Chinese. Hands-free control is invaluable when mounting the camera on helmets, bikes, or tripods.

Flip Screen for Vlogging

This feature alone makes the Ace Pro our top pick for travel vloggers. The 2.4-inch screen flips 180°, so you can see yourself while recording. Benefits:

  • Perfect framing — no more guessing if you're in shot
  • Check settings — verify exposure, focus, and battery without stopping
  • Vertical shooting — mount vertically for native 9:16 Reels/TikTok

Compared to GoPro's front screen (tiny, info-only) or DJI's fixed rear screen, the Ace Pro's flip design is infinitely more practical for self-recording.

Real-World Travel Testing

We took the Ace Pro to 20 countries over 10 months. Highlights:

🇲🇽 Mexico (Underwater Cenotes)

Tested waterproofing to 8m depth in Dos Ojos cenote. Colors remained vibrant, no fogging, and touch controls worked through the waterproof seal. No case needed.

🇯🇵 Japan (Urban Night Scenes)

Shinjuku and Shibuya at night pushed the low-light capabilities hard. PureVideo mode produced surprisingly clean footage of neon signs and crowded crossings.

🇮🇸 Iceland (Cold Weather)

-15°C (5°F) reduced battery life to ~60 minutes. We kept spares in jacket pockets and swapped them warm. Camera itself handled cold flawlessly.

🇦🇺 Australia (Surf Testing)

FlowState stabilization handled paddle-out chop and wave rides well. Not quite GoPro HyperSmooth level for extreme action, but 90% there for recreational surfing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Insta360 Ace Pro better than GoPro Hero 13 for travel?

It depends on your priorities. The Ace Pro wins for vlogging (flip screen, better low-light, AI framing) and cinematic footage (Leica lens, 8K). GoPro Hero 13 excels for extreme sports (better stabilization, rugged build, HyperSmooth). For travel creators who vlog and want versatile shooting angles, the Ace Pro is our pick.

How good is the Insta360 Ace Pro in low light?

Best-in-class for an action camera. The 1/1.3-inch sensor and Leica lens gather significantly more light than competitors. We filmed night markets in Bangkok and evening street scenes in Rome with usable footage at ISO 6400. It's not a dedicated low-light camera, but it's the closest action cam gets.

Does the Insta360 Ace Pro need a case for waterproofing?

No, it's waterproof to 10 meters (33 feet) without a case. This is a huge advantage over GoPro, which requires a housing for depths beyond 10m. We used it snorkeling in the Great Barrier Reef and cenotes in Mexico with zero issues.

What AI features does the Insta360 Ace Pro offer?

Three game-changers: AI Framing automatically keeps you centered (great for solo vlogging), Auto Highlight detects and marks exciting moments for quick editing, and Voice Control works reliably in multiple languages. These features save hours of editing time on travel videos.

How's the battery life on the Insta360 Ace Pro?

About 90–100 minutes per battery when shooting 4K/30fps. In cold weather or with 8K recording, expect 60–75 minutes. We recommend buying the 3-battery bundle for full-day shooting. Batteries charge quickly (~45 minutes each) in the included dual charger.

Can the Insta360 Ace Pro shoot vertical video?

Yes, and it's brilliant for social media. Mount the camera vertically, and it records native 9:16 footage without cropping. Combined with the flip screen for self-framing, it's the best action camera for Instagram Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts creators.
