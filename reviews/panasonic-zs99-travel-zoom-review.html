Verdict: The Panasonic ZS99 is the best travel zoom compact for travellers who need reach in a pocket. The 24-720mm lens is a genuine superpower — perfect for wildlife, concerts, and landmarks. Image quality is good for its sensor size, but low‑light and video stabilisation are weak.

📋 Key Specifications

Panasonic Lumix ZS99 / TZ99 – Specs
Sensor20.3MP 1/2.3" BSI CMOS
LensLeica DC Vario‑Elmar 24‑720mm f/3.3‑6.4 (30x optical)
Image Stabilisation5‑axis Hybrid OIS+ (stills)
ViewfinderNone (tilt touchscreen only)
Video4K/30, 1080p/60
Weight~322g
Price~$499 – $699 MSRP

✅ Pros

  • 30x optical zoom – 24‑720mm
  • Good image quality for daylight
  • Effective stills stabilisation
  • Pocketable (~322g)
  • Tilt touchscreen

❌ Cons

  • Small 1/2.3" sensor – noise in low light
  • Soft at full 720mm
  • Video stabilisation is poor
  • No viewfinder
  • Contrast‑detect AF
