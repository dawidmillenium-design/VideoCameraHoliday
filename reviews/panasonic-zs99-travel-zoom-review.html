🔭 Panasonic Lumix ZS99 / TZ99 Travel Review (2026)

Is this the best travel zoom compact? With 30x optical zoom (24-720mm) in a pocketable body, the ZS99 fills a hole none of our other picks cover.

📅 Tested: Sep 2026 🔭 Zoom: 30x (24-720mm) ⭐ Rating: 8.5 / 10

Verdict: The Panasonic ZS99 is the best travel zoom compact for travellers who need reach in a pocket. The 24-720mm lens is a genuine superpower — perfect for wildlife, concerts, and landmarks. Image quality is good for its sensor size, but low‑light and video stabilisation are weak.
