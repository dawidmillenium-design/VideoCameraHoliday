Holiday Video Camera Rating

Easier software than Insta360, but lower resolution. Best for casual 360° shooters who value simplicity.

The GoPro Max 2 is GoPro's second attempt at a 360° camera. It trades the Insta360 X4's 8K resolution for simpler software and better integration with the GoPro ecosystem.

Key Specifications

360° Video5.6K30
Single Lens4K30
StabilizationMax HyperSmooth
Waterproof5m
Battery~85 minutes
Price$499

📱 The GoPro Quik Advantage

GoPro's Quik app is significantly simpler than Insta360's. Reframing is more limited but much faster. If you want to shoot 360° without learning complex software, the Max 2 is the better choice.

🎬 Video Quality

5.6K sounds lower than the X4's 8K, but reframed to 1080p, the difference is minimal. The Max 2's colors are more saturated and "GoPro-like" — punchy and ready for social media without editing.

❌ Where It Falls Short

  • Only 5m waterproof (vs 10-15m for competitors)
  • No 4K60 in single lens mode
  • Reframing options are more limited
  • Battery life is average
💡 Verdict: Choose Max 2 for simplicity and GoPro ecosystem. Choose Insta360 X4/X5 for maximum quality and creative control.
