**Mandate:** Sequence the 3-hub / 15-spoke topical map into a seasonality-aware 24-month publishing calendar that aligns field-data collection windows with travel seasons, product cycles, and AI Overview citation windows.

---

## Strategic Assumptions (Stated)

1. **Assumption:** You have physical access to all 5 core cameras (Osmo Pocket 3, GoPro Hero 13, Sony ZV-1 II, Insta360 X5, Osmo Action 6) and can field-test in ≥4 destinations across 24 months.
2. **Assumption:** Data collection must precede publication by **60–90 days** (capture → archive → analyze → publish per E-E-A-T Auditor workflow).
3. **Assumption:** Seasonality drives both *field conditions* (monsoon, aurora, alpine snow) and *search demand* (pre-trip planning peaks 8–12 weeks before travel).
4. **Assumption:** Hub 2 (Durability) publishes first — it has the longest data lead time and anchors cluster E-E-A-T (per Topical Architect sequencing).
5. **Assumption:** Each quarter delivers exactly **3 pieces**: 1 Comparison, 1 Destination Guide, 1 Tutorial — mapped to the 15 spokes + 3 hubs across 8 quarters (24 pieces total).
6. **Assumption:** Product cycle: DJI/GoPro/Insta360 typically refresh flagships in **Aug–Oct**. Calendar reserves Q3 2027 for "next-gen vs. current-gen" field comparisons.
