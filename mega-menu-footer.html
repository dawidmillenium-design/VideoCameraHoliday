How to Use This Site

Most visitors arrive with one of three questions: which camera to buy, how to shoot a specific destination, or how to fix something in the edit. The navigation is arranged to answer those directly.

If you are still choosing gear, start with Best Cameras 2026 and then narrow down using the budget and condition guides — Under $500, Waterproof Cameras, Hiking Cameras, or Ski Holiday Cameras. The Camera Checklist is worth reading before any purchase, because it lists what a travel kit actually needs rather than what a spec sheet rewards.

If you already own a camera, the Popular Reviews section covers the models travelers ask about most: the DJI Pocket 3, GoPro Hero 13, Insta360 X5, DJI Action 5 Pro, Sony ZV-1 II, and iPhone 16 Pro. Each review is written from trip experience rather than lab measurement.

If you are planning a specific trip, the Destinations section pairs locations with the right gear — Beach Holidays, Ski Holidays, City Breaks, Desert Travel, and the Underwater Guide. The interview archive goes deeper on individual destinations, with dedicated guides for Iceland, Japan, Southeast Asia, US National Parks, European Night Markets, and Desert Dusty Conditions.

If the problem is technique, the How-To Essentials cover the recurring ones: how to Stabilize Without Gimbal, how to Backup Footage, how to Film Yourself Solo, how to Choose Memory Card, and how to Film in Rain.

And if the footage is already shot, the Editing section handles Color Grading, Editing Apps, Post to YouTube, and the perennial 24fps vs 120fps decision.

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Holiday Video Camera different from other camera sites?

The site is built on real travel camera testing rather than lab charts. Reviews and guides reflect how gear performs on actual trips — in cities, on trails, in snow, in deserts, and underwater — not how it performs in a controlled test environment.

Where should I start if I'm buying my first travel camera?

Begin with Best Cameras 2026 for the overall picture, then narrow down with the guide that matches your trip: Under $500 if budget is the priority, Waterproof Cameras for water and rain, Hiking Cameras for long days on foot, or Ski Holiday Cameras for cold-weather travel. The Camera Checklist covers what to pack alongside the camera.

Does the site cover editing and post-production, or only gear?

Both. Alongside the buying guides and reviews, the Editing section covers Color Grading, Editing Apps, Post to YouTube, and 24fps vs 120fps. The interview archive also includes sessions on AI color grading and audio cleanup, AI video editing travel workflows, and audio tips for noisy locations.
