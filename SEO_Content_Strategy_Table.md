## 🎯 EEAT Enhancement Checklist (All Pages)

Apply these EEAT signals across ALL consolidated and new pages:

### Experience Signals
- [ ] Add "Tested in [Location]" badges with geotagged photos
- [ ] Include real-world failure stories (what went wrong during testing)
- [ ] Show wear-and-tear evidence on long-term gear
- [ ] Embed original video/photo proof (not stock media)

### Expertise Signals
- [ ] Author bio with relevant credentials (certifications, years of experience)
- [ ] "How We Test" methodology section with measurable criteria
- [ ] Technical specifications explained in plain language
- [ ] Comparisons based on controlled testing (not just specsheets)

### Authoritativeness Signals
- [ ] Cite industry standards (IP ratings, aviation regulations, etc.)
- [ ] Link to manufacturer documentation and third-party lab tests
- [ ] Include expert quotes or collaborations (other creators, engineers)
- [ ] Add "Featured In" logos if mentioned elsewhere

### Trustworthiness Signals
- [ ] Clear affiliate disclosure (FTC compliant)
- [ ] Last updated date with changelog for major revisions
- [ ] Correction policy for errors
- [ ] Real contact information and about page linkage

1. **Location Badge Generator**: Auto-add geotags and "Tested in [City]" markers from EXIF data
