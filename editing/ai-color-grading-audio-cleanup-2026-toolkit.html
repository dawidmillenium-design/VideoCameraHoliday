Master AI color grading & audio cleanup for travel

AI won't make bad footage look like Hollywood. But if you shot decent footage, AI can save you 10–15 hours per project.

By Dawid Millennium | Published August 31, 2026 | 16 min read | ✂️ Editing

AI in video editing won't make bad footage look like Hollywood. But if you shot decent footage, AI can save you 10–15 hours per project by automating the tedious parts.

⚡ Key Takeaways

  • DaVinci Resolve (free) wins for color matching with Shot Match
  • Adobe Podcast Enhance (free) removes 95% of echo and noise
  • AI handles 80% of technical work — you handle the 20% creative
  • Time saved per project: 10–14 hours

What AI Can (and Can't) Do

✅ AI CAN Match colors across cameras, remove wind noise, basic grading, upscale 1080p, stabilize, auto-transcribe, detect silence.
❌ AI CAN'T Fix blown highlights, recover out-of-focus, make 1080p look like 4K, replace bad storytelling.

DaVinci Resolve vs. CapCut vs. Premiere

DaVinci Resolve
Free · Best Overall

Shot Match saves 2–3 hours per project. Free version handles everything most travel editors need.

CapCut
Free · Best for Social

Best for quick social edits. Saves 30–45 minutes per project on vertical content.

Premiere Pro
$23/mo · Full Pipeline

Best for full production pipelines. Only worth it if you're already in the Adobe ecosystem.

Matching Shots from 3 Different Cameras

  1. Shoot a color reference at start of each day
  2. Normalize Log footage to Rec.709 first
  3. Use AI Match (Shot Match in Resolve)
  4. Check skin tones on vectorscope
  5. Match exposure separately
  6. Apply creative grade LAST

AI Audio Cleanup

The 3 Problems

Wind Noise
Adobe Podcast · CapCut · Resolve

Adobe Podcast Enhance removes most wind rumble in one click. DaVinci Voice Isolation works for ambient.

Crowd Roar
Adobe Podcast · Auphonic · iZotope RX

Layer in clean ambience at −20 dB after cleaning. Preserves crowd energy without the harshness.

Room Echo
Adobe Podcast (95% removal)

Adobe Podcast Enhance is the standout tool here — it flattens echo better than paid plugins.

My Workflow

  1. Upload to Adobe Podcast Enhance (free, browser)
  2. For ambient audio: DaVinci Voice Isolation
  3. Extreme cases: iZotope RX ($129)
  4. Layer clean ambient at −20dB
  5. Normalize to −14 LUFS for YouTube

⚠️ The Over-Processing Trap

If your narrator sounds like a 2005 phone call, reduce noise reduction by 20% and add room tone back.

The AI-Assisted Workflow

  • 🤖 Color matching: DaVinci Shot Match −2–3 hrs
  • 🤖 Basic exposure: Auto Color −1 hr
  • 🤖 Video noise reduction: DaVinci NR −30 min
  • 🤖 Audio cleanup: Adobe Podcast −1–2 hrs
  • 🤖 Subtitles: CapCut auto-caption −2 hrs
  • 🤖 Silence removal: Descript −1 hr
  • 👤 Story structure: My brain Human
  • 👤 Creative grade: My eye Human
  • 👤 Music selection: My ears Human
Total AI Time Saved
10–14 hours per project

Before/After: DJI Pocket 3 D-Log Footage

  1. Color Space Transform: D-Log → Rec.709
  2. Auto Balance (2 seconds)
  3. Shot Match to reference clip
  4. Creative LUT + manual adjustments
  5. Add 35mm grain at 10% opacity

Total time: 4 minutes per clip.

Worth Paying For

DaVinci Resolve Studio
$295 one-time

AI noise reduction, magic mask, and GPU acceleration. The single best paid upgrade for travel editors.

Adobe Podcast Enhance
Free tier incredible

The free browser tool removes echo and noise like nothing else at this price point (which is zero).

Topaz Video AI
$199 one-time

Upscaling and frame interpolation. Worth it only if you regularly rescue older 1080p travel footage.

Skip
Most $30–50/mo "AI editors"

Subscription AI tools rarely justify their monthly cost for travel editors who work on a few projects a year.
