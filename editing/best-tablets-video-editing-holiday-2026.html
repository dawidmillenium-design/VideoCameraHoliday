Best Tablets for Video Editing on Holiday 2026: iPad vs. Android

⚡ Quick Answer

Best Overall: iPad Pro 13" (M4) — Unbeatable performance, best screen, and exclusive access to Final Cut Pro for iPad. Best Android: Samsung Galaxy Tab S9 Ultra — Massive 14.6" screen, includes DeX mode for desktop-like workflow. Value Pick: iPad Air (M2) — 90% of the Pro performance for significantly less money.

Key Takeaways

  • RAM is Critical: For 4K editing, do not buy a tablet with less than 12GB RAM. 8GB will choke on multicam timelines.
  • Storage Speed: Internal storage is fast; external SSDs via USB-C are essential for large projects. Ensure the tablet supports USB 3.2/Thunderbolt speeds.
  • App Ecosystem: iPad wins on optimized apps (LumaFusion, Final Cut). Android is catching up with DaVinci Resolve but still has bugs.
  • Screen Quality: OLED is superior for color grading. Mini-LED (iPad Pro) is excellent but suffers from slight blooming in dark scenes.

Best Editing Apps for Tablet

Hardware is useless without software. Here is the stack we use:

  • Final Cut Pro for iPad: (iPad Only) The fastest editor on the platform. Magnetic timeline is unique. Subscription-based but worth it for pros.
  • LumaFusion: (iPad & Android) The veteran choice. One-time purchase ($30). Excellent multicam support and file management. Best for traditional editors.
  • DaVinci Resolve for iPad: (iPad Only) Desktop-grade color grading. Free version is powerful; Studio requires subscription. Steep learning curve.

FAQ

Can I edit 4K 60fps footage smoothly?

Yes, on the iPad Pro M4 and Samsung Tab S9 Ultra. Both handle 4K/60fps H.265 footage without dropping frames. On lower-end tablets (8GB RAM), you may need to use proxy files.

Do I need a keyboard and trackpad?

For serious editing, yes. Touch is great for trimming, but keyboard shortcuts (J, K, L) speed up the workflow significantly. The Magic Keyboard (iPad) or Samsung Book Cover are recommended.

How much storage do I need?

256GB is the minimum. However, we recommend buying the base model (128/256GB) and spending the savings on a fast 1TB or 2TB external SSD (like Samsung T7 or SanDisk Extreme).

Is Android better than iPad for editing?

Currently, no. iPad has better-optimized apps (Final Cut, DaVinci). Android hardware is great (Samsung), but software support lags behind. Choose Android only if you strictly prefer the OS or need the massive 14.6" screen.
