Key Takeaways

  • Efficiency: Turn one 10-minute vlog into a 1,500-word blog post in under 20 minutes.
  • Accuracy: Use specialized transcription tools (not just YouTube auto-captions) for clean input.
  • Prompt Engineering: The key is telling the AI to remove "spoken language" filler words and structure the text for reading, not listening.
  • SEO Boost: Add internal links and headers manually after the AI draft is generated.

Why Repurpose Video to Text?

You already did the hard work: researching the location, filming the B-roll, and explaining the details on camera. Why stare at a blank page when you already have the script?

Google cannot watch your video. By converting your video content into a well-structured blog post, you capture the search traffic that prefers reading over watching, while simultaneously boosting the SEO of the video page itself through internal linking.

Step 1: Get Accurate Transcripts

Garbage in, garbage out. If you feed a messy transcript to an AI, you get a messy article. Avoid copying YouTube's auto-captions directly if possible, as they lack punctuation and context.

Recommended Tools: Use Riverside.fm, Descript, or Otter.ai to generate a high-accuracy transcript with speaker separation and timestamps. Export as a .TXT or .SRT file.

Step 2: The AI Prompt Framework

This is the secret sauce. Don't just say "rewrite this." Use a structured prompt to force the AI to act like an editor.

Copy/Paste this Prompt:

"Act as a professional travel editor. I will provide a raw transcript of a travel video. Your goal is to convert this spoken content into a comprehensive, SEO-friendly blog post.

Rules:
1. Remove all filler words (um, ah, like, you know).
2. Convert spoken first-person ('I walked here') to engaging narrative text.
3. Structure the text with H2 and H3 headers based on topics.
4. Create a bulleted list for any tips or gear mentioned.
5. Keep the tone authoritative but friendly.
6. Do not invent facts not present in the transcript.

Input Transcript:
[PASTE YOUR TRANSCRIPT HERE]"

Step 3: Human Polish & SEO

The AI gives you a 90% draft. The final 10% is where you rank.

  • Add Internal Links: Link to your other guides (e.g., link "camera gear" to your Best Cameras guide).
  • Insert Images: Place screenshots from your video or photos from the trip between paragraphs.
  • Meta Data: Write a custom meta description and title tag (AI titles are often boring).
  • Embed the Video: Place the original YouTube video at the top or middle of the post to increase dwell time.

FAQ

Is this considered duplicate content by Google?

No. As long as the text is significantly rewritten and structured differently from the video captions, it is considered unique content. The medium (text vs. video) changes the user experience entirely.

Can I do this for Shorts/Reels?

Shorts are too brief for full articles. However, you can combine 5-10 Shorts about one destination into a single "Ultimate Guide" blog post using this same method.

What if my video has background music?

Most modern transcription tools (like Descript) have audio enhancement that isolates voice from music. Ensure you use these features before generating the transcript.
