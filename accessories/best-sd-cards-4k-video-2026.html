Best SD Cards for 4K Video 2026: Speed & Reliability Test

Slow cards cause dropped frames and corrupted files. We tested write speeds for 4K 60fps and 8K recording to find the safest options.

⚠️ Critical Warning: Avoid cheap no-name cards on eBay/Amazon. Fake cards often report false capacity (showing 128GB but actually 8GB), leading to instant data loss when traveling. Stick to reputable brands like SanDisk, Samsung, or Lexar.

Understanding V30 vs V60 vs V90

For 4K 30fps, a V30 card is sufficient. For 4K 60fps/120fps or 8K, you need V60 or V90. The "V" number indicates minimum sustained write speed in MB/s.
