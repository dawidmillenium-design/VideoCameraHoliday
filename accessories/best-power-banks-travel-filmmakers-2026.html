Best Power Banks for Travel Filmmakers 2026: 24-Hour Charge

⚡ Quick Answer

Best Overall: Anker 737 (24,000mAh) — 140W output charges laptops and drones simultaneously. Best Compact: Shargeek Storm 2 Slim — Transparent design, fits in pocket, 100W output. Budget: Xiaomi Power Bank 3 Pro — Reliable 20,000mAh at half the price.

Key Takeaways

  • Wattage Matters: Drones need 45W+, Laptops need 65W+. Standard 18W phone banks are too slow.
  • Airline Limits: Keep capacity under 100Wh (approx 27,000mAh) to avoid special approval.
  • Pass-Through Charging: Essential for editing while the bank itself recharges.

Airport Security Rules (Crucial)

⚠️ Critical: NEVER check power banks in luggage. Lithium batteries must be in carry-on only. If found in checked bags, they will be confiscated, and you may be fined.

The 100Wh Rule: Most airlines allow batteries up to 100Wh without approval. This equals roughly 27,000mAh at 3.7V. All picks below are under this limit.

FAQ

Can I take these on a plane?

Yes. FAA limits lithium batteries to 100Wh without approval. The Anker 737 is 86.4Wh, so it's safe for carry-on. Never check power banks in luggage.

How many drone batteries can I charge?

A 24,000mAh bank can fully charge roughly 3-4 DJI Mavic 3 batteries (5000mAh each) from empty, accounting for conversion loss.

Does it support pass-through charging?

Most modern banks (including all listed here) do. You can charge the bank while it charges your camera simultaneously.
