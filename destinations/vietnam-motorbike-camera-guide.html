📹 Holiday Video Camera

Vietnam by motorbike is a bucket-list adventure – winding mountain passes, rice terraces, and chaotic city streets. Filming from a bike requires compact gear, dust/rain protection, and excellent stabilisation. We spoke to Linh Nguyen, a Vietnamese travel videographer who has ridden the length of the country twice, to get the real story.

⚡ Quick Takeaways for Motorbike Filmmakers

  • Best motorbike camera: DJI Osmo Pocket 3 (stabilised handlebar shots) or Ricoh GR IV (pocketable street).
  • Top routes: Ha Giang Loop (karst mountains), Ho Chi Minh Trail (jungle), Hai Van Pass (coastal).
  • Dust is everywhere: Carry a rain cover and a small blower. Clean your gear daily.
  • Backup your footage: SD cards can get lost on the road. Back up daily to a portable drive.

📍 4 Must-Film Motorbike Routes

1. Ha Giang Loop – Karst Mountains

Why it's great: The most spectacular motorbike route in Vietnam – limestone karsts, deep valleys, and ethnic minority villages.

Filming tip: October–November is the best season (dry, golden rice). A gimbal (Pocket 3) is essential for handlebar shots.

2. Ho Chi Minh Trail – Jungle & History

Why it's great: A legendary supply route through the mountains, now a quiet road through dense jungle.

Filming tip: Morning mist creates a moody atmosphere. Shoot in flat profile to capture the greens.

3. Hai Van Pass – Coastal Views

Why it's great: The iconic pass featured in Top Gear – winding roads with ocean views.

Filming tip: Sunset is spectacular. Use a telephoto lens to compress the mountains and sea.

4. Hanoi Old Quarter – Chaotic Streets

Why it's great: Dense motorbike traffic, street food, and colonial architecture.

Filming tip: Ricoh GR IV is perfect for discreet street photography. Shoot at 1/250s to freeze the action.

🌧️ Weather & Road Realities

  • North vs South: North has cooler winters and hot summers; South is tropical year-round.
  • Rainy season: May–September. Always carry a rain cover for your gear.
  • Dust: Highways and mountain roads are dusty – clean your gear daily.

"The Pocket 3 is perfect for motorbike travel – it's tiny, stabilised, and the handlebar mount gives you hands-free shots. For street photography in Hanoi, the GR IV is unbeatable." — Linh Nguyen, Vietnamese Travel Videographer

📜 Permits & Etiquette

  • International Driving Permit: Required for motorbikes in Vietnam.
  • Helmet rule: Always wear a helmet (legal requirement).
  • Respect locals: Ask before filming village markets or people.
