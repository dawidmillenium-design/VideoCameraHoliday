📍 3 Must-Film Locations in Los Angeles

1. Hollywood Sign – at Sunrise

Why it's great: The most iconic landmark in LA. The sunrise light hits the sign perfectly, and the crowds haven't arrived yet.

Filming tip: 6:00 AM is the sweet spot – the light is soft, the streets are empty, and you'll have the classic view all to yourself. The best spot is Lake Hollywood Park or the Mulholland Drive viewpoint. A telephoto lens (70–200mm) compresses the sign against the city skyline, while a wide-angle captures the full Hollywood Hills panorama. A tripod is essential for sunrise timelapses.

2. Santa Monica Pier – at Sunset

Why it's great: The iconic pier, the Ferris wheel, and the Pacific Ocean create a stunning sunset scene. The golden hour light is magical.

Filming tip: Golden hour (just before sunset) is the best time – the warm light over the ocean and the pier lights create a cinematic scene. The pier entrance offers a classic composition with the Ferris wheel and the ocean. A wide-angle lens (16–35mm) captures the full scene, while a telephoto compresses the pier and the mountains. A polarizing filter helps cut through the glare on the water.

3. Griffith Observatory – at Night

Why it's great: The observatory offers a panoramic view of the LA basin, the Hollywood Sign, and the city lights. At night, the view is breathtaking.

Filming tip: Nighttime (8–11 PM) is the best time – the city lights create a stunning backdrop. The observatory terrace offers the classic view of the Hollywood Sign and the city below. A fast prime lens (f/1.8 or faster) is essential for low-light shots. A tripod is essential for long-exposure shots. Shoot in flat profile to capture the dynamic range of the city lights.

🌞 LA Weather Reality for Filmmakers

Los Angeles has a Mediterranean climate with sunny weather year-round. Here's what to expect:

  • Summer (June–August): Hot and dry – temperatures hit 25–35°C (77–95°F). The sun is intense, and crowds are at their peak. Film early morning (6–9 AM) and golden hour (6–8 PM) to avoid the harsh midday sun.
  • Autumn (September–November): One of the best times to film – temperatures are 20–30°C (68–86°F), the light is soft, and the crowds are thinner. October is ideal – the autumn colours and mild weather make for perfect filming.
  • Winter (December–February): Mild and pleasant – temperatures are 10–20°C (50–68°F). The light is beautiful, and the skies are often clear. December offers holiday lights and festive scenes.
  • Spring (March–May): Mild and pleasant – temperatures are 15–25°C (59–77°F). The wildflowers bloom in the hills, adding colour to your shots.

🌡️ Pro Tips for LA's Sun

  • Use a polarizing filter: Essential for cutting through the glare on the ocean and car windows.
  • Use a variable ND filter: Keeps your shutter angle correct in the bright California sun.
  • Bring a lens hood: Prevents lens flare from the intense sun.
  • Shoot in flat profile: S‑Log or similar gives you the most dynamic range for grading the high-contrast LA light.
  • Protect your gear from heat: Keep cameras and batteries in the shade when not shooting.

✈️ Airport & Gear Logistics

Los Angeles International Airport (LAX) is the busiest airport in California. Here's what you need to know:

  • Getting to the city: Taxis, ride-hailing apps (Uber/Lyft), and shuttles are available. The LAX FlyAway bus connects to Union Station.
  • Rental cars: LA is a car city – renting a car is recommended for getting around to different filming locations.
  • Security: US airport security is thorough. Keep your gimbal batteries, camera bodies, and lenses in your carry-on.
  • Gear insurance: Consider travel insurance that covers camera gear – LA's crowds and heat are tough on equipment.

📜 Permits, Rules & Local Etiquette

  • Hollywood Sign: Filming the sign is allowed from public viewpoints. Commercial filming requires a permit from the City of Los Angeles.
  • Drones: Strictly regulated around LAX and Hollywood. Drones are banned near airports and restricted over many landmarks. Always check local regulations before flying.
  • Beach filming: Filming on public beaches is allowed. Ask permission before filming people – especially surfers and beachgoers.
  • Respect local customs: LA is a diverse city – avoid intrusive filming. Always ask before filming people.

💰 Budget Travel & Camera Gear

LA is an expensive city, but it's possible to film on a budget:

  • Accommodation: $50–150 per night for a budget hotel or hostel. Mid-range hotels are $150–300.
  • Food: A meal at a casual restaurant is $15–30. Street food and food trucks are cheaper ($10–20).
  • Transport: LA's public transport (Metro) is affordable, but a rental car is recommended for flexibility.
  • Camera gear insurance: Essential – LA's sun and crowds are tough on equipment.
