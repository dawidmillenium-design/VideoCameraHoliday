🏝️ Thailand Island Camera Guide 2026

The best cameras for Koh Phi Phi, Phuket, Krabi, and filming the Andaman Sea – tested by a local island videographer.

✅ Updated: September 2026 • Tested on 3 island-hopping trips

Thailand's islands are the ultimate tropical escape – limestone karsts, turquoise water, and vibrant nightlife. Filming here means balancing beach vlogging, underwater shots, and jungle treks. We spoke to Chai Sorn, a Phuket-based travel videographer who has filmed every major island in the Andaman Sea, to get the real story.

⚡ Quick Takeaways for Island Filmmakers

  • Best island camera: GoPro Hero 13 (beach/water) + DJI Pocket 3 (walking B‑roll).
  • Top islands: Koh Phi Phi (limestone cliffs), Phuket (Old Town & beaches), Krabi (Railay climbing), Koh Tao (diving).
  • Salt is your enemy: Rinse your gear with fresh water daily.
  • Shoot sunrise and sunset: The light is magical in the tropics.

📍 4 Must-Film Island Locations

1. Koh Phi Phi – Limestone Cliffs & Maya Bay

Why it's great: The iconic "The Beach" location – towering limestone karsts and crystal-clear water.

Filming tip: Sunrise (6 AM) is the only time Maya Bay is uncrowded. A GoPro Hero 13 is perfect for the longtail boat shots.

2. Phuket Old Town – Sino-Portuguese Architecture

Why it's great: Colourful shophouses, street art, and a vibrant food scene.

Filming tip: Golden hour is best for the pastel buildings. Use a Fujifilm X100VI for film simulations.

3. Railay Beach – Rock Climbing & Jungle

Why it's great: Accessible only by boat – stunning limestone cliffs and secluded beaches.

Filming tip: DJI Osmo Pocket 3 is great for climbing B‑roll. The cliff views are spectacular at sunset.

4. Koh Tao – Diving & Snorkeling

Why it's great: One of Thailand's best diving destinations – whale sharks, turtles, and coral gardens.

Filming tip: OM System TG-7 is excellent for underwater macro. Use a red filter for colour correction.

🌴 Island Weather & Logistics

  • Andaman Sea (Phuket, Krabi): Best season November–April. May–October is rainy.
  • Gulf of Thailand (Koh Tao): Best season February–September.
  • Heat & Humidity: Protect gear from heat. Keep batteries cool.

"The GoPro Hero 13 is the ultimate island camera – it's waterproof, rugged, and the colours are incredible in the Andaman Sea. For walking shots, I always carry the Pocket 3." — Chai Sorn, Phuket Videographer

📜 Permits & Etiquette

  • National Parks: Entry fees apply (e.g., Maya Bay, Phi Phi).
  • Drones: Strictly regulated in national parks. Always ask permission.
  • Respect nature: Do not touch coral. No sunscreen in the water (chemicals damage reefs).
