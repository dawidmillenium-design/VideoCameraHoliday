Maldives Underwater Camera Guide 2026

🎬 Real field footage: DJI Osmo Action 6 underwater test in the Maldives

The Maldives is a diver's paradise – turquoise water, vibrant coral reefs, manta rays, whale sharks, and luxury overwater villas. Filming underwater presents unique challenges: colour correction (red disappears first), housing reliability, and battery life in warm water. We spoke to Ahmed Nazeer, a Maldivian underwater videographer and PADI instructor, to get the real story.

⚡ Quick Takeaways for Underwater Filmmakers

  • Best underwater camera: OM System TG-7 for stills/macro, GoPro Hero 13 for video/action.
  • Top spots: Manta Point (rays), Hanifaru Bay (whale sharks), Fish Tank (reef), Maafushi (house reef).
  • Red filter is essential: Use a red filter or shoot in flat profile and grade later.
  • Housing check: Always test your housing before getting in the water.

📍 4 Must-Film Underwater Locations

1. Hanifaru Bay – Whale Sharks & Manta Rays

Why it's great: UNESCO Biosphere Reserve where whale sharks and manta rays gather to feed. The plankton-rich water makes it a feeding frenzy.

Filming tip: June–November is the best season. A GoPro Hero 13 with a dive housing is perfect for action shots.

2. Manta Point – Drone-Like Underwater

Why it's great: A cleaning station where manta rays come to be cleaned by smaller fish. They swim incredibly close.

Filming tip: Morning (8-11 AM) is best. Shoot in 4K/120fps for slow-motion glory.

3. Fish Tank (North Male Atoll) – Reef Life

Why it's great: One of the most accessible dive sites with stunning hard coral gardens and thousands of reef fish.

Filming tip: Macro lens (TG-7) is excellent for nudibranchs and clownfish.

4. Overwater Villa – Aerial & Above Water

Why it's great: The iconic Maldivian overwater villas with crystal-clear water underneath.

Filming tip: DJI Osmo Pocket 3 is great for villa walkthroughs and pool shots.

🌊 Underwater Filming Tips

  • Use a red filter: The ocean absorbs red light – a red filter brings it back.
  • Get close: Underwater visibility is limited. Get within 1-2 meters of your subject.
  • Shoot manual white balance: Auto WB will look blue. Set it manually at depth.

"The TG-7 is my go-to for macro – the super macro mode captures tiny nudibranchs that you can't even see with your eyes. For sharks and rays, the GoPro Hero 13 is unbeatable."

— Ahmed Nazeer, Maldivian Underwater Videographer

📜 Permits & Etiquette

  • Marine life: Do not touch corals or marine life. Maintain distance from whale sharks (minimum 3m).
  • Drones: Strictly regulated near resorts. Check local laws.
  • Housing: Always pressure-test your housing before the trip.
