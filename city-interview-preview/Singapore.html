⚡ Quick Answer

Best spots: Marina Bay Sands (Gardens by the Bay), Chinatown, Hawker Centers, Jewel Changi Airport. Drone law: Strictly banned without CAAS permit (very difficult for tourists). Gear: Dehumidifier bags, rain covers, polarizer for glass buildings.

Key Takeaways

  • Strict Drone Laws: No flying within 5km of airports or city center without CAAS permit. Fines up to SGD 20,000.
  • Humidity Control: 90% humidity causes lens fog. Use silica gel packs and acclimate gear slowly.
  • Permit Free Zones: Most public parks allow handheld filming; commercial shoots require NParks permit.
  • Best Time: Blue hour (7:00-7:30 PM) for Supertree Grove light show; early morning (7:00 AM) for empty streets.

Top 5 Filming Locations in Singapore

1. Gardens by the Bay (Supertree Grove)

The iconic futuristic trees light up nightly (7:45 PM & 8:45 PM). Film the Garden Rhapsody light show from the OCBC Skyway for elevated angles. The Cloud Forest dome offers stunning indoor waterfall shots.

Camera Settings: Tripod essential for night show (1/30s shutter). ISO 800-1600. Aperture f/4 for sharpness across the grove.

2. Marina Bay Sands & Merlion Park

Classic skyline shot from Merlion Park. The infinity pool view (hotel guests only) is unmatched. Film the Spectra light show at Event Plaza (free, nightly 8 PM).

Tip: Arrive 45 mins early for front row at Event Plaza. Use ND filters for daytime reflections on the bay.

3. Chinatown & Maxwell Food Centre

Vibrant street life, red lanterns, and authentic hawker food. Maxwell Food Centre is perfect for food vlogging (ask vendors first). Buddha Tooth Relic Temple offers ornate interiors.

Lighting: Mixed lighting (neon + fluorescent). Set custom white balance (around 4500K) to avoid orange/green casts.

4. Jewel Changi Airport

The world's tallest indoor waterfall (HSBC Rain Vortex). Light show in evenings. Stunning architecture perfect for wide-angle gimbal shots.

Note: Handheld filming allowed; tripods require approval from Changi Airport Group.

5. Haji Lane & Kampong Glam

Colorful street art, boutique shops, and Sultan Mosque. Great for energetic walking tours and fashion/lifestyle content.

Permits & Drone Laws

⚠️ Critical: Drone ban: Flying drones in Singapore is highly restricted. You need approval from CAAS (Civil Aviation Authority of Singapore) and police. Tourists rarely get permits. Penalties include heavy fines and imprisonment.

Commercial Filming: Requires permits from relevant authorities (e.g., NParks for parks, MBS for private property). Personal vlogging is generally tolerated in public spaces if not obstructing traffic.

Temple Etiquette: Ask permission before filming inside temples (e.g., Buddha Tooth Relic). No flash photography.

Safety & Humidity Management

Humidity Shock: Moving from AC (cold) to outdoors (hot/humid) causes instant lens fog. Keep gear in a sealed bag with silica gel when transitioning. Let it acclimate for 15 mins before unpacking.

Rain Season: Monsoon season (Nov-Jan) brings sudden downpours. Always carry a rain cover or plastic bag for your camera.

Laws & Order: Singapore is very safe but has strict laws. Don't litter, don't jaywalk, and respect no-filming signs in MRT stations (commercial requires permit).

FAQ

Can I fly my drone in Singapore?

Generally no. You need permits from CAAS and police. Flying without approval is illegal and punishable by fines up to SGD 20,000 or jail. Best to leave the drone at home.

Is tripod use allowed in public?

Yes, in most outdoor public spaces like Marina Bay promenade. However, tripods are restricted in MRT stations, airports, and some malls without permission.

How do I prevent lens fog?

Use silica gel packs in your camera bag. When moving from AC to outdoors, keep the camera in a sealed ziplock bag until it reaches ambient temperature (approx. 15 mins).

Can I film in hawker centers?

Yes, but always ask vendors before filming them specifically. Most are friendly if you buy food first. Avoid blocking walkways with gear.

What about filming in MRT stations?

Handheld personal filming is usually tolerated if not disruptive. Tripods and commercial shoots require a permit from SMRT or LTA. Do not film security checkpoints.
