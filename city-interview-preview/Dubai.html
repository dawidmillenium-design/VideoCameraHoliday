Dubai Filmmaker's Guide: Best Spots, Settings & Laws

From the Burj Khalifa to the deep desert—how to film the world's most futuristic city while navigating strict drone laws and extreme heat.

📅 September 4, 2026⏱️ 7 min read

⚡ Quick Answer

Best spots: Burj Khalifa (sunrise), Dubai Marina (blue hour), Al Fahidi Historic District (culture). Drone law: Strictly banned without GCAA/DCAA permit (very difficult for tourists). Gear: ND filters for bright sun, silica gel for AC transitions.

Key Takeaways

  • Permit Required: Commercial filming requires approval from Dubai Film Commission. Personal vlogging is generally tolerated in public spaces.
  • Drone Ban: Flying drones without a permit from GCAA and DCAA is illegal and can lead to confiscation/deportation.
  • Heat Management: Summer temps exceed 45°C. Cameras overheat quickly. Shoot in winter (Nov-Mar) or early morning.
  • Cultural Respect: No filming of people (especially women) without permission. Avoid government buildings.

Top 5 Filming Locations in Dubai

1. Burj Khalifa & Dubai Fountain

The tallest building in the world. Film from Dubai Mall exterior or The Palm for wide shots. The fountain show runs every 30 mins in the evening.

Camera Settings: Aperture f/8 for sharpness. Shutter 1/50 for fountain water smoothness. Use ND filter in daylight.

2. Dubai Marina & JBR

Modern skyscrapers reflecting on water. Perfect for blue hour timelapses. The Walk at JBR offers street life and beach views.

3. Al Fahidi Historic District (Al Bastakiya)

Wind towers and narrow alleyways. Contrast to the modern city. Great for cultural storytelling and slower paced shots.

4. Dubai Desert Conservation Reserve

Red dunes and camels. Requires guided tour. Golden hour here is magical. Watch out for sand getting into gear.

5. Palm Jumeirah (The View)

Iconic palm shape visible from The View at The Palm observation deck. Best shot is the monorail moving along the fronds.

Permits & Drone Laws

⚠️ Critical: Drones are strictly banned in Dubai without permits from both GCAA (federal) and DCAA (local). Tourists rarely get these. Flying illegally can result in jail time, heavy fines, and deportation. Do not risk it.

Commercial Filming: Requires permit from Dubai Film Commission. Fines for unauthorized commercial shooting are high. Personal travel vlogs are usually okay if discreet.

Safety & Etiquette

Privacy Laws: It is illegal to photograph/video people without consent, especially locals and women. Blur faces in editing if unsure.

Government Buildings: Never film police stations, military sites, or palaces. Security will intervene.

AC Shock: Moving from 45°C heat to 18°C AC causes condensation on lenses. Keep camera in a sealed bag for 15 mins when entering AC.

FAQ

Can I fly my drone in the desert?

No. The entire emirate requires permits. Even in the desert, you are within controlled airspace. Confiscation is common at customs.

Is filming allowed in Dubai Mall?

Personal handheld filming is tolerated. Tripods and large rigs require security permission. No filming inside certain stores.

When is the best time to visit?

November to March (25°C-30°C). Summer (June-August) is too hot for comfortable filming (45°C+).

Can I film inside mosques?

Only specific mosques open to tourists (e.g., Jumeirah Mosque) allow filming during guided tours. Others are restricted.

Do I need a permit for YouTube?

If monetized, technically yes. However, most travel vloggers operate under personal use unless doing paid brand deals on location.
