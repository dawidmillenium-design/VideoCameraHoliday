⚡ Quick Answer

Best spots: Victoria Peak (timelapse), Mong Kok night markets, Central escalators, Star Ferry. Rules: No filming inside MTR stations without permit. Drones banned in most urban areas. Gear: Fast prime lenses (f/1.8) for neon nights, rain cover for typhoon season.

Key Takeaways

  • MTR Ban: Filming inside subway stations is prohibited without written permission from MTR Corp.
  • Neon Mastery: Use manual white balance (3200K-4000K) to preserve colorful neon signs.
  • Crowd Density: Mong Kok is the densest place on earth; keep gear strapped tight against pickpockets.
  • Typhoon Season: June-Sept brings heavy rain; waterproof housing essential for outdoor shoots.

Top 5 Filming Locations in Hong Kong

1. Victoria Peak (The Peak)

Iconic skyline view. Take the tram up at dusk. Film the transition from blue hour to full night illumination. The Sky Terrace offers the best angle.

Camera Settings: ISO 800, f/5.6, 1/30 for handheld night shots. Use ND filter for long-exposure car trails.

2. Mong Kok Night Markets

Ladies Market and Temple Street. Chaos of neon, food steam, and crowds. Perfect for cyberpunk-style footage.

Tip: Shoot at ISO 3200+ to freeze motion in low light. Embrace the grain for atmosphere.

3. Central-Mid Levels Escalator

World's longest outdoor covered escalator system. Great for moving shots through old neighborhoods. Filming while riding is tolerated if handheld.

4. Star Ferry

Classic crossing between Kowloon and HK Island. Film the skyline from the deck. Cheap and iconic.

Warning: Tripods blocked by crowds during rush hour. Go late evening (21:00+) for space.

5. Tai O Fishing Village

Stilt houses on Lantau Island. Slower pace, traditional culture. Good for drone shots (check local rules) and boat tours.

Permits & Filming Laws

⚠️ Critical: MTR Subway Ban: No photography/filming inside MTR stations or trains without prior written consent. Security will stop you. Drone Law: Strict no-fly zones over most of HK Island and Kowloon. CAD permit required.

Commercial Filming: Requires permit from Food and Environmental Hygiene Department for markets, and MTR Corp for transit. Personal vlogging often overlooked if unobtrusive, but technically restricted in malls/stations.

Safety & Etiquette

Pickpockets: Extremely high risk in Mong Kok and Tsim Sha Tsui. Use cross-body bags. Never set camera down on market tables.

Typhoon Safety: Signal No. 8 shuts down transport. Don't film near waterfront during typhoons (waves sweep over promenade).

Privacy: Hong Kongers value privacy. Avoid filming faces close-up without permission, especially in protests or sensitive areas.

FAQ

Can I film inside the MTR subway?

No. MTR bylaws prohibit photography/videography without permission. Staff will intervene. Film outside stations instead.

Is it safe to film at night?

Yes, HK is very safe. Main risk is theft of gear in crowded markets. Keep straps secure.

Can I fly a drone in HK?

Highly restricted. Most urban areas are no-fly zones. You need CAD approval. Fines are heavy. Better to use ground-based timelapses.

How to protect gear from rain?

Typhoon season (Jun-Sep) brings torrential rain. Use rain covers or waterproof housing. Silica gel packs prevent fungus in humid storage.

Best time for neon shots?

Blue hour (just after sunset) balances neon signs with sky detail. Fully dark skies make signs pop but lose context.
