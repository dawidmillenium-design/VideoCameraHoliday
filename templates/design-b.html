Design B — Template Overview

This page is the design-b.html template for Holiday Video Camera, a travel videography blog built on real-world field testing rather than lab charts. The template carries the full site navigation, the complete reviews and guides library, and the destination and how-to hubs that readers use to plan what they shoot and what they shoot it with.

Below you will find the structure this template supports, the sections it links to, and answers to the questions readers ask most often about how the site is organised.

What This Template Covers

Design B is a hub-style layout. It surfaces the highest-traffic entry points first — reviews, guides, destinations, how-to, editing, and compare — then funnels readers into the specific camera pages and city series they came for. The navigation is deliberately flat so a reader planning a beach holiday can reach the waterproof camera guide in one tap.

About the Testing Approach

Every camera on this site is tested in real travel conditions. That means the reviews reflect how gear behaves on a beach holiday, on a ski slope, on a safari, or on a motorbike in Vietnam — not how it performs on a test bench. The About page, Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Contact page, and Sitemap are all linked from the footer of this template.

FAQ

What is design-b.html?

It is a page template for Holiday Video Camera. It renders the site's navigation, review library, guides, destination hubs, how-to sections, editing resources, and comparison pages in a hub-style layout.

How is the content organised?

Content is grouped into Reviews, Guides, Destinations, How-To, Editing, and Compare. Destinations break down further into Beach & Water, Adventure, and City Breaks, with a City Through the Lens series running alongside the city pages.

Is the site available in other languages?

Yes. Language links for Deutsch, Español, Français, Italiano, 日本語, 한국어, Polski, Português, ไทย, and 中文 appear in the header and footer.
