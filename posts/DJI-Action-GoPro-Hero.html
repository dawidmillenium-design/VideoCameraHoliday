The DJI Action 5 Pro vs GoPro Hero 13 debate is the biggest question for action camera buyers in 2026. Both are top waterproof cameras for beach holidays. We tested both across three countries to bring you the ultimate comparison.

Video Quality

GoPro Hero 13: 5.3K at 60fps – sharper details, better dynamic range.

DJI Action 5 Pro: 4K at 120fps – smoother slow-motion, excellent color science.

In low light, DJI wins with larger pixels and better noise reduction. In bright sunlight, GoPro captures more detail.

For underwater footage , DJI's color science produces more accurate colors.

Stabilization

GoPro Hero 13: HyperSmooth 6.0 – industry-leading stabilization.

DJI Action 5 Pro: RockSteady 3.0 – excellent, but slightly less smooth than GoPro.

Learn more about stabilization techniques in our guide.

Battery Life

GoPro Hero 13: 1.5 hours of continuous recording.

DJI Action 5 Pro: 1.2 hours of continuous recording.

GoPro also offers a larger battery ecosystem with more accessories.

Audio

Both cameras have decent internal mics, but GoPro's Media Mod offers better external mic integration. Check our accessory guide for audio recommendations.

Value

DJI Action 5 Pro: More features for the price – includes more accessories in the box.

GoPro Hero 13: Premium price but unmatched ecosystem and community support.

Read our full comparison for more details.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is better for travel, GoPro or DJI?

GoPro wins for overall travel versatility, but DJI offers better value and color science.

Is DJI Action 5 Pro waterproof?

Yes, it's waterproof to 20m without a housing.

Can I use GoPro accessories with DJI?

Some accessories are compatible, but mounts may need adapters.
