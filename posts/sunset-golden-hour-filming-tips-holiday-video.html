Golden hour – the magic time just before sunset – is every videographer's dream. Learn to stabilize your shots to capture this beautiful light without shake. Here's how to master sunset videos on your next holiday.

What is Golden Hour?

Golden hour is the first hour after sunrise and the last hour before sunset. The sun is low, creating warm, soft light that's perfect for video.

Key features:

  • Warm tones: Golds, oranges, and reds
  • Soft shadows: No harsh midday contrast
  • Long shadows: Adds depth to your footage

Underwater golden hour is also magical – the light penetrates differently.

Best Camera Settings

Exposure

Use manual exposure and meter for the highlights. Underexpose slightly to protect details in the bright sky.

White Balance

Set to "Daylight" or "Cloudy" for warm tones. Auto WB can remove the warm glow.

Frame Rate

24fps for cinematic feel, 60fps for slow-motion waves or clouds.

Histogram

Use your camera's histogram to avoid clipping highlights or shadows.

Composition Techniques

Silhouettes

Place subjects between you and the sun. Expose for the sky, creating dramatic silhouettes.

Reflections

Use water, glass, or shiny surfaces to reflect the sunset light.

Rule of Thirds

Place the horizon on the lower or upper third line for balanced composition.

Foreground Elements

Add interest with trees, rocks, or people in the foreground.

Beach sunsets are especially beautiful – use the ocean as a foreground element.

Gear for Sunset Videos

  • Lens: Wide-angle for landscapes, telephoto for compressed shots
  • ND filter: Allows you to shoot wide open in bright conditions
  • Stabilizer: Use a tripod or gimbal
  • Spare battery: Filming uses more battery than photos

Editing Sunset Footage

  • Color grade: Enhance warm tones, add contrast
  • Exposure: Bring up shadows slightly without clipping highlights
  • Sharpening: Add subtle sharpening for details
  • Music: Choose calm, atmospheric tracks

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best time to film sunset?

The best time is 30-45 minutes before sunset and 15-20 minutes after.

What camera settings are best for golden hour?

Use manual exposure, warm white balance, and meter for highlights. Shoot in log or flat profile for better color grading.

Can I film golden hour with my smartphone?

Yes! Modern smartphones capture excellent golden hour footage. Use manual mode if available for better control.
