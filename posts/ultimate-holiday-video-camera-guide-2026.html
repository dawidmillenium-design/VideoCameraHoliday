Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best holiday video camera for beginners?

The Sony ZV‑1 II or the DJI Osmo Pocket 3 are excellent choices. They offer simple controls, great image quality, and built‑in stabilisation – perfect for beginners.

Can I use my smartphone instead of a dedicated camera?

Yes – modern iPhones and flagship Androids capture great video. However, dedicated cameras offer better zoom, audio, and durability in extreme conditions. Read our comparison to decide.

What memory card speed do I need for 4K holiday videos?
