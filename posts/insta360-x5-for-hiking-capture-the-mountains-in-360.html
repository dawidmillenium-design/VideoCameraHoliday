Weight and Packability

The X5 easily fits in a hip belt pocket. It weighs significantly less than a mirrorless camera, making it perfect for long distances without fatigue.

Capture the Grand Scale

To capture a massive waterfall or mountain, place the X5 on the invisible stick, hold it high, and the 360 view will include the sky and the ground, giving a dramatic perspective.

Stabilization on Trails

Walking on uneven trails causes jarring footage. The X5’s FlowState Stabilization completely smooths out the hike, making it look like you are gliding.
