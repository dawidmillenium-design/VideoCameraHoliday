DJI Osmo Pocket 3 Review: Pocket-Sized Cinematic Power

The DJI Osmo Pocket 3 is the smallest camera with a 3-axis mechanical gimbal. It promises cinematic footage from a device that fits in your jeans pocket.

What is the Osmo Pocket 3?

The Pocket 3 combines a 1” sensor, 4K120 video, and a mechanical 3-axis stabilizer in a 179g package.

🎬 The Gimbal Difference

Why Mechanical Stabilization Matters

Digital stabilization (GoPro, phone) crops your footage and can look artificial. Mechanical gimbals physically move the camera — no cropping, natural motion.

Side-by-Side Test: Walking Through Tokyo

Camera Footage Quality
iPhone 16 Pro (digital stab) Good, slight jitter
GoPro Hero 13 (HyperSmooth) Very smooth, “floaty”
Osmo Pocket 3 (gimbal) Cinematic, natural

🎥 Video Quality Test

4K120 Slow Motion

The Pocket 3 shoots 4K at 120fps — a feature usually reserved for $2,000+ cameras.

Tested: Fushimi Inari shrine in Kyoto — 4K120 of people walking through torii gates. Cinematic gold.

Low Light Performance

The 1” sensor is significantly larger than action cam sensors. Low light is excellent for the size.

✈️ Travel-Friendly Features

1. Rotatable Screen

The 2” screen rotates from horizontal to vertical in one motion. Perfect for Instagram Reels/TikTok.

2. ActiveTrack 6.0

Tap a subject and the gimbal follows automatically. Works well for markets, pets, and self-tracking.

3. Quick Startup

From off to recording in 2 seconds. No missed moments.

❌ Limitations

1. Not Waterproof

This is the dealbreaker for beach holidays. One splash and it is dead. You need a separate action camera for water activities.

2. Fragile Gimbal

The mechanical gimbal is delicate. A drop on concrete likely breaks it.

3. Audio Without DJI Mic

Built-in mics are mediocre. For vlogging, you need the DJI Mic 2 ($99 extra).

🏆 Who Should Buy the Pocket 3?

Buy it if:

  • You want cinematic footage without carrying a gimbal
  • You film lots of walking tours and city exploration
  • You need 4K120 slow motion
  • You prioritize stabilization over waterproofing

Skip it if:

  • You need waterproofing (get GoPro Hero 13)
  • You want the best autofocus (get Sony ZV-1 II)
  • You are rough with gear (gimbal is fragile)

Is the Pocket 3 good for vlogging? Yes, with the DJI Mic 2. Built-in audio is mediocre.

How does it compare to a phone gimbal? Much better. Phone gimbals are bulky. Pocket 3 is always ready.
