Introduction

Solo travel vlogging usually involves awkwardly setting up a tripod and running back and forth. The Insta360 X5 completely eliminates this problem with the invisible selfie stick, creating the famous “drone-follow” effect. If you want to elevate your solo content, this is the ultimate tool. The Invisible Stick

The X5 uses AI to automatically stitch and hide the selfie stick. You just walk forward, and it looks like you have a personal drone cameraman. It is the biggest disruption to solo travel vlogging since the invention of the action cam.

How to Talk to the Camera

Talk directly to the lens. The wide 360 lens means you can hold the camera low and still have your whole body in frame. It gives a unique, cinematic perspective.

Vlogging Audio Setup

While the built-in mic is decent, for crystal clear audio while moving fast, you should clip a wireless mic to your shirt. Read our Best Microphones for Travel Vlogging guide to ensure your voice is crisp.
