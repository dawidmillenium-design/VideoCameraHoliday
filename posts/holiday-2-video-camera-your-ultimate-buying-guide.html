How to Load Film, Set Exposure, and Maintain Steady Shots

Because the camera is a manual film camera, it is best not to force any part. If you do not know how to put in film or how the controls work, read the manual first. A printed manual can be easier to use than a pdf in a folder on your phone. It is hard to right click through pages on your phone while you are holding the camera.

Keep these basics in mind:

  • Load the film in a gentle way and stop if you feel any strange resistance.

  • Follow the manual for both exposure settings and how to place the controls.

  • Hold the camera using both hands. This will help you get steady shots.

  • Do some short practice runs before you film your holiday. Take care while doing these practice runs.

Old gear gives you good results if you take your time. You get steady shots by moving slow, holding your ground, and not starting too fast. For exposure, use the steps given in the original instructions. Do not just guess. If you do things wrong, you might break the inside parts. If you do not want any trouble, practice at home before you use it on a trip.
