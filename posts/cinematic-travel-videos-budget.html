You don’t need a $10,000 cinema camera to make your travel videos look like Hollywood movies. With a few simple techniques and a bit of planning, you can make stunning, cinematic travel films using budget gear.

The “Cinematic” Formula

Cinematic video is not about the gear; it’s about the shot composition, lighting, and storytelling.

1. Shoot 24 FPS

The “Film Look” is literally just 24 frames per second. It gives video a slight, natural motion blur that feels much more cinematic than the smooth 60fps standard used for sports.

2. Apply the 180-Degree Shutter Rule

If you are shooting 24fps, set your shutter speed to 1/48th of a second (or 1/50th). This ensures your motion blur looks natural.

3. Use a “Log” or “Flat” Picture Profile

Most budget cameras and even modern smartphones have a “Flat” or “Log” setting. This makes the image look washed out and gray, but it captures way more information in the shadows and highlights, allowing you to color-grade it later to look like a movie.

Lighting on a Budget

You don’t need expensive studio lights. The best light is the sun. If you are recording someone, keep the sun behind them and to the side (called “Rembrandt Lighting”). If you are inside a dark hotel, use a cheap LED panel to light up your face.

For more detailed lighting setups, check out our Travel Video Lighting Guide.

Color Grading for Free

You can get a cinematic look using free editing apps. Adjust the temperature (make it slightly warmer), add a “teal and orange” tint (blue shadows, orange highlights), and add a slight film grain. You can learn how to do this on the go in our Edit Travel Videos on Phone & Tablet guide.
