1. Sony ZV-1 II — Best Overall Holiday Camera

The Sony ZV-1 II remains my top recommendation for most travelers in 2026.

Why it wins: - 18-50mm wide-angle lens perfect for selfies and landscapes - Real-time Eye AF keeps you in focus - Product showcase mode for travel gear reviews - Compact enough for any backpack

“I shot my entire Chiang Mai series with the ZV-1 II. Never missed a focus pull.” — My YouTube comment

Downside: No 4K60 and battery life is ~45 minutes of recording.

2. GoPro Hero 13 — Best Action Camera

For adventure holidays — snorkeling, hiking, skiing — the Hero 13 is unbeatable.

New in 2026: - 5.3K60 video - HyperSmooth 6.0 (gimbal-like stabilization) - 177° field of view mode - Better low light than Hero 12

3. DJI Osmo Pocket 3 — Best for Minimalists

The built-in 3-axis gimbal gives you smooth cinematic footage from your pocket. Perfect for city walking tours and food vlogs.

Final Verdict

For most holiday travelers, get the Sony ZV-1 II. It is the sweet spot of quality, size, and ease of use.

On a tighter budget? The Canon PowerShot V10 at $429 shoots excellent 4K and fits in any pocket.

Want 360° memories? The Insta360 X4 lets you reframe shots after filming — never miss the moment.
