🏖️ Beach Camera Care Tips

1. Rinse After Every Salt Water Use

Fresh water rinse prevents corrosion. Even “waterproof” cameras benefit.

2. Use a Floaty

Attach a floaty grip. Dropped cameras sink in seconds.

3. Keep Spare Batteries Cool

Heat kills batteries. Store spares in a cool bag.

4. Lens Protection

Use lens protectors ($15). Scratches ruin footage.
