📋 What to Look For in a Holiday Camera

1. Portability

You will carry this every day. Under 300g is ideal. Anything heavier stays in the hotel.

2. Battery Life

Holiday days are long. Look for swappable batteries or USB-C charging on-the-go.

3. Durability

Sand, salt water, dust, and drops happen. Waterproofing or a good case is essential.

4. Ease of Use

Complex menus kill spontaneity. Touchscreen, auto modes, and quick startup matter more than manual controls.

5. Video Quality

4K is the minimum in 2026. 4K60 is nice for slow-motion. 5.3K+ is overkill for most travelers.

FAQ

What is the best holiday camera for beginners? The Canon PowerShot V10 at $429 is the easiest to use with excellent auto modes and built-in stand.

Do I need 4K for holiday videos? Yes. 4K future-proofs your memories and allows cropping/reframing. Most TVs and phones now display 4K.

Is GoPro good for normal holiday videos? GoPro excels for action but can look too wide for casual family shots. Pair with a compact camera for versatility.

What is the best camera for snorkeling? GoPro Hero 13 or DJI Action 5 Pro. Both are waterproof to 10m+ without a case and have excellent underwater color.

Should I buy a 360° camera for holidays? If you want unique perspectives and do not mind post-processing, yes. For simple point-and-shoot, stick to traditional cameras.
