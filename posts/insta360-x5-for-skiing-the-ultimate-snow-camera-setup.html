Mounting on the Helmet

The best way to use the X5 on the slopes is to mount it on the top of your helmet using the official adhesive mounts. Because it sees 360 degrees, you don’t need to perfectly angle your head; the mountain and the skis will always be in frame.

The Invisible Pole for POV

If you don’t want to wear a helmet, use the invisible selfie stick. It looks like a drone is following you down the trail. It’s universally loved by ski creators for its “impossible” drone-like look.

Cold Weather Battery Tips

Batteries drain fast in the cold. Keep a fully charged spare in your inner jacket pocket to swap out when the camera dies mid-run. Always pair the X5 with a quality bag. Read our Best Waterproof Camera Bags for Travel to keep your gear dry in powder snow.
