Power & Batteries

1. Spare Batteries (Essential)

Holiday days are long. One battery is never enough.

Camera Stock Battery Life Spares Needed Best Third-Party Brand
Sony ZV-1 II 45min 3-4 Wasabi Power ($25)
GoPro Hero 13 90min 2-3 Wasabi Power ($20)
DJI Pocket 3 100min 2 DJI Official ($40)
Insta360 X4 75min 2-3 Insta360 Official ($35)

Pro tip: Third-party batteries from Wasabi or JJC perform identically to OEM at half the price. I have used them for 2 years without issues.

2. Portable Charger / Power Bank

Why: Charge batteries during lunch breaks, flights, and bus rides.

Recommended: Anker PowerCore 20000mAh (~$50) - Charges a camera battery 4-5 times - Charges phone, tablet, and camera simultaneously - TSA-approved for carry-on

Alternative: Anker PowerCore Slim 10000mAh (~$25) for ultralight travelers.

3. Dual Battery Charger

Why: Charge two batteries overnight while you sleep.

Recommended: - Sony: Sony BC-TRX (~$40) or Wasabi dual charger (~$20) - GoPro: GoPro Dual Battery Charger (~$25) - DJI: DJI Charging Case (~$50) — charges camera + batteries

Storage

4. Memory Cards (V30 Minimum)

4K video requires fast write speeds. A slow card causes dropped frames and recording errors.

Minimum spec: V30 (30MB/s write speed) for 4K30. V60 for 4K60.

Recommended: - Best overall: SanDisk Extreme Pro 256GB (~$35) — 170MB/s read, 90MB/s write - Best value: Samsung EVO Select 256GB (~$30) — 130MB/s read, reliable - Budget pick: Lexar Professional 633x 128GB (~$18) — good enough for 4K30

Pro tip: Buy 2x 128GB cards instead of 1x 256GB. If a card fails, you do not lose everything.

5. Card Reader (USB-C)

Why: Transfer footage to laptop/tablet for backup and editing.

Recommended: Anker USB-C SD Card Reader (~$15) - USB-C for modern laptops - SD + microSD slots - No drivers needed

Stabilization & Mounts

6. Mini Tripod

For: Timelapses, vlogging, stable shots on tables and rocks.

Recommended: - Best overall: Manfrotto PIXI Mini Tripod (~$25) — sturdy, compact, reliable - Best value: Ulanzi MT-08 Extendable Tripod (~$15) — extends to handheld grip - Most versatile: Joby GorillaPod 1K (~$35) — flexible legs wrap around anything

7. Action Camera Mounts

Essential mounts by activity:

Activity Mount Type Price Best For
Skiing/Snowboarding Helmet mount $15 POV footage
Snorkeling Hand strap $12 Underwater stability
Hiking Chest mount $20 Immersive walking
Motorbike Handlebar mount $18 Road POV
Vlogging Extension pole $15 Selfie shots

Recommended brand: GoPro official mounts are best but expensive. Ulanzi and Neewer make excellent third-party alternatives at 50% of the price.

8. Gimbal (For Non-Gimbal Cameras)

Only needed if your camera does not have built-in stabilization.

If you have a Sony ZV-1 II or Canon V10, a small gimbal helps:

Recommended: DJI OM 6 (~$150) for smartphones. For compact cameras, the Zhiyun Crane M3 (~$300) is the lightest option.

My take: Skip the gimbal and get a camera with built-in stabilization like the DJI Osmo Pocket 3. Less gear, better results.

Protection

9. Waterproof Case (For Non-Waterproof Cameras)

If your camera is not waterproof (Sony ZV-1 II, Canon V10, DJI Pocket 3), you need protection for beach and water activities.

Recommended: - Universal bag: DiCAPac WP-One (~$25) — fits most compact cameras - Sony ZV-1 II: Sony MPK-URX100A (~$80) — dedicated underwater case to 40m - Canon V10: No dedicated case available. Use DiCAPac bag.

10. Camera Case / Pouch

Why: Protects from drops, dust, and scratches in your bag.

Recommended: - Best overall: Peak Design Field Pouch (~$40) — weatherproof, organized - Best value: AmazonBasics Camera Case (~$12) — padded, simple - For action cams: GoPro Casey (~$50) — fits camera + accessories

11. Lens Cleaning Kit

Essential for: Salt spray, dust, fingerprints.

Recommended: Altura Photo Cleaning Kit (~$12) - Lens pen - Microfiber cloths (3-pack) - Cleaning solution - Air blower

Pro tip: Keep a microfiber cloth in every bag pocket. You will use it constantly.

Audio

12. External Microphone (For Vloggers)

Built-in camera mics pick up wind and ambient noise. An external mic dramatically improves audio quality.

Recommended: - Best overall: Rode VideoMicro II (~$80) — compact, no battery needed - Best value: Boya BY-MM1 (~$30) — 80% of Rode’s quality at 1/3 price - Wireless: DJI Mic 2 (~$99) — for DJI Pocket 3 users

Note: The Sony ZV-1 II has excellent built-in mics. External mic is optional unless filming in wind.

Lighting

13. Portable LED Light (Optional)

For: Night markets, indoor restaurants, vlogging after sunset.

Recommended: Ulanzi VL49 (~$20) - Credit card sized - 5500K daylight balanced - 800 lumens - Rechargeable via USB-C

My take: Nice to have, not essential. Most modern cameras handle low light well enough.

Organization

14. Cable Organizer

Why: USB-C, micro-USB, Lightning — travel creates cable chaos.

Recommended: BAGSMART Electronics Organizer (~$15) - Fits cables, batteries, SD cards - Compact, fits in any bag - Multiple compartments

15. Dry Bag (For Water Activities)

Why: Protects ALL your gear from water, sand, and humidity.

Recommended: Earth Pak Waterproof Dry Bag (~$20) - 10L size fits camera + accessories - Roll-top seal - Floats if dropped in water

What to Skip

Accessory Why Skip Better Alternative
Professional lighting kit Too bulky Portable LED (~$20)
Multiple lenses Defeats compact purpose Camera with good zoom
External monitor Overkill for holidays Camera’s built-in screen
Slider / dolly Impossible to carry Gimbal or stabilization
Drone (for most) Regulations, weight, risk 360° camera for aerial-like shots

FAQ

What accessories do I need for a holiday camera? Essential: spare batteries (3-4), 20,000mAh power bank, 2x 128GB SD cards, mini tripod, camera case. For water: waterproof case and dry bag.

Are third-party camera batteries safe? Yes, reputable brands like Wasabi and JJC are safe and perform identically to OEM at half the price.

What SD card for 4K video? V30 rated for 4K30. V60 for 4K60. SanDisk Extreme Pro and Samsung EVO Select are reliable.

Do I need a gimbal for travel videos? Not if your camera has built-in stabilization. GoPro HyperSmooth and DJI Pocket 3 gimbal handle stabilization internally.
