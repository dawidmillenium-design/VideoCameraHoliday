Filming water sports on holiday is thrilling but challenging. Water, splashes, and fast movement require the right technique. Start with our waterproof camera guide to choose the right gear. Here are our pro tips for capturing epic water sports footage.

Best Cameras for Water Sports

Action cameras are the go-to for water sports. Our top picks:

  • GoPro Hero 13: Excellent stabilization and waterproofing
  • DJI Osmo Action 5 Pro: Great color and low-light performance
  • Insta360 X4: 360° capture for creative edits

Compare GoPro and DJI action cameras to decide which suits your needs.

Camera Settings for Water Sports

Frame Rate: 60fps or Higher

Shoot at 60fps or 120fps for smooth slow-motion of splashes and jumps.

Field of View: Wide

Wide angle captures more action and makes movement look more dynamic.

Stabilization: Max

Use the highest stabilization setting for handheld shots on boats or jet skis.

Exposure: Auto or +1

Water reflects light – underexpose slightly to avoid blown-out highlights.

Filming Techniques

  • Get close: Water reduces visibility, so get as close as possible.
  • Use a floating handle: Keeps your camera safe and adds stability.
  • Film from multiple angles: First-person, third-person, and underwater.
  • Follow the action: Move with the subject for dynamic shots.

Essential Accessories for Water Sports Filming

  • Floating handle: Prevents losing your camera
  • Wrist strap: Extra security
  • Polarizing filter: Reduces glare from water
  • Waterproof housing: For cameras not waterproof on their own
  • Extra batteries: Cold water drains batteries faster

Post-Production Tips

  • Color grade: Enhance blues and greens for underwater footage
  • Slow motion: Use 60fps footage at 30fps for smooth slow-mo
  • Music: Choose upbeat tracks to match the energy of water sports

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best camera for surfing videos?

The GoPro Hero 13 is excellent for surfing with its HyperSmooth stabilization and waterproof design.

How do I keep my camera safe in saltwater?

Rinse it with fresh water after every use and avoid opening ports near water.

Can I film water sports with my phone?

Yes, with a waterproof case and float, but action cameras are more durable and offer better stabilization.
