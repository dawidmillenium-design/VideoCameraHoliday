Introduction

Editing 360-degree video sounds intimidating, but the Insta360 app has made it incredibly easy for travelers. The X5 captures all the data; the app lets you choose the angles later. Master this workflow, and your videos will look like they were shot with a professional camera crew. First, find the right editing tools in our Editing Travel Videos on Phones & Tablets guide.

Table of Contents

  • Using AI Tracking

The Reframe Workflow

Import your footage, then use the Reframe tool. You scroll to the frame you like, double-tap to set your keyframes, and the app will pan from angle to angle automatically. This is the “magic” of the X5.

Using AI Tracking

Select a person or animal in the shot, and the app will track them perfectly, turning your 360 shot into an automated cinematic pan.

Color Grading 360 Video

The X5 footage looks flat. Use the app’s LUTs or adjust the contrast and saturation. To really level up your travel colors, read our Color Grading Travel Videos Guide.

Exporting

Export at the highest quality. But remember, 360 files are huge. You can always export in 4K for social media to save space.
