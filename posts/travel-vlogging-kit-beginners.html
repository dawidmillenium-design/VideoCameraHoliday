So you want to start a travel vlog? Welcome! You do not need to spend thousands of dollars to get started. A travel vlogging kit is just the right combination of camera, audio, and stabilization.

The Essential 3-Piece Kit

1. The Camera

For beginners, your smartphone is often more than enough! If you want a dedicated camera, look for a compact point-and-shoot with a flip-out screen.

2. The Audio (The Most Important Part)

Never use built-in camera microphones. They sound muffled and pick up every background noise. You need a simple clip-on wireless microphone (lavalier) or a small shotgun mic. * Read our full guide on the Best Microphones for Travel Vlogging to get crystal clear audio!

3. The Stabilizer

Shaky footage is the #1 sign of a beginner. * Step 1: Try to hold the camera with two hands against your chest. * Step 2: Buy an inexpensive handheld gimbal (a motorized stick that keeps your camera level).

Accessories You Shouldn’t Forget

  • A Tripod: For stable timelapses and standing shots.
  • Extra Batteries: You will always run out at the worst time!
  • A Memory Card: Get a fast one! See our Best Memory Card for 4K Holiday Video guide.

The “Vlog” Mindset

Don’t worry about being perfect. Vlogging is about telling your story. Film your planning, film your packing, film the highs and the lows of your journey.
