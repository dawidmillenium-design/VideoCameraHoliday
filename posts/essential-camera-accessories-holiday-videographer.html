Stabilization

Shaky footage ruins even the most beautiful destination. Modern cameras have built-in stabilization, but accessories take it to the next level.

DJI OM 6 Smartphone Gimbal
Essential for Phone

The best smartphone gimbal for travelers. Folds to pocket size, has a built-in extension rod for selfies, and ActiveTrack 6.0 keeps you in frame automatically.

Weight 309g
Battery 6-7 hours
Payload Up to 290g
Price $149
✅ Pros Pocket-sized when folded, ActiveTrack is magical for solo travelers, extension rod built-in
❌ Cons Only for phones, not action cameras. Setup takes 30 seconds each time

Buy if: You shoot most of your holiday video on a smartphone and want gimbal-smooth walking shots.

PGYTECH CapLock Action Camera Mount
Recommended

The fastest mounting system for action cameras. Magnetic snap-in design means you can move your camera between tripod, handheld grip, and backpack clip in under 2 seconds.

Compatibility GoPro, DJI, Insta360
Mount Types 6 included
Material Aluminum + Magnets
Price $89
✅ Pros Fastest mount changes possible, rock-solid magnetic lock, premium build quality
❌ Cons Expensive for a mount system, magnets can interfere with compass/GPS

Buy if: You switch between handheld, tripod, and mounted shots frequently during your trip.

ULANZI MT-16 Mini Tripod
Essential

The perfect travel tripod. Extends to 28cm for table-top vlogging, collapses to 16cm for your pocket. The ball head adjusts to any angle, and the legs grip uneven surfaces.

Max Height 28cm
Collapsed 16cm
Weight 120g
Price $19

Buy if: Everyone. At $19 and 120g, there's no reason not to carry this. Perfect for time-lapses, vlogging, and stable shots on any surface.

Audio

Bad audio ruins good video faster than bad video ruins good audio. Viewers forgive shaky footage; they won't forgive inaudible dialogue or wind noise.

DJI Mic 2 Wireless System
Essential for Vloggers

Two wireless transmitters + one receiver. Clips to your shirt, records directly to the receiver, and has 32-bit float recording that prevents clipping even if you shout or whisper.

Range 250m (line of sight)
Battery 6 hours (case: 18h)
Recording 32-bit float internal
Price $349
✅ Pros Crystal clear audio, impossible to clip, charges in case, works with cameras and phones
❌ Cons Expensive, transmitters are visible on camera, case is bulky

Buy if: You vlog, interview locals, or narrate your travel videos. Skip if you only shoot B-roll and music-backed montages.

Rode VideoMicro II
Budget Pick

A tiny shotgun mic that mounts on your camera's hot shoe (or phone with an adapter). Dramatically better audio than built-in mics for $60. No battery required — powered by the camera.

Type Shotgun (supercardioid)
Power Plug-in (no battery)
Weight 39g
Price $59

Buy if: You want better audio without the complexity and cost of wireless. Great for ambient sound and directional recording.

Wind Muff for Built-in Mics
Essential

A $10 foam windscreen that slips over your action camera or phone. Reduces wind noise by 80% in breezy conditions. The cheapest audio upgrade you'll ever make.

Fits GoPro, DJI, most phones
Weight 5g
Price $8-15

Buy if: Everyone. Seriously. Wind noise is the #1 audio killer in travel video, and this fixes it for the price of a coffee.

Power & Batteries

Running out of battery mid-shot is a travel videographer's nightmare. Here's how to avoid it:

Anker 737 Power Bank (24,000mAh)
Essential

Charges everything — phone, camera, gimbal, laptop — at full speed. 140W output means your MacBook charges as fast as from a wall outlet. The digital display shows exact remaining percentage.

Capacity 24,000mAh
Max Output 140W
Ports 2x USB-C, 1x USB-A
Price $109
✅ Pros Charges laptop + camera simultaneously, TSA-approved for carry-on, premium build
❌ Cons Heavy (630g), expensive, overkill if you only charge phones

Buy if: You carry multiple devices or work on a laptop during travel. For phone-only travelers, a 10,000mAh model is sufficient.

Essential

Always carry at least 2 spare batteries for your camera. Third-party batteries from brands like Wasabi Power or Patona offer 90% of OEM performance at 30% of the price.

GoPro Hero 13 Enduro Battery ~$25
DJI Action 5 ~$30
Sony RX100 NP-BX1 ~$20
Fuji X100VI NP-W126S ~$25

Buy if: Everyone with a removable-battery camera. A $25 battery saves you from missing the shot of a lifetime.

Capacity 1TB / 2TB / 4TB
Speed 1,050 MB/s read
Durability IP65, drop resistant 3m

Essential

Holds 12x SD + 12x microSD

Mounts & Grips

Floating Hand Grip
Essential for Water

Prevents your camera from sinking if dropped in water. Doubles as a comfortable handheld grip for above-water shots. Most have a watertight compartment for spare batteries or cash.

Fits All action cameras
Floatation Camera + housing
Storage Watertight compartment
Price $12-20

Buy if: Any trip involving water — beach, pool, boat, rain. The $15 insurance policy against losing your camera to the ocean.

Chest Mount Harness
Recommended

Mounts your action camera on your chest for hands-free POV footage. Perfect for hiking, cycling, skiing, and any activity where your hands are busy. More immersive than helmet mounts.

Adjustable Fits most body sizes
Angle Adjustable tilt
Price $15-25

Buy if: You do adventure activities and want POV footage without holding the camera. Skip for city/cultural trips.

Filters & Lenses

ND Filter Set (Action Camera)
Recommended

Neutral Density filters reduce light entering the lens, allowing slower shutter speeds for natural motion blur. Essential for cinematic footage in bright daylight. Without ND, action camera footage looks jittery because the shutter speed is too fast.

Set Includes ND8, ND16, ND32, ND64
Fits GoPro, DJI (check model)
Price $25-40

Buy if: You want cinematic motion blur in bright conditions. Essential for anyone shooting at 24fps or 30fps in daylight.

Moment Phone Lenses
Nice to Have

High-quality glass lenses that mount over your phone's camera. The anamorphic lens creates cinematic widescreen footage with lens flares; the macro lens captures incredible detail shots.

Options Anamorphic, Macro, Wide, Tele
Requires Moment phone case
Price $80-130 per lens

Buy if: You shoot primarily on phone and want to level up your footage creatively. Skip if you already carry a dedicated camera.

Protection & Cases

Pelican Case (Micro or Small)
Recommended

Crushproof, waterproof, dustproof cases for your camera and accessories. The 1060 Micro Case fits an action camera + 3 batteries + cards. The 1200 fits a compact camera with accessories.

1060 Micro $35 — Action cam kit
1200 Small $55 — Compact camera kit
Protection Crushproof, waterproof

Buy if: You check camera gear in luggage, or travel to rough environments. Overkill for carry-on-only city trips.

Silica Gel Packets
Essential

Throw these in your camera bag to absorb moisture. Critical in humid climates (Southeast Asia, Caribbean) and after water activities. Prevents lens fog and internal condensation.

Pack of 50 ~$8
Reusable Dry in oven to recharge

Buy if: Everyone. The cheapest protection against moisture damage.
