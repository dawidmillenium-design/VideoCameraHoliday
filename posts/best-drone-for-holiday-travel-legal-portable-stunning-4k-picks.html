Why Drone Weight Matters

Drones under 250g face the least regulation worldwide. Most countries exempt them from: - Registration - Pilot licenses - Commercial permits - Complex paperwork

The magic number: 249g. DJI specifically designs the Mini series to hit this weight.

Drones over 250g require registration in most countries and may be banned outright.

Flying Tips for Travelers

1. Scout Locations First

Use Google Earth to identify: - No-fly zones (airports, military bases) - Interesting compositions - Safe landing spots

2. Fly at Golden Hour

Sunrise and sunset provide: - Warm, cinematic light - Long shadows for depth - Less wind (usually)

3. Respect Privacy

Never fly over: - Private property without permission - Crowds of people - Religious ceremonies - Government buildings

4. Insurance

DJI Care Refresh (~$100/year) covers crashes and water damage. Essential for travel.

Travel insurance: Most policies do not cover drones. Check before you fly.

FAQ

Do I need to register my drone for holiday travel? Under 250g drones (DJI Mini series) do not require registration in most countries. Over 250g requires registration in EU, USA, Thailand, and others.

What is the best drone for beginners? DJI Mini 3 at $469. Affordable, under 250g, easy to fly. Mini 4 Pro adds obstacle avoidance for $290 more.

Can I bring a drone on a plane? Yes, batteries must be in carry-on. Under 250g drones rarely have issues. Check with your airline.

Is a drone worth it for holiday videos? Yes if you want aerial shots and accept regulations. For casual travelers, a 360° camera is simpler.
