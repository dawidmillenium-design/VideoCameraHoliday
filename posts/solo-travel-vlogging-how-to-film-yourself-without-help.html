Solo Travel Vlogging: How to Film Yourself Without Help

📅 2026-10-28 📖 2 min read ✍️ Dawid
⚠️ Disclosure: Some links are affiliate links. I earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

Traveling alone is liberating, but vlogging alone is hard. How do you talk to the camera when strangers are staring? How do you get a shot of yourself walking when no one is there to hold the camera?

Here is the ultimate playbook for the solo travel vlogger.

The “Talk to Yourself” Mindset

The hardest part of solo vlogging is the social anxiety. You must retrain your brain. Remind yourself that nobody remembers the person with the camera. They are too busy living their own lives. Just do it. The memories are worth the weird looks.

The Gear Setup for Solo Creators

You don’t need an assistant. You need the right tools.

1. The Vlogging Tripod (The “Best Friend”)

Look for a lightweight, extendable tripod that can double as a selfie stick. A gimbal with a tracking feature is even better—it will follow you as you walk!

2. The Wireless Microphone

When you are filming from a distance, your camera can’t hear you. You need a wireless mic (like a DJI Mic) clipped to your shirt. Check out our full guide on the Best Microphones for Travel Vlogging.

The “B-Roll” Strategy

Solo vlogging is all about the B-Roll (extra shots). * The POV Shot: Put your camera on your chest or a backpack strap. * The Walk-Away Shot: Place your camera on a tripod, hit record, and walk away from it. * The “Hand” Shot: Film your hands holding a map, a cup of coffee, or a snack.

How to Sound Natural

You will feel like you are talking to a robot at first. Don’t write a script. Just plan a few bullet points in your head or on a piece of paper. Say them in your own words, and if you mess up, just pause and continue. You can easily cut out the mistakes later.

Editing Solo Footage
