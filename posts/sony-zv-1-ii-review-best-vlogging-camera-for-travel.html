Sony ZV-1 II Review: Best Vlogging Camera for Travel?

90 days of real-world testing in Thailand, Bali, and Vietnam

The Sony ZV-1 II is the most recommended vlogging camera for travelers. But is it actually worth $898? I spent 90 days with it to find out.

“The ZV-1 II is not perfect. But it is the camera I reach for first on every trip.” — Dawid

What is the Sony ZV-1 II?

Released in 2023, the ZV-1 II is Sony’s second-generation vlogging camera. It is essentially a compact point-and-shoot optimized for video creators.

Key Specs

Spec Details
Sensor 1” stacked CMOS (20.1MP)
Lens 18-50mm f/1.8-4 (fixed)
Video 4K30, 1080p120
Screen 3” flip touchscreen
Weight 292g
Battery NP-BX1 (~45min 4K)
Price $898

🎬 Video Quality: The Real Test

Daylight Performance

The ZV-1 II produces stunning 4K video in good light. Sony’s color science delivers natural skin tones without the yellow cast of Canon or the oversaturation of GoPro.

Low Light Performance

At ISO 1600-3200, noise becomes noticeable but acceptable for YouTube. Above ISO 6400, footage gets grainy.

Autofocus: The Star Feature

Sony’s Real-time Eye AF is genuinely incredible. It locks onto faces instantly and tracks them across the frame.

Test: Walking through Chatuchak Market (crowded, chaotic) — focus never lost my face.

✈️ Travel-Friendly Features

1. Compact Size

At 292g, it fits in a jacket pocket. I carried it every day for 90 days without fatigue.

2. Flip Screen

The 3” flip screen is bright enough for outdoor use. Perfect for framing selfies and vlogs.

3. Product Showcase Mode

Tap a button and the camera instantly focuses on objects you hold up. Great for travel gear reviews.

4. Built-in ND Filter

Essential for beach videos, snow scenes, and desert landscapes.

❌ What is Not Great

1. No 4K60

For slow-motion or fast action, you are stuck with 1080p120. The DJI Osmo Pocket 3 offers 4K120 for $380 less.

2. Battery Life

~45 minutes of 4K recording per battery. For a full day, you need 3-4 spares.

3. No Headphone Jack

You cannot monitor audio while recording.

4. Fixed Lens

No zoom beyond 2.7x digital. For wildlife or distant landmarks, you need a different camera.

🏆 Who Should Buy the ZV-1 II?

Buy it if:

  • You vlog or film yourself regularly
  • You prioritize autofocus and ease of use
  • You want the best image quality in a compact body
  • You have the budget for spare batteries

Skip it if:

  • You need 4K60 or slow-motion (get DJI Pocket 3)
  • You film lots of action sports (get GoPro Hero 13)
  • You are on a tight budget (get Canon V10)

FAQ

Is the ZV-1 II worth it in 2026? Yes, if you need compact 4K vlogging. Consider waiting for the ZV-1 III (rumored late 2026) if you need 4K60.

Can I use external microphones? Yes, via the multi-interface shoe. Sony’s ECM-G1 shotgun mic is popular.

How many batteries do I need for a day? 3-4 for heavy 4K shooting. 2 for casual use.
