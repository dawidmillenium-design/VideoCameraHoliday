The ocean spray, sudden downpours, and beach sand are the worst enemies of a camera lens. A waterproof camera bag isn’t just a luxury; it’s a necessity for any traveler.

The 3 Best Types of Waterproof Bags

1. The Hard Case (Heavy Duty)

Perfect for extreme equipment and long-term storage. They are crush-proof and dust-proof, ideal for flights or checked luggage, but heavy to carry around on a daily basis.

2. The Roll-Top Dry Bag (Best for Beach Days)

These are lightweight, flexible, and completely submersible. Throw your camera inside, roll the top down three times, and you can safely take it kayaking or on a boat trip without worrying about water damage.

3. Weather-Resistant Backpack (Best for Daily Travel)

These look like normal travel backpacks but feature high-quality waterproof zippers and outer coatings that repel rain. Great for day-to-day city exploration.

What to Look For

  • IP Rating: Look for an IPX7 or higher rating (means it can be submerged in 1 meter of water for 30 minutes).
  • Padding: Make sure it has enough internal padding to prevent bumps and scratches.
  • Aesthetics: If you are traveling in crowded cities, buy a bag that doesn’t shout “expensive camera inside”.

Don’t Forget the Elements!

Even with a waterproof bag, you need to protect your gear from temperature extremes and physical shocks.
