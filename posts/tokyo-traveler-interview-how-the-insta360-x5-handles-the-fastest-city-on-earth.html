Expectations for the X5 Series

Traveler: “I expected the X5 to be a gimmick, but the resolution is the real deal. The 8K capture allows me to shoot in 360 and then crop to standard 4K, giving me multiple angles from one take. It’s like having a camera crew in my pocket.”

Shooting in Neon and Low Light

Traveler: “The upgraded sensor is fantastic for night shots in Shinjuku. The dynamic range handles the neon signs beautifully without crushing the shadows. For editing on the go, I rely heavily on my tablet. You can learn exactly how I do it by reading our Editing Travel Videos on Phones & Tablets guide.”

Traveler: “The biggest letdown is the internet in Tokyo hotels. Uploading a 20GB 8K file to the Insta360 Cloud can take 4 hours on terrible hotel networks! For fast-paced, neon-drenched cities, the Insta360 X5 is an unstoppable tool.
