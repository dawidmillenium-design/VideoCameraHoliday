SD Card Speed Classes Explained

SD cards have multiple speed ratings. Here is what matters for 4K video:

Speed Class (Number in Circle)

Class Minimum Write Speed 4K Suitability
Class 10 10 MB/s ❌ Too slow
UHS-I U1 10 MB/s ❌ Too slow
UHS-I U3 30 MB/s ✅ Minimum for 4K30
UHS-II V60 60 MB/s ✅ Great for 4K60
UHS-II V90 90 MB/s ✅ Best for 4K120

What You Actually Need

Recording Format Required Speed Recommended Card
4K30 (most cameras) V30 SanDisk Extreme
4K60 (action cams) V60 SanDisk Extreme Pro
4K120 (high-end) V90 ProGrade V90
5.3K60 (GoPro Hero 13) V60 SanDisk Extreme Pro

The rule: Your card’s write speed must exceed your camera’s recording bitrate.

My Recommendation

For holiday travel, get 2x 128GB cards instead of 1x 256GB.

Why? If a card fails (corruption, physical damage, loss), you lose half your footage instead of all of it. I learned this after a 256GB card corrupted on day 6 of a 10-day trip.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Mistake 1: Buying “High Speed” Cards Without Checking Write Speed

Many cards advertise “up to 170MB/s” but that is READ speed. Write speed is what matters for recording. Always check the V-rating (V30, V60, V90).

❌ Mistake 2: Using One Huge Card

A 512GB card seems convenient. But if it corrupts, you lose everything. Use 2x 128GB or 2x 256GB instead.

❌ Mistake 3: Buying Fake Cards

Amazon and eBay are flooded with counterfeit SanDisk cards. Buy from: - Authorized retailers (B&H, Adorama) - Amazon “Ships from and sold by Amazon.com” - Manufacturer direct

Spot fakes: Test with H2testw (free software). Fake cards report false capacity.

❌ Mistake 4: Not Formatting in Camera

Always format the card IN THE CAMERA before first use. This ensures the card structure matches what the camera expects. Formatting on a computer can cause recording errors.

❌ Mistake 5: Using Old Cards in New Cameras

That 5-year-old Class 10 card? It is too slow for 4K60. Cards wear out over time. Replace every 2-3 years of heavy use.

FAQ

What SD card for 4K video? V30 for 4K30, V60 for 4K60, V90 for 4K120/8K. SanDisk Extreme Pro is most reliable.

Is 128GB enough for 2 weeks? No, get 256GB minimum. Or 2x 128GB for redundancy.

Can I use microSD in SD slot? Yes with adapter, but full-size SD is more reliable.

How to spot fake SD cards? Test with H2testw software. Buy from authorized retailers only.
