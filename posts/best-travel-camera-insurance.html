Traveling with expensive camera gear is a risk. Theft, loss, water damage, and accidental drops are a reality of travel.

One of the biggest mistakes travelers make is assuming their home insurance covers their gear abroad. In most cases, it does not. Dedicated travel camera insurance is your safety net.

What Does Camera Insurance Cover?

  • Theft & Burglary: If your bag is stolen from your hotel room or a train.
  • Accidental Damage: Dropping your camera or scratching a lens.
  • Water Damage: The ultimate killer of camera gear.
  • Loss: Forgetting your gear in a taxi or losing it in transit.

Standard Home Insurance vs. Dedicated Travel Insurance

Your standard home insurance might cover your gear inside your house, but it usually has a low limit for items taken outside. Furthermore, they require proof of purchase and often have high deductibles.

Dedicated travel camera insurance is tailored specifically for gear, covering you worldwide, including higher limits for professional equipment.

How to File a Claim

If the unthinkable happens, report the loss to the local police immediately (you will need a police report), document the serial numbers of your gear, and keep all receipts from your trip.

Protect Your Gear Physically

While insurance is great, your goal should be to never have to use it. One of the best ways to prevent damage is to invest in high-quality bags. Read our guide on the Best Waterproof Camera Bags for Travel to protect your gear from the elements.
