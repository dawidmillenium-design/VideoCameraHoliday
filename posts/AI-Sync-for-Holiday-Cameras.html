6. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I fix wind noise on beach holiday videos for AI audio editing?

Use internal hardware windscreen suppression settings or an external lavalier with a deadcat filter. This prevents low-frequency clipping, ensuring stems can be split cleanly in AI tools like Suno or Eleven Music.

How do I match 120fps slow-motion footage to AI music beat-grids?

Calculate your shutter angle based on target export frame rates (such as 24fps) rather than leaving shutter speed on auto, allowing smooth motion blur alignment with standard BPM tracks.

How do I prevent pocket cameras from thermal throttling in 40°C heat?

Lower your encoding bitrate slightly, avoid prolonged standby recording under direct sun exposure, and use modular clip-on heatsinks or shaded intervals to maintain continuous operation.
