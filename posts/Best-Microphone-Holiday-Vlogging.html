Why Audio Matters

Viewers will forgive imperfect video, but bad audio is unwatchable. A dedicated microphone captures:

  • Clear dialogue without background noise
  • Rich ambient sound
  • Professional presence that builds trust

Pair your microphone with a compact camera for the perfect vlogging setup.

Best Vlogging Microphones

1. DJI Mic 2 – Best Wireless

The DJI Mic 2 offers 250m range, 15-hour battery, and excellent audio quality. It's our top pick for travel vloggers who need flexibility.

2. Rode VideoMicro – Best Shotgun

Compact, lightweight, and affordable – the VideoMicro is perfect for on-camera audio without interference.

3. Rode Wireless Go II – Best for Versatility

Dual-channel wireless with built-in recording. Perfect for interviews and multi-person vlogs.

4. Shure MV88+ – Best for Smartphones

A stereo microphone for smartphones with multiple polar patterns. Great for iPhone users .

How to Choose a Vlogging Microphone

  • Consider your camera: Does it have a 3.5mm jack or USB-C?
  • Think about your environment: Windy beaches need deadcats/windscreens
  • Check portability: Travel-friendly size and weight
  • Battery life: Wireless mics need charging

Tips for Better Audio

  • Position the mic close: 6-12 inches from your mouth
  • Use a windscreen: Essential for outdoor recording
  • Monitor audio: Use headphones while recording
  • Record room tone: 10 seconds of silence for noise reduction

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use a wireless microphone with my smartphone?

Yes, many wireless mics like the DJI Mic 2 and Rode Wireless Go II support USB-C connections for smartphones.

What's the best budget microphone for vlogging?

The Rode VideoMicro is our top budget pick at around $50. It delivers great sound without needing batteries.

Do I need a windscreen for holiday vlogging?

Yes – beach and outdoor environments are windy. A deadcat windscreen is essential to prevent wind noise.
