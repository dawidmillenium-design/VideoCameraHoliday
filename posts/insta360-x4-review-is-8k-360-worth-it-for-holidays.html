Insta360 X4 Review: Is 8K 360° Worth It for Holidays?

🌐 How 360° Changes Travel Filming

The Problem with Traditional Cameras

You point, you shoot, you hope you got the angle right. Miss the moment? It is gone forever.

The 360° Solution

The X4 records everything in all directions simultaneously. Later, you:

  1. Choose your angle — Any direction, any zoom
  2. Create multiple clips — One recording = 5+ videos
  3. Add impossible shots — Tiny planet, invisible selfie stick, “drone” aerials

Real Example: Bangkok Temple Visit

One 10-minute recording became: - 📹 Walking through the gate - 🙏 Close-up of the golden Buddha - ⏱️ Timelapse of crowds - 🌍 Tiny planet creative shot - 🏃 Follow shot of my partner

All from one walk.

🎬 Video Quality Test

8K 360° Mode

  • Reframed to 1080p: Stunning detail
  • Reframed to 4K: Good, but softens at edges
  • Full 360° playback: Only useful for VR headsets

Verdict: 8K is marketing. 5.7K60 is the sweet spot.

Low Light

Small sensors struggle after sunset. Avoid night markets and indoor restaurants.

✈️ Travel-Friendly Features

1. Invisible Selfie Stick

Extend the selfie stick and the X4 stitches it out completely. Result: Looks like a drone shot.

2. Waterproof to 10m

Snorkeling without a case. Color is decent, but not as good as GoPro’s underwater processing.

3. FlowState Stabilization

6-axis stabilization is excellent. Walking footage looks gimbal-smooth.

❌ Limitations

1. Post-Processing Required

360° footage is useless without reframing. You MUST spend time in the app. Not for “shoot and share” travelers.

2. Battery Life

~75 minutes of recording. Less than GoPro. Carry 2-3 spares.

3. Not for Interviews

360° captures everything — including the interviewer and background mess. Use a traditional camera for talking-head content.

📊 X4 vs X3 vs GoPro Max 2

Feature Insta360 X4 Insta360 X3 GoPro Max 2
360° Resolution 8K30 5.7K30 5.6K30

Verdict: X4 for best 360° quality. X3 for budget. Max 2 for simpler software.

🏆 Who Should Buy the X4?

Buy it if:

  • You want unique, creative shots
  • You do not mind post-processing
  • You film lots of walking/touring content
  • You want “drone” shots without a drone

Skip it if:

  • You want instant, shareable footage
  • You hate editing
  • You film lots of interviews
  • You are on a budget (get the X3 or Canon V10)

FAQ

How long does reframing take? Simple clips: 5 minutes. Complex edits: 30-60 minutes per video.

Can I livestream in 360°? Yes, via the Insta360 app. Quality is 1080p.

Is the X4 good for vlogging? Not ideal. Use a Sony ZV-1 II or Canon V10 for vlogging.
