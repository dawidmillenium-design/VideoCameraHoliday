Why Every Traveler Needs a 360° Camera in 2026

How I stopped missing shots and started capturing everything

📅 2026-08-15 📖 2 min read ✍️ Dawid
⚠️ Disclosure: Some links are affiliate links. I earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

I was skeptical of 360° cameras until I missed the perfect sunset shot in Bali. Now I never travel without one.

What is a 360° Camera?

Unlike traditional cameras that capture what is in front, 360° cameras record everything around you — all directions simultaneously.

After filming, you “reframe” in software to create standard videos, choosing any angle you want.

Why 360° Changes Travel Filming

1. Never Miss the Moment

At a busy Bangkok temple, I held the camera up and walked. Later, I extracted 5 different shots: - Me walking through the gate - The golden Buddha close-up - A timelapse of crowds - A tiny planet shot - A “follow” shot of my partner

All from one recording.

2. Impossible Shots Become Easy

  • Drone-like aerials — extend a selfie stick, reframe from above
  • Invisible selfie stick — the camera stitches it out
  • Tiny planet — creative spherical shots

3. Less Time Filming, More Time Experiencing

I spend 70% less time setting up shots. Just record and enjoy.

Step 2 — Reframe in Insta360 Studio: - Extract 3 vertical clips for Reels/TikTok - 1 horizontal clip for YouTube - 1 tiny planet for thumbnail

Step 3 — Export & Post: Total editing time: 30 minutes

Tips for Better 360° Footage

  1. Hold steady — 360° shows all angles, including your hand movements
  2. Use a selfie stick — gives “drone” perspective, gets edited out
  3. Stay in motion — static 360° is boring; walk, drive, ride
  4. Protect the lenses — scratches are catastrophic (use guards)
  5. Shoot in good light — small sensors struggle at dusk

When NOT to Use 360°

  • Interviews/talking head — traditional camera is better
  • Low light — noise becomes noticeable
  • Quick social posts — reframing takes time

Final Verdict

The ability to reframe later means you will never again say “I wish I got that shot.”
