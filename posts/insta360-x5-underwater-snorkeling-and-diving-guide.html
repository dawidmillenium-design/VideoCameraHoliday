Waterproof Rating

The X5 is waterproof to 10 meters without a case. For any diving deeper than that, you must buy the optional dive case. Without a case, the camera captures clear audio as well, which is great for splashing around in the shallows.

Color Correction

Underwater footage always looks flat and blue. Because the X5 shoots 8K, you have immense room to crop in and apply a color correction filter in the app. Red filters are essential if you go deep.

Diving Accessories

You need to keep your camera safe from rocks and corals. Ensure you use a floating hand grip so you don’t lose it in the deep. Check out our guide on Holiday Camera Accessories Must-Have for floating handles.

Shooting Tips

For the best underwater footage, shoot horizontally. This allows the AI to keep the horizon level. For editing your reef footage, read our Cinematic Travel Videos on a Budget guide.
