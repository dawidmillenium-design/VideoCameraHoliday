Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Last updated: September 12, 2026

We are committed to protecting your privacy and complying with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), Brazil's Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD), and other applicable data protection laws.

1. Who We Are

The data controller for the Site is:

  • Name: [YOUR FULL LEGAL NAME]
  • Address: [YOUR FULL POSTAL ADDRESS]
  • Email: [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL]

For any privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at the email above.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide directly

We only collect personal information you voluntarily provide to us, including:

  • Email address — if you subscribe to our newsletter
  • Name — if you contact us directly
  • Message content — if you send us a message via email or a contact form

2.2 Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we and our service providers automatically collect certain technical information when you visit the Site:

  • Server logs — IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URL, and pages viewed. These are collected by GitHub Pages (our hosting provider) for security and operational purposes.
  • Cookies — small text files stored on your device. See Section 5 below.
  • Analytics data — if analytics are enabled, we collect aggregated data about how visitors use the Site.

2.3 Third-party content

Some pages embed content from YouTube. When you view such a page, YouTube may set cookies and collect data about your viewing behavior. See Section 6 below.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Site
  • Respond to your questions, comments, and requests
  • Send you our newsletter (if you subscribed)
  • Monitor and analyze usage patterns and trends
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues, fraud, or abuse
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), we process your personal data under the following legal bases:

  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR) — for newsletter subscription and non-essential cookies
  • Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) — for security, fraud prevention, and basic site operation
  • Legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR) — where required by law

5. Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We use:

  • Essential cookies — required for the Site to function (e.g., remembering your cookie consent choice). These do not require consent.
  • Analytics cookies — if enabled, these help us understand how visitors use the Site.
  • Third-party cookies — set by embedded content such as YouTube videos.

On your first visit, we display a cookie banner allowing you to accept or reject non-essential cookies. You can change your choice at any time by clearing your browser's local storage for this Site.

6. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

GitHub Pages (hosting)

The Site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect server logs including your IP address. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

YouTube (video embeds)

Some pages embed YouTube videos. When you play a video, YouTube (Google LLC) may set cookies and collect viewing data. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Amazon Associates (affiliate program)

We participate in the Amazon Associates Program. When you click an Amazon affiliate link, Amazon sets cookies to track the referral. See Amazon's Privacy Notice.

Other affiliate programs

We may also participate in other affiliate programs. Each program sets its own cookies when you click through to a merchant. These programs use cookies to attribute purchases and pay commissions.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the Site (e.g., GitHub, our email newsletter provider)
  • Affiliate networks — when you click an affiliate link, the merchant receives the referral information needed to attribute a purchase
  • Legal authorities — when required by law, court order, or to protect our rights

8. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy:

  • Newsletter subscriptions — until you unsubscribe
  • Contact messages — up to 2 years after our last communication
  • Server logs — retained by GitHub according to their retention policy

9. Your Rights

9.1 Rights for EEA, UK, and Swiss residents (GDPR)

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate personal data
  • Erasure — request deletion of your personal data
  • Restriction — limit how we process your data
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw consent — at any time, without affecting lawfulness of prior processing
  • Lodge a complaint — with your local supervisory authority

9.2 Rights for California residents (CCPA/CPRA)

California residents have the right to:

  • Know what categories of personal information we collect and how we use it
  • Delete personal information we collected from you
  • Correct inaccurate personal information
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (we do not sell personal information)
  • Non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights

9.3 Rights for Brazilian residents (LGPD)

Brazilian residents have similar rights under the LGPD, including confirmation of processing, access, correction, anonymization, portability, and deletion.

How to exercise your rights

To exercise any of these rights, email us at [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL]. We will respond within 30 days.

10. International Data Transfers

Our hosting provider (GitHub) is based in the United States. If you are in the EEA or UK, your data may be transferred outside your region. GitHub uses Standard Contractual Clauses and other legal mechanisms to protect such transfers. See GitHub's Privacy Statement for details.

11. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top. Material changes will be highlighted on the homepage. Your continued use of the Site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
