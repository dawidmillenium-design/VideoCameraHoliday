About Holiday Video Camera

Real travel camera testing — no lab charts. Every camera on this site was filmed on actual holidays, and judged the way you'll watch the footage: on a phone, in the sun, after a long day.

Holiday Video Camera is an independent review site with one rule: we only recommend cameras we have actually filmed holidays with. No lab charts, no spec-sheet rewrites, no press-release summaries. Since 2019 we have tested more than 50 cameras on real trips across Southeast Asia, Europe, and South America — on beaches, ski slopes, night markets, and desert trails.

Our Mission

To help travelers choose the right camera by showing real footage from real holidays — not lab charts or spec sheets. A camera that wins a studio test can still fail you at a beach in Bali: sand in the gimbal, fog on the lens, a battery that dies in the cold. We test for the moments you actually travel for, like the ones covered in our best holiday video cameras of 2026 roundup.

How We Test

Every camera on this site goes through the same field protocol before we write a word:

  • Real trips only: each camera is taken on at least one genuine holiday — not a weekend in the garden.
  • Multiple conditions: harsh midday sun, golden hour, low light, rain or humidity where the trip allows it.
  • Judged on a phone: we review footage the way you will watch it — on a phone screen, in daylight, after a long day.
  • Carried, not curated: if a camera is annoying to pack, charge, or pull out at the right moment, that counts against it. Our hiking camera guide is a good example of weight and battery discipline in practice.
  • No loaner bias: review units and purchases are treated identically, and rankings never change because of brand relationships.
